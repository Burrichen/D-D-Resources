## Overview
Kelemvor is a title, not a name. The role is passed along to deserving mortals when they pass. The new death is often groomed before taking its place. A Scion of Death who doesn't die means an ineffectual God. 

He acts as the judge of the damned, sending those who have passed on to their appropriate final resting place. Many of the other scions 'dont get Kelemvor'. Each Kelemvor tends to associate with different Scions, but the most common relationships are between Hermod and Chauntea.

Many of the other Gods 'dont get them', and he is only seen around [[Hermod, God of Luck|Hermod]] and [[Chauntea, Goddess of Agriculture]].

**Alignment**: True Neutral
**Domains**: Death

## Story

### Abernath - Starting Year
Abernathe believed he was above impunity. The first being ever to die in the world a bitter old man. Abernath was forced into the role of Kelemvor, although that term hadn't been coined yet. He was cold and calculated, and prided himself on his self taught merit. Because no ceremony for power transferrence had been created, the soul of his estranged daughter, Dianye was kept in Caceri until Abernath died.

### Dianye - 0 CA
 There was absolutely nothing special about Dianye, apart from the fact her father is death, and abandoned her when he was mortal. Upon her ascention, instead of a peaceful afterlife, she found herself imprisoned in Caceri until Abernath relinquished the position. Upon her ascension, she was able to come to the conclusion of what had happened to her by pulling her father back to life, and speaking directly to him. Abernath was of no use, and disgusted the first thing she did was breathe life back into him, a sacrilegious act. 

The two were bitter, with Dianye asking for help and Abernath refusing to support her, the two bickered often. 

Her powers didn't come close to Abernate's and she realised that she had her work cut out for her. Carving her own space inside her own realm, she created an institution to work hard. It took thousands of years to create a sustainable environment where she could work, successfully bringing an end to the Grasping Passage. She is depicted as the very first Aaisimar. 

However in a cruel twist of fate, just as Dianye was beginning to feel comfortable in her new position, she began aging, her powers dwindling and her mind fading. She spent the last few hundred years recording all she knew, and set about transferring her power to a mortal she believed was worthy. It was her who created the title Kelemvor.

### Carvell - 6,924 CA
Carvell was a man of the law. Inspired by his father to take up the law, he went out to make a difference in the world. However, within his first year of service, he was killed, protecting a family from outlaws. His own father held his body as he died. Dianye saw the pure of heart this man had, and knew that this tragedy could still have a somewhat happier ending. 

Carvell was taken, and given full power. The transfer was simple and easy, Carvell worked to reinforce and reiterate on what Dianye founded. When it was his fathers time to pass, instead of bringing him to death, he employed his father to help out with his duties, and able to retire at any point. But Carvell had an issue, the years toiled on him, feeling guilt for those he transport, thusly the seed was sown for the next Kelemvor.

### Wernicke - Lingering Silence
Wernicke was an executioner of the Cognitive Age, dying to mistake. Carvell chose him for his clear-headedness while performing executions, however upon transference, Wernicke and Carvell famously hated each other. Both had completely different views on the Kelemvor role, Carvell wanting it to be an honourable, neutral and homely experience. Wernicke wanted it to be efficient but cold. The two often argued and tensions were high as Carvell knew his time was up. Wernicke ushered in the Lingering Silence as pseudo-revenge, showing how bad he could make the world if he so chose. But eventually, he realised how spiteful he was. Trying to resurrect his master, Wernicke wanted to re-do his studies, to learn how to be a good Kelemvor, but instead was met by Dianye. She comforted him, and taught him anew. For many years they worked together, and Wernicke ended up serving an awful long time, proud of how far he came. 

But then the Oblate happened, and for the first time in centuries, Kelemvors powers were out of their hands. A sheer amount of death strained their powers to strain and quickly lost control. Seeing this world that had been so well maintained before, and how he had let everyone down, Carvell, Dianye - Wernicke killed himself.
