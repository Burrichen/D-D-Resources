Composed of 1,182 modron programs, Primus has become assigned as the leader of [[Mechanus]]. Particually composed of every metal currently available across planes, Primus is content to operate within it's own Plane. 

Primus does not leave it's own Plane, and is notoriously isolationist, with Mechanus being one of the least visited Planes. However, it is known to send Modrons to other Planes to perform tasks (Such as the construction of [[Fleshless]]), ostensibly to help itself however it's true motivations remain unknown. 

It is protective of the Modron 'creatures', believing them to be imperfect, but an adequate enough proxation, unlike other races.. It holds no ego despite considering its construction 99% perfect.
