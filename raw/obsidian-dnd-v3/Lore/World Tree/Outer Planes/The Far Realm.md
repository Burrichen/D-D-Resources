**Alignment:** Neutral Evil
**Plane God:** Cthulhu (Aka, The Woodland Man, The Librarian)
**Plane God Motivations:** Dissolution. Anything tangible it despises. Form, thought memory or consciousness. It desires nothing. The Oblate showed Cthulhu light, and awoke Him.
**Key Features:** 
 - **Wrecks of Sanity:** The Abyss is mostly featureless, but every now and then, there will be things that the Great Old One has dreamed of. A shall of a dead god's thoughts, the petrified memory of a forgotten city, a continent of a mortal's failed dream. 
 - **Aberrations:** Tumours of thought. 
 - **Abyssal Strain:** When the Oblate receded, it didn't leave the material plane unscathed. Like greased paper, reality seeps through. Just like how the Abyss seeps back. [[Seeping Maw]] cultists on the other end are desperate to pierce reality.  

**Effect on players:**
- Entropy of the Abyss. Whenever the creature deals bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage, it can change the damage type to necrotic.
- Poison Tolerant. The creature has advantage on saving throws it makes to avoid or end the [poisoned](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#poisoned_phb) condition on itself.
- Siege Monster. The creature deals double damage to objects and structures.
