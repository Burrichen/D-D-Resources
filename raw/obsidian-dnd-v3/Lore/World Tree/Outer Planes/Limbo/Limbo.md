Limbo is a realm of pure, untamed, creative chaos. It is the plane of raw potential, where the very substance of reality has not yet decided what it wants to be. The landscape is a terrifying, beautiful, and utterly unpredictable storm of creation and dissolution. Continents of churning earth might rise from a sea of liquid ideas, only to crumble into a snowstorm. The "air" can be a breathable fog, a current of pure emotion, or a rain of molten paradoxes.

There are no laws here—not of physics, magic, or society. The only force that matters is willpower. Beings of immense self-conviction can temporarily impose their will on the chaos, creating temporary islands of stability. Those with weak minds are torn apart, their very essence unravelled and re-purposed as raw material for the Maelstrom. This is not a plane of good or evil; it is the ultimate expression of freedom, in both its creative and destructive forms.
