Sometime after creation, an interplanar bargain was struck between the Old Gods - there would be those to rival their power, to offend them, to challenge them. They would need a place to teach these beings a lesson - death was not final enough. 

A pact was created, as long as no God opposite it, any creation would be sent to a new creation -  Carceri, a sub-realm of Limbo.  This technically makes it the first Demi-Plane, predating the others by epochs. 

Moulded like a whirlpool at the center of Limbo, it ironically forms the largest coherent landmass in the Plane. There are a multitude of beings, oft equally as powerful than Gods, but without their mercy. To be locked in Carceri is the greatest sentence known to the Universe.
