A successful assassination attempt in 186 caused the Halingo to splinter, or at least is what would have happened, had Danny not survived.

Instead, the bullet passed through his brain, out the other side and Danny still stood there. He wobbled slightly, his words slurring. He collapsed. The crowd went insane. The shooter was apprehended and hung on the spot. 

Pictures flashed through Danny's brain. He could barely comprehend anything. His body was placed in an Ichor pool by his close companion, and Axius on top of him. It would be his grave. However, something happened. 

It is commonly spoken that Axius and Ichor have a mind of their own. Seeing it's hosts brain functions fail, the Ichor spread into his brain, reconnecting damaged neurons, editing them slightly to he would still function. Meanwhile, the Axius, coaxed by the Ichor, began to reconstruct his old body. 

A day later, Danny rose again. His skin a glistening, his veins black. He was reborn as the first Sorcerer King. Amazed and power hungry at his new immortality, he created a plan. He publicly killed and brought back his follower. He would then go on a new holy quest - finding worthy Halingo residents and turning them into Sorcerer-Kings. However, his search was not limited to just live specimens. Famous tombs were raided, and important historical figures from Halingo's past were brought back to life. 

They ruled Halingo with an iron fist, their own denizens being at strategic points of Ichor wells. At this time, 9 Sorcerer Kings are known to exist.
