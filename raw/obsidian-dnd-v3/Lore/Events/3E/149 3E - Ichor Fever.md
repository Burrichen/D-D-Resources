After the Fall of Orcus' cult in the late second era, Halingo's reputation had finally reached rock bottom. Now not only was it an unliveable wasteland, but even attempts to create a tourist hub had failed. 

After the rise of Ordau, the power of Axius had been proven as industrial powerhouse. Those with nothing else to lose began searching. Thaumaturgists had claimed that the metal could theoretically be everywhere, even if other regions had already claimed their lack of interest in finding it. Halingo had no such luxuries, and many backwater towns began converting their workforce into miners and scavengers, raiding old tombs and opening holes in the ground, desperate to find something.

It was in 149 that the first piece of Axius was found, in a nothing town named Dustfall, named after the frequent duststorms that ravaged the area, but ironically was the thing that unearthed Axius. However, after the metal was removed from the ground, black ooze began to leak out of the earth, like pus from a wound. Curious, a young, patriotic man named Danny touched the stuff. Whilst others would be contacting the press, and other states, Danny began imbibing the ooze. He began to see the true power of axius, and what one could do, if infused with the ooze. He named it Ichor. 

A year passed, and Danny was now the leader of Dustfall. He began to construct machines, ones he seemingly already knew the answer to and banned the consumption of Ichor. He killed men and framed their deaths on Ichor consumption, but constructed an elaborate plot to publicly show him drinking it and living, and another man doing the same but dying (who had been poisoned the night before). He claimed only the truly strong could survive consumption. He gave it to a rare few of his trusted friends and colleagues - ones he would believe he drive Halingo in the right direction.

Halingo remained the only country to produce Ichor. Thousands would pour in hunting for something, a Ichor rush had gripped the country. 

By 165 3E, it ended, with no discoveries being found in 5 years. But those with Ichor and Axius now had bustling, thriving communities (and often being puppeted by Danny), and those without were in an even worse space.
