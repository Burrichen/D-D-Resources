Lead by the enigmatic [[Seneth]], this was the first campaign ever played in this world. The events detailing what happened can now be found in the book [[The Serpent's Price]]. However it is not fully factual. Some events that happened that have been lost to time are the following.

- Corrin ended up returning to Heresy about 10 years after splitting from Seneth's Company. He has a grave in the town and warriors spirit lives on, even if his soul resides in Mount Celestia, forgiven for his crimes.
- Amanda directly help write the book on her deathbed, and was actually the reason the book got published on her deathbed as some sort of atonement.
