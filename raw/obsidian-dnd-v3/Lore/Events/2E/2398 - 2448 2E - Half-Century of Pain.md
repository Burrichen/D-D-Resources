These years indicate the approximately 50 year long span during which Orcus was summoned into the world by Din'Juul. This half-century was one of terror and fear for most people - even if they weren't directly affected. 

It ended when Ark Troops slew Orcus.


The book [[The Humans Behind Orcus]] details further elements.
