> [!info]
> To an outsider, Hjarland is a harsh outback of snow-capped forests and desolate wilderness, inhabited by backwater isolationists. The truth is the people here are harsh, but offer a unique, often kindly, views on community.

- Inspirations: Norse mythology, Norway, Christmas, Skyrim.
- **Governance:** Loose coalition of matricidal led tribes, each with their own view of survival:
	- **Hearthbound (Hange Name):** Believe sharing and kindness is key.
	- **Skahld-Raider (Change Name):** Believes the weak cannot survive in the world.
	- **Traditionalists (CN):** Isolationists who believe in Hjarland independence and refusal of outside support. Hoard ancient secrets.
- **Level of technology:** Level 1.
- **Attitude towards Axius:** Disgust (Aside from some Skahld).
- **Attitude towards magic:** Revence.
- **General environment:** Snowy forests, borealis, glaciers and fjords. 
- **Key Locations:** 
	- **Fireside: (Change Name)** Several longhouses joined together to form one community.
	- Skyrim inspired place
	- Norway inspired places
