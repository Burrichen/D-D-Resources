Fleshless, also known as The Fleshless One was a human who's consciousness remains bound to a immovable machine, deep below the world.

He was a normal citizen in the second era, who had high expectations. He and his squad of technologists and sorcerers worked hard to construct a giant humanoid machine, guided by a miniature version they had discovered (Unbeknownst to all of them, a Modron). However, the project proved much longer and more difficult than expected, and funds were cut. The man worked long and hard alone on the project, desperate to finalise the machine. 

Asking a group of investors to come and look at his project, finally ready to show the world, an accident occurred. It's main power supply blew (Due to containing The Eye), and drew upon his consciousness instead. The machine moved just a little, enough for it's hand to reach closer to the investors before stopping completely. His body never moved again. Some say his consciousness remains trapped inside the body of the machine.

Every few thousand years, it is forgotten and rediscovered indiscriminately. Stories tell of a fleshless giant who lives under the world - unknown to all, the truth is far more sinister.


# The Eye
Fleshless was built with "The Eye", and internal piece of Modron technology that was inadvertently copied by it's creators through reverse engineering. It was this piece of technology that caused the robot to malfunction in the first place. It allows Fleshless to feel emotions and general trends in society within it's reach. 

It is unclear if the accident behind Fleshless was an intentional move by Primus to better understand the mortal realm, or if his original plan simply went wrong - thwarted by human folly, leading to horrific consequences.
