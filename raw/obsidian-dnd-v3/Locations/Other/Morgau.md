>  Much like it's namesake, Prenumbia is a scarred, warped land. It is the region of the world that was at the epicenter of the Oblate's explosion and it's never recovered. The land is saturated with Haze. The Haze erodes the walls of reality and the rules within it. This constant, chaotic flux is a posion to all mortal life and ordered machinery, but is a font to unimaginable power to those who already defy the natural order.

**History**: Before the Oblate, the land was connected to the main continent, the location of the Kingdom of Dornig. Fertile lands and honour-bound knights protected the poor and rich alike. When the Oblate occurred, the rules of reality shattered, and all hell broke lose. It started small, thoughts infecting others, acts of insanity and depravity. It came to an apex when the rich kings ordered a slaughter of those who deemed 'unworthy' - mostly those of poor social standing. Their bodies were brought to the castle, and all were ordered to feast on the corpses. Those who took part became vampires. 

As time passed, evil began to spread. The blassie-faire attitude of the new vampire ruling class meant dark research into forbidden schools of magic became common-place.
