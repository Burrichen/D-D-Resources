# Overview
**History**: Quinten Hearspar always wanted to be a scholar. 
Defeat crept into his becoming, his most familiar feeling. He found himself toiling in the fields, just like his father before him. His blood, sweat and body being given to the ground, for a manager who never even showed his face at their plot, but would still demand ever-increasing payments. 

Quinten's luck wouldn't end there, after a bandit-raid led to his mother being left immobile and brain damaged. Five months later, his father abandoned them - going on to raise a family with another woman. A year after that, he stopped supporting them financially, while mother and son burned through their collective savings to rehabilitate her. 

One hot day, Quinten suffered a stroke whilst working in the field. His body spasmed, falling to the ground, choking on his own spit. His fractured mind glimpsed something no other mortal had. A world of pure order, of complete logic - lacking in all the wrongs this world had shown him.

He awoke days later in that field, his body deemed unworthy of a coffin. Quinten could never see the world in the same way again. Lines of logic, as clear as words on parchment began appearing to him. He tried to tell those who asked about his recovery, but the stroke made his speech slurred, and went he was speaking coherently, the content of his speeches made very little sense. When he followed the logic, good things began to happen. He began making real money by implementing new irrigation techniques on his plot, which owned him a promotion. His mother was left, funds pulled when his new brain couldn't understand the familial bond. 

Years later, fortune acquired, the lines began to fade. Loss after loss occurred to him. In a desperate bid to see what he once did, he sank money into the last lines. Into a machine to show him the logic of the world, to show him that clockwork universe.  Eye of Gold was it's name. By utilising its power, Quinten was able to transform his wealth into a settlement, then a town, then a city. Eventually it began the capital. 

Technological advancements filled the city, and it became an industrial powerhouse, at the cost of those living there. The use of Axius became widespread. Quinten found his body growing old, less useful. The logic told him to discard it. His body was found, mutilated and partially fused to the engine. It never worked again.
