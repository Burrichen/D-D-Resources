Originally published in 2456 2E, 2nd edition published in 9 3E by Tiberian Fairen

This book, particularly through excellent timing - first publication just before the turn of an Epoch, and second edition after Axius had demonstrated its new power - has become perceived as the definite piece of literature on the [[2398 - 2448 2E - Half-Century of Pain|Half-Century of Pain]], especially through it's human-centric lens.

The first half of the book is detailed with character profiles of Shabiz, Din'Juul, Elora and Valakoss, as well as Lav as a supporting character. It details how a group of friends stuck by each others side, no matter what, even as they found themselves deep inside a criminal conspiracy. 

Then it takes a third person perspective, how a prison raid went wrong. Locked away in a secure wing was the Demonomicon, a relic of immense power. Cornered on all sides, Din'Juul spoke from the book, unaware of the power he held. The spell he had inadvertently cast, tethered his soul to Orcus. Orcus, caught by surprise was flung across Planar space and into Kurovia. As part of a faustian bargain to enter the mortal world, he gave them Din'Juul a wish. Either by accident or on purpose, Din'Juul crafted an excellent wish 'To be taken away to a safe place' - Orcus had no clue where Din'Juul had vanished. And he needed to kill him, to free his soul of this connection.

Focusing back on the group, it explains how Orcus must have reaped Valakoss' soul somehow, as he died upon arrival in Halingo - even their arrival to Halingo is hotly contested - why would Orcus send them there as a representation of a 'safe place'? The group were contacted by The Ambit, specifically Hadlar Malsir and Feena Malsir (who were revealed to have wed in secret, following the events of their encounter with Seneth's group.) The group were enlisted to help find Orcus' artifacts and avoid his wrath - to avoid him getting stronger and to atone. Unfortunately, it was not to be. The group failed in their efforts. In the first 10 years, they were met with tragedy. All while troops fought to contain Orcus, their search was in vein, whilst attempting to find the Wand of Orcus, Shabiz was ensnared in a trap, tortured and killed. Orcus obliterated Thevenovgrad, his homeland while Shabiz was forced to watch. However, Orcus was unable to destroy Thevethalas, most likely due to his connection to Din'Juul, who was growing increasingly erratic. 

A plan was formed, by utilising a new experimental material, so strong it could withstand the astral plane, and so powerful as to traverse it. The Ambit had been contacted by various blacksite scientists about a material so powerful, and they decided to use it. A mechanised suit, piloted by Hadlar, but guided by Feela (Who would remain in Kurovia), containing Din'Juul would be sent to Avernus, where Din'Juul could sever the connection manually with remains of Orcus there. With that, Orcus power to the demonic Plane would be severed, drastically reducing his power. The operation began, 20 years after this had began.

Plunging into the depths of Avernus, Hadlar, Lav (a separate contact contact of the Ambit), Corren (Not **the** Corrin), Din'Juul and Elora fought devils to find some connection to Orcus. Eventually they found it. The beating Heart of Orcus. The engine of Hell itself. They attempted to destroy it, but they were nowhere near powerful enough. More time together, more cohesion, a little less drama and more commitment and maybe they stood a chance. Corren died on the job, and was buried without much ceremony. Din'Juul stood at the altar of the demon, and stabbed himself. It wasn't an easy death, each time he fought himself a little bit more, but eventually, he stopped breathing. Orcus' connection to the our world had lessened. Out of grief, Elora asked to be killed also, her old age catching up to her, and her life seeming much more useless now. 

But they weren't alone. Valakoss, sent to hell for his transgressions saw the group he loved die one by one. Instead of letting Elora die, Valakoss possessed her. Keeping her soul trapped in Avernus with her love, and Kalakos was given a second chance at life, even in an aging body. The five, now three were pulled back into reality. Unfortunately, significant amounts of time had passed. 25 years had passed in Kurovia. Thevethalas was destroyed, but Orcus was weakened significantly recently. 

The last act cover how Ark troops, armed with the now proven safe Axius, slew Orcus. Now no longer a governmental secret, businesses and companies began using Aixus, ushering in a new era of technology. The final chapter is dedicated to those who lost their life to Orcus, and finally - to Madlar, Feena, Din'Juul and Elora.
