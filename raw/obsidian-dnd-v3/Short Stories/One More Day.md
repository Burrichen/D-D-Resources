The Oblate happened so many years ago. [[Ashaiek]] was one of the last recorded minds to have experienced the phenomenon. Thrown out of the [[The Underdark]] as a youngling, Ashaiek was as familiar of his natural realm as he was of Avernus. 

The priests on hand were constantly casting spells, the once familiar warmth of restoration replaced with numbness, that cold repetition. The featureless stone of the interior offered no reprise either. The featureless interior of bland stone would be the only anchor in the whirlwind of faces. The tall man was the other one. His tall, lanky form leered just out of his limited vision, but his pointy ears and nasally tone gave him away. Question after question. 'What were you doing before it happened? What did you see? Did it hurt?'. 'I don't know, I don't know, yes.'

The tall man would then leave, and come back an indescribable time later to ask the same thing. It gave Ashaiek time to remember: It was an average Tuesday (Change name), his grandchildren, too old to sit on his lap, but too young to leave him alone sat playing around him. He had just closed his eyes for a moment, the voices of the children fading.

'Mum', 'Grandpa isn't breathing'

The peaceful silence turned into a cocaughany, his consciousness ebbing and lapping like the tide. First was the local apothecarists. His children, and then this room. They had told him he was important, he was special and somehow he had what they needed. 

He'd lost count of the amount of times he'd answered those questions. The amount of times he had recalled his family. Their faces were beginning to blur. The next time the tall man came in, Ashaiek was able to say it: 'I can't help you anymore, let me die.'

The tall man looked down, an almost sympathetic smile the only thing visible on his face.
'Just one more day.'
