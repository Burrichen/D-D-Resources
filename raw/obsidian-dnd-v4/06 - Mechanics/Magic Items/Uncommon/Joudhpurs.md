## One-line identity
A well crafted pair of riding trousers.

## Effect
While wearing these trousers, you count as one size smaller when riding a nonhumanoid creature as a mount if you aren't wearing heavy armor.

## Appearance
The original Joudhpurs were a deep green with white sewn on around the hem. After years of criticism from Halingoites, Joudhpur created several alternate designs

## Lore
Her father a hunter and her mother a mage, Joudhpur was taken around the country constantly. Wanting to find her niche she refused to expected womanly expectations of being a mage and instead a station that was considered only acceptable for the old and young - making clothes. 

These days, Joudhpurs are known around the Halingo region for their high quality.
