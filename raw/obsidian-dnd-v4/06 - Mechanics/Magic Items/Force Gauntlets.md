# Force Gauntlets
*Wonderous Item, Rare, Requires attunement*

## One-line identity
Gauntlets that can push you or enemies away with an attack.
## Effect
5 charges and regains 1d2 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. When you make a melee attack with a hand that's wearing this gauntlet, you can choose to expend 1 of its charges as part of the attack. If it hits, the target takes an extra 2d6 force damage from the attack and must make a DC 15 Strength saving throw. On a failure, the target is pushed 10 feet away from you. On a success, you're pushed 10 feet away from it instead. Targets two or more sizes larger than you automatically succeed on the saving throw.
## Appearance
A non-descript metallic gauntlet with bright blue gems embedded across the knuckles, with a bulls head emblazoned on the back fist.
