 # General landmass and borders
 Ordon is the central landmass of Kurovia, grasping like a withered hand to the West, straining to touch the sunken lands of Thevethalas. The most western region was once considered part of Ordon but unlivable, but since Skgurr has risen from the grave, it has claimed independence. The constant storms prevent an all out war. 

To the south of Skgurr lies Shinkiro, another area once deemed unusable, now though connections are being forged, with a new thunderline network in construction to connect the two. On the south-east tip, a thin strip of land connects the Halingo desert with the mainlands, the connection is tentative and seemingly fragile. 

In the northwest, the only land Ordon ever unwillingly succeeded lies. Bratislor, the land of ARK, once beautiful and complete now destroyed utterly. The contmination threatens the world, and thusly the barrier was constructed. Maintained by the best and brightest (hmm) mages and artificers, it stands as the last line of defense from the horrors and beauty of Bratislor. It's a shame it was so flawed from the start. 

Jutting to the northern spine of the country, Hjartheland sits atop, straddling the border regions, separated by a mountainous sprawl, navigated by experienced sherpas and hunting crews.

# Travel Routes
All Major Settlements will have a thunderbolt directly connected to Morgenwelde, for transport of people and resources. Some larger settlements may even have another one to popular areas, but this is unlikely. The further from Morgenwelde (generally) the more primitive transport becomes. In 70% of the country, a horse drawn carriage is still normalised and expected.
