**Aureluch** (Awe-uh-lo-k) is one of the greatest industrial cities in Ordon outside Morgwen: a vast cliffside settlement of rail-yards, lift-chains, foundries, and suspended neighborhoods built around a colossal system of gates, bridges, and vertical engines. It is the city that **binds Ordon together** — a place where inland ore, outer-region grain, military freight, and Axius fuel all pass through before reaching the capital or the coast.
