# Ruined Keep
## Traits

## Lair Actions
**Crumbling Rubble.** Pieces of the structure crumble into three 5-foot cubes within 30 feet of the ruined keep creature. Creatures in those areas must succeed on a DC 12 plus PB Dexterity Saving throw or take PBd6 bludgeoning damage. Those areas become difficult terrain.

**Express Descent.** The ruined keep creature collapses a 5-foot square section of floor within 30 feet of them, opening a hole to the floor below. A medium or smaller creature in that area must succeed on a DC 12 plus PB Dex saving throw or fall through the floor.

**Swaying structure.** Old timber and decrepit stone sways, shifts and lurches. Each other creature witin 60 feet of the ruined keep creature must succeed on a DC 12 plus PB Strength saving throw or fall prone. Flying creatures automatically succeed on this saving throw.
