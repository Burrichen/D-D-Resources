#Rule #Mechanic #Original



On the start of your turn, eliminate one of the following:

- Action
- Bonus Action
- Reaction
- -10 feet of movement

Once you have lost all of these, drop to 0 HP and make death saving throws. If you pass, you are frozen in stone but are alive. If you fail you become your own grave.

No saves, no mercy.
How to stop:
- Kill the monster before you transform
- Drinking an antidote 
- Casting remove curse
- Pleading with a devil
- Doing something clever
