A creature might sustain a lingering injury under the following circumstances:
- When it drops to 0 hit points
- When it fails a saving throw by 5 points or more

# <font color="red"> Fire & Lightning</font>
Fire damage is characterised by its stacking effects, repeated falling to fire damage causes more and more intense effects.

| 2     | Fourth Degree Burns    | You have disadvantage on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution checks and saving throws. If you fail a saving throw against an effect that causes fire damage, you also gained the stunned condition until the end of your next turn.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | -         |
| 3     | Third Degree Burns     | You have disadvantage on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution checks and Constitution saving throws. Someone can tend to the burns and make a DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check once every week. After ten successes, the burns heal. # <font color="purple"> Necrotic </font>
Necrotic injuries tend to have longer lasting and debilitating effects, further hindering players without divine intervention.

| d20   | Injury                  | Description                                                                                                                                                                      | Length    |
| ----- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1     | Full Decay              | Your body is fundamentally damaged. Your hit point maximum is reduced by 1 every 24 hours, for every week that passes, a new body part falls off. Holy intervention is required. | 10 weeks  |
| 2     | Incubator               | You become an incubator for a new plague.                                                                                                                                        | 1d6 weeks |
| 3     | Dark Spirit             | You believe you see a demon inside of you. It my appear to you.                                                                                                                  | -         |
| 4     | Necrotic Discharge      | Everything you touch, including allies and items, takes 1d6 of necrotic damage.                                                                                                  | 1d4 weeks |
| 5-7   | Insanity.               | Roll on the long-term [[madness]] table.                                                                                                                                         | -         |
| 8-10  | Undead Stench           | You smell like rotting flesh. You have disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks. Magical healing of 6th level or higher removes the smell.                                   | 1d4 weeks |
| 11-13 | Minor Decay             | Your hit point maximum is reduced by 1 every 24 hours the wound persists. If your hit point maximum drops to 0, you die. The wound heals if you receive magical healing.         | -         |
| 14-16 | Inflammation            | Your muscles are irritated and inflamed. You have disadvantage on strength checks.                                                                                               | 2 weeks   |
| 17-20 | Necrotic Discolouration | You get white and grey spots on your cheeks. The spots don’t have any adverse effect. Magical healing of 6th level or higher, removes the spots.                                 | -         |
