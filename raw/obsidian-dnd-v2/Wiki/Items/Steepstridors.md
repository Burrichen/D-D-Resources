Steepstridors are large, spherical gyros. They have a cabin, like that of a captains quarters but are much larger. The outer gyro has been varnished with magic and a fabric canopy fills half of the space. The gyro can either decrease its mass and fill with helium, allowing the ship to float, or invesely fill with a heavier density, becoming a submarine.
