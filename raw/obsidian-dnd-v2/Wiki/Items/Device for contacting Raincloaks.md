Portable tripod made of wood, deploys onto a three-stand with spikes. After deploying into the ground, sucks in the air with a thunk. From the base, attach a metallic cylinder
(like a giant comical silencer in PAYDAY)
Once deployed, set the nozzle for distance and time. Deploy colored piece for urgency:
- Green - latest convience
- Orange - Serious
- Red - Immediate
- Black - Emergency
