# Basic Information:
Mourcroft is a medium sized city built around an ancient mausoleum complex, with large portions of it lying underground. The upper city consists of narrow, twisting streets and defensive walls. 
## Descriptions 
As your party approaches the imposing main gate of Mourcroft, a sense of both awe and trepidation washes over them. The gate, crafted from dark iron and adorned with intricate designs that hint at the city’s reverence for Kelemvor, stands as a formidable entryway. Guarded by stern-looking sentinels clad in deep gray uniforms, the atmosphere feels charged with an unspoken authority.

Upon reaching the gate, the guards halt the group with a practiced formality. "State your purpose for entering Mourcroft," one of them demands, eyes scanning each member of the party. The interview is thorough; they inquire about origins, intentions, and any potential disturbances the newcomers might bring. The air is thick with the weight of rules and regulations, and the guards make it clear that access to the underground areas of the city is a privilege not granted lightly. Only those with explicit reasons—like urgent business with the Death-Priests or a summons from the city’s leadership—will be permitted below.

Once through the gate, the party steps into the Botanical District, greeted by an unexpected contrast to the somber exterior. The sunlight filters through the canopy of lush, overhanging branches, casting playful shadows across the cobblestone paths. Vibrant flowers bloom in an array of colors, their sweet fragrances mingling with the earthy scent of damp soil. The gardens stretch endlessly, filled with peculiar plant life that seems to thrive in the city's unique climate, often shrouded in a gentle mist that rolls off the ground like a soft embrace.

This mist, locals whisper, is a blessing from Kelemvor, marking Mourcroft as one of the closest places to the realm of Death. It adds an ethereal quality to the gardens, where paths meander through beds of flowers, creating a labyrinthine beauty that beckons exploration.

Among the vibrant greenery, several holes punctuate the ground, wide enough for a person to peer down. Rays of light stream through these openings, illuminating the darkness below and hinting at the mysteries of the city’s underground layers. The soft rustling of leaves and the distant sound of water trickling blend into a calming ambiance, yet an undercurrent of unease remains palpable, as if the land itself holds secrets of its own.

As they navigate the botanical beauty, the party spots multiple signs proclaiming “Janee’s Fine Fabric.” The signs are elegantly crafted, featuring intricate designs of thread and needles, inviting passersby to explore the wares of this renowned tailor. The shop, known for its exceptional craftsmanship and unique fabrics, appears to be a local favorite, and the cheerful chatter of customers can be heard wafting from within.

In this vibrant yet somber atmosphere, the party can feel the duality of Mourcroft—its beauty intertwined with the ever-present acknowledgment of mortality and the watchful gaze of Kelemvor. Their adventure in this city has only just begun, and the path ahead promises intrigue, challenges, and the allure of secrets waiting to be uncovered.
