**Rookhaven** (15-17 days’ travel from Irvar):  
A mountain town nestled in the shadow of the Thornspire Peaks. Rookhaven was originally founded as a mining settlement, but its mines were long exhausted. Now, the town serves as a retreat for scholars and hermits. It is famous for its Rook Tower, an old observatory that once studied celestial movements and still holds ancient astronomical texts. The town has a melancholic air, as its population dwindles, leaving behind ruins of past prosperity.
