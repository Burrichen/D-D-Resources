# Plot hooks
The village leader, an Orc Arddrik Crane, has gone missing without a trace. The village elders offer a reward for anyone who can find out what happened to him. Rumors spread that something is stirring in the marsh, leading many to suspect foul play. **Twist**: Aldric discovered a long-lost salt extraction technique deep in the marshes but fell into a sinkhole while investigating. He is now trapped underground, and the party will need to navigate the treacherous, misty landscape to find him, all while avoiding deadly marsh creatures.

Locals have been reporting mysterious, glowing lights in the marsh at night. Some say they are spirits; others believe they’re a natural phenomenon. The village contracts the party to investigate before more fishermen go missing, fearing the lights might lure people to their deaths. **Twist**: The lights are from phosphorescent algae growing on a newly discovered, ancient wreck in the marsh. Pirates, however, have started using the lights as a lure to ambush travelers in the fog. The players must choose whether to fight off the pirates or avoid drawing more attention to the wreck, which may hold a dangerous secret.

The local baron has raised the salt tax significantly, which is causing unrest among the town’s salt miners and traders. The party is asked by the town’s merchants to negotiate a better deal, as the increased tax threatens to ruin their livelihoods. **Twist**: The baron’s tax hike was driven by corruption—he is using the extra income to fund a private expedition to recover hidden treasure in the marshlands. The party can choose to expose his scheme or bargain with him to uncover the location of the treasure for themselves.
