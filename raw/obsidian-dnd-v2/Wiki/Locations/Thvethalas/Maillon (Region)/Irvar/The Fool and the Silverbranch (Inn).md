## Summary

The Fool and the Silverbranch is the only tavern in Irvar, and thusly, there is a serious lack of clean surfaces. Whilst not outwardly dirty, the further you inspect a single surface, the more damp patches and dirt seem visible. Despite this, a cozy atmosphere is still able to be maintained. A roaring fire in the corner forces out the cold atmosphere from outside. A stage has been set up, but it is currently empty, and a layer of dust implies there hasn't been for quite some time. There are around 5 tables, but only one of which is empty.
