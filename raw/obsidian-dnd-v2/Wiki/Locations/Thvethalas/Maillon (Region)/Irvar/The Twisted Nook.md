## Summary

The Twisted Nook itself is nestled in-between two unassuming buildings and without Elora's knowledge, you feel you would have passed by it. However, on approach you see strange carvings of twisted trees and flowers. Stepping inside, the building itself is dimly lit and the smell of old herbs assails you, however you also smell a slight tinge of something mould like underneath it. The walls seem to all press down on you, and shelves line the walls and are overflowing with jars and potions of various sizes, each with a small handcrafted label. Most of these potions are ones you have all seen before in your travels, but a few are unique. A huge bowl of nuts are positioned on a table in the centre of the room. A wooden bell also stands next to it, with a label saying 'ring for attention'
