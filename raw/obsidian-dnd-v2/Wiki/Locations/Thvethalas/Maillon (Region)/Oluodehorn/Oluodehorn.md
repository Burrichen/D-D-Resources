# Title 
## Basic Information:
### Overview
Oluodehorn is a weathered, hardscrabble mining town nestled in the rocky foothills northeast of Irvar, surrounded by jagged mountains that loom like ancient, stoic sentinels. The town itself appears worn and rugged, bearing the marks of its grueling history and the harsh conditions it has endured. The roads leading into Oluodehorn are treacherous, winding through narrow mountain passes and deep ravines. Clusters of crooked wooden buildings line the uneven streets, the architecture practical and devoid of unnecessary adornment. The town seems to have grown out of the mountains themselves, the stone foundations of many homes and shops carved directly from the rock.

#### Unique Features

**The Great Split Quarry**: This vast, gaping wound in the earth is the lifeblood of Oluodehorn. It was once a simple mine for extracting valuable minerals, but after a seismic event several decades ago, the quarry expanded into a network of subterranean tunnels and caverns. The event, now referred to as "The Sundering," is said to have opened up ancient, hidden veins of rare metals and gemstones that made the town a key resource for the Rhalâin Empire. However, it also released strange, unseen forces that some believe have cursed the town.

**Echoing Stones**: Scattered around the quarry are towering, jagged rock formations that locals call the Echoing Stones. These mysterious pillars hum faintly at night, and many believe they are tied to the strange phenomena that occasionally plague Oluodehorn. Some of the older miners whisper that the Echoing Stones are remnants of an ancient civilization, long forgotten, and that disturbing the earth around them has stirred their dormant magic.

**The Black Lantern Tavern**: A central gathering place for miners, mercenaries, and travelers alike, the Black Lantern is a dimly lit, smoke-filled tavern with an unsettling aura. It’s not just the dark, grimy interior that gives the tavern its reputation—many claim to have seen shadowy figures slipping through the cracks in the walls or heard voices whispering from the floorboards. The tavern is owned by a grizzled ex-miner named Galvar Gristlehand, a man with more scars than anyone can count and a demeanor that speaks of horrors left untold.

**The Hollowed Workshop**: Hidden away at the edge of the quarry, the Hollowed Workshop is a derelict forge where master blacksmiths once crafted legendary weapons and tools. Now, it's abandoned and crumbling, but rumors persist that the forge still holds ancient equipment capable of infusing weapons with eldritch powers. Few dare enter, as those who do often return babbling incoherently about strange shadows and faces seen in the glowing embers.

**Rhalâin Watchtower**: Overlooking the town, a crumbling, heavily fortified watchtower serves as a grim reminder of the Rhalâin Empire’s presence. Though it’s been abandoned for years, the tower still holds strategic importance, and occasional patrols of the Empire’s forces come through to maintain a semblance of control over the town. Inside the watchtower, it’s rumored that secret imperial experiments were conducted during The Sundering, and deep in its bowels, something foul might still be lurking.

#### History

Founded over a century ago, Oluodehorn began as a humble mining outpost. Its population boomed during the discovery of vast mineral deposits in the nearby mountains, attracting settlers, traders, and opportunists alike. The town grew rapidly, becoming a critical supplier of raw materials to the Rhalâin Empire’s burgeoning biothaumaturgy industry. The event known as The Sundering drastically changed the town’s fortune. While it unlocked previously inaccessible mineral veins, it also brought about mysterious disappearances, strange apparitions, and the gradual decay of the town’s social fabric.

Over time, miners and their families began to speak of strange sightings in the tunnels, odd sounds at night, and bizarre accidents that claimed lives. Some say the town has been cursed ever since, while others believe that it's merely the result of greed—digging too deep into forbidden earth. In recent years, Oluodehorn has begun to empty out, with fewer miners willing to risk their lives for dwindling profits.

#### Potential Plot Hooks

1. **The Disappearance of the Overseer**: The former overseer of the quarry, a man named Rezak Torvain, vanished without a trace a month ago. The town’s remaining leadership is too afraid to investigate properly, but rumors circulate that Rezak found something deep within the tunnels—something that drove him mad or worse. The players could be hired by the miners' guild to find out what happened to Rezak, leading them into the heart of the cursed quarry and the Echoing Stones.
    
2. **The Whispers in the Quarry**: Miners have begun reporting strange whispers in the dark while working deep underground. These whispers speak of long-buried secrets and dangerous knowledge, tempting those who hear them into dangerous actions. Several miners have gone missing recently, believed to be lured into the depths by these disembodied voices. The party could be tasked with investigating the source of these whispers, which might turn out to be the remnants of an ancient spirit or a malevolent entity awakened by The Sundering.
    
3. **The Echoing Stones' Curse**: A miner collapses in the street, muttering incoherently about the Echoing Stones. His body is riddled with strange, glowing runes that fade away as he dies. The townsfolk believe he was cursed by the stones, and a growing number of them are too frightened to continue their work. The party could investigate the nature of this "curse" and discover that the stones are remnants of an ancient spellwork designed to keep something contained within the earth—a containment that may be weakening.
    
4. **The Black Lantern’s Secret**: The Black Lantern Tavern is rumored to be a front for an illicit organization trading in dangerous Wild Magic artifacts. Players could be hired by a suspicious town official or rival group to infiltrate the tavern and uncover the truth. This could lead to a tense social encounter where the players must navigate the tavern’s dangerous clientele or a stealth mission to find hidden rooms where the artifacts are stored.
    
5. **The Hollowed Workshop’s Legacy**: An old blacksmith approaches the party with a mysterious request—to retrieve a lost artifact from the Hollowed Workshop. The artifact is said to be the key to forging a weapon powerful enough to protect the town from whatever has been lurking in the tunnels. The party must venture into the dilapidated forge, confronting the strange, ghostly apparitions and traps left behind by the master blacksmiths who once worked there.
    
6. **The Broken Watchtower**: A message from the Rhalâin Empire arrives, requesting the party's assistance in reclaiming the old watchtower. The Empire has lost contact with the patrols they sent, and the townsfolk have reported strange lights and sounds coming from within the tower. The party will need to investigate the dilapidated fortress, battling through its crumbling halls and uncovering the truth behind the Rhalâin experiments that took place within.
    
7. **The Miners’ Revolt**: Tensions between the miners and the town’s leadership are reaching a boiling point. The miners claim that they are being sent to work deeper into the tunnels than ever before, risking their lives for dwindling rewards. The leadership, fearing a full revolt, hires the party to act as mediators—or enforcers. As the players become embroiled in the town’s political turmoil, they may uncover a deeper conspiracy involving the town’s leadership and the Rhalâin Empire.
    
8. **The Veins of Bloodstone**: Bloodstone, a rare and powerful mineral, has been found deep within the mountains. Its discovery has sparked a rush of interest from the Rhalâin Empire and other powerful factions. The party could be hired to escort a caravan of Bloodstone through the dangerous mountain passes, facing ambushes from rival factions, wild magic storms, or worse—supernatural entities tied to the very nature of the Bloodstone itself.
    

Oluodehorn stands as a harsh and mysterious frontier, filled with potential riches but cursed by the dark secrets it harbors beneath its rocky surface. The party's time here will be fraught with danger and intrigue, as the town's eerie history slowly unravels before them.
