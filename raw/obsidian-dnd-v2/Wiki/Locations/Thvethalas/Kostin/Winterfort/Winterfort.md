### Stats
**Origin**: Military Camp. A deployment wound up lasting longer than anticipated, so the camp began building more solid fortifications in an increasingly permanent fashion.
**Priority**: Military. The town prioritizes defences and law enforcement. 
**Specialty**: Education. The town is known as the location of a widely-renowned educational institution. (Free Location: Non-Commercial - Place of Education)
**Age**: Mature. The town has been around for decades. 
**Size**: Medium. Up to 1000 standing structures in an area able to support around 4000 people.
**Condition:** Moderate. Structures seem sound enough though are, perhaps, not attractive. It is likely that most homes keep occupants dry. Roads are relatively clear, if not clean. The smells are not pleasant, but tolerable.
**Ruler(s)**:
**Key NPCs'**:

**Prosperity:** Successful. The town is functional and generates a modest to good amount of coin.
**Market Square**: Tight. Only room for a few, key vendor stalls.
**Defences:** Formidable, Strong, Average, Weak, Pathetic, None.
**Religion**: [[Tyr, God of Justice]]
**Environment**: Mountain. The town is found on a plateaux of a stony pass or soaring peak.
Fortification: Heavily Fortified. The town proper is surrounded by a heavy wall of wood or stone, with several watchtowers built along its length. The gate is double-thickness and reinforced with metal bands. There are additional watchtowers built at various points throughout the surrounding countryside. 


## Community:
**Population Density**: Dense. There is a large amount of people living here. There are few, if any, vacant buildings. In high traffic areas, one generally has elbow room, but not much more. 
**Population Overflow**:
**Farms and Resources**: Resource Harvesting (Stone/ore). Farming (Livestock)
**Visitor Traffic**: Crowds. A noticeable amount of people come through town on a regular basis. Congestion is increased. 
**Night Activity**: Slow. Almost everything is closed except for taverns, which may be open until early morning, and inns, which stay open perpetually.
**Demographics**:
**Disposition**: Open. The locals actively enjoy visitors, and their culture incorporates this. Just about anyone is welcome.
**Leadership**: Military Rule. The town is controlled by a current or ex-leader of a military group. 
**Law Enforcement**: Strong Town Watch. The watch is run by a seasoned captain with two lieutenants, a few corporals, and an ample quantity of boots on the ground. Presence allows for extra patrols and well-rested guards. (+4 to crime roll)
**Population Wealth**: Prosperous. The majority have enough to live a good life and, of them, a fair amount can even live comfortably. (+1 to quality rolls)
**Crime**: Uncommon. Crime does not occur often but, when it does, it is a noteworthy occurrence. (+1 to urban encounter rolls)
