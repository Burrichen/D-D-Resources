Small but highly influential, the Arcane Collegium rarely takes more than a few dozen students. Its fields of study are peculiar as well, with an emphasis on clockwork magic (sometimes called gear magic), dwarven forms of alchemy and divination, and the school of illumination, sometimes called star and shadow magic. It is led by Collegium Guildmaster Orlando, a wizard of deep insight and peculiar habits, prone to whimsy and to powerful intuition. Other masters of the collegium specialize in divination and illusion, but the outside world sees the Farrow Collegium as both inventive and frankly bizarre, with little respect for the traditions and practices upheld elsewhere. At the same time, it’s hard to argue with results, and the Arcane Collegium turns out excellent students with shocking regularity, keeping up with the much more cutthroat schools of Bemmea and Runklestad.
