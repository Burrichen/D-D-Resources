# Story:
A country proud of it's dwarven heritage. Before [[The Oblate]], it was held by particularly aggressive Dwarves, protecting their home. However, when [[The Oblate]] finally hit, they were some of the first Dwarves to lay down their arms and provide aid to others. They welcome humans back, and argued for continued co-operation in the years to come. With [[Drogon Bronzebeard]] playing a vital role in the collective effort to support the continent moving forward. However, what started as a way to repair the damage caused by [[The Oblate]], became a sweeping force of vengance and renewed power. Corrupted by the Five Nobels, [[Drogon Bronzebeard|Drogon]] retreated and publicly denounced the organistion he believed in, what was now known as the [[Tolagean Empire]] in the dead of night. Troops followed closely after, with knowledge of the landscape and politically motivated troops, the old and new Limiates fought together to drive off Tolagean supports and troops. 

Relations remain tense, and whilst no wars are fought, political moves are constantly being made across the country, desperate to gain footing. Limate however, remains: a small and proud nation, determined never to be tricked or taken advantage of again.
