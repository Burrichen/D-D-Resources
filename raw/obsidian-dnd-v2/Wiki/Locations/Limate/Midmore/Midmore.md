## Background
For several hours you and the party venture northwards. The forest seems familiar, and yet slightly different, every now and then you pause and take stock, attempting to recall the right way. After clambering over mossy tree logs and upturned rocks you hear running water. You follow it upstream, the slight feeling of familiarity becoming even more apparent. After a few minutes of following it, you turn in-between two trees, and see the village. It remains almost frozen in time, the dark, brooding forest and damp cliffs dominating the landscape behind the town, naturally enclosing it in. A flock of birds fly overhead. The village initially appears as it ever was, isolated almost timeless in this grassy plain. The thatch houses still stand proud, but as you gaze upon them you can't help but feel as if the whole town is smaller than it once was. The stone city hall still stands modestly in the centre. The streets which once felt alive, now feels like a dull and washed out painting.
