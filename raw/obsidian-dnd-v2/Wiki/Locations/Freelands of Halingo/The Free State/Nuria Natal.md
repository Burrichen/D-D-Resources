Nuria Natal is a holy group of zealots who worship the Tree of Life. Rumour has it this tree of life came to life a few hundred years ago, but no one believes that. Weird but oddly peaceful, their town is the one only place that looks relatively normal - lush landscapes and borderline rainforests surround their lake.
