Sometimes called the Invisible College, this infamous group was founded by a group of wizards and apprentices who survived the Oblate and agreed to meet 20 years later to restore the magic of their lost lands. They include necromancers, ooze wizards, astromancers, and diabolists. Widely considered an evil and malicious group, they use magical kidnapping, blackmail, and extortion to gather funds, and ally with the worst elements of magical practice in Halingo and elsewhere.
