As time rages on, the hope is by renaming this place the ‘Yuan-Ti Ziggurat’ instead of it’s original name will empower the Yuan-Ti race. Unfortunately that much time hasn’t passed. Seneth built this structure, originally a haven for his people, but eventually became a monument to the monster he became. Despite his change, it is still considered a holy site, and a safe place to Yuan-Ti a symbol of their hope. It’s beginning to sustain itself as it’s own settlement now however.
