Far to the west of The Free State, embedded at the base of Alma’s Neck, a tower stands, blazing its light even during the light of day. By far the most technologically advanced civilisation in Halingo, known for its debauchery, questionable ethics and unique set of laws. 

  

Whilst Val may have visited Sunspite (it sounds like his kinda place) it is extremely unlikely the travelling circus would have toured here - these guys hire in house with stringent security checks and a ‘circus’ is pretty vanilla for these guys.
