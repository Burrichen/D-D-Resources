Heresy is one of the oldest and most respected places in Halingo, so much so it could be described as one of the only cities in the region. Two-Hundred years ago, a warlock known as Seneth of Draugr declared war on the (at the time) small town, declaring it to be his target of holy war. His follower’s attacked the city overnight, with only three members of law enforcement being able to defend their home.. Their names were Rael, Garrus and Corrin. Rael and Garrus were the deputy and Sheriff respectively, and had been married for some time. Corrin was their adoptive son, who had previously adventured with Seneth, but split after realising the true nature of his companion. In the fight, Garrus passed, supposedly bringing Rael to surrender. Corrin though, remained. 


The two previous friends battled, even when all others gave up and died around them. Eventually, upon seeing the destruction and death around them, the two gave up. Some say both of them forever wander the desert, each seeking each other for a finale. Some say they walked away that day. Some say they died. All that is known is that they haven’t been seen again. All that is known that, a few years after the initial combat, Rael founded Heresy II (usually just referred to as Heresy) and alongside it, the Raincloaks, determined to never let what happened to his husband, happen again. 

These days, Heresy is a place of peace and prosperity. It has functioning mines, is connected via Thunderbolt network to larger settlements, has strict rules and is where many Raincloaks are trained.
