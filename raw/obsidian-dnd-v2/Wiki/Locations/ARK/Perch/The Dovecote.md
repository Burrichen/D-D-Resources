Built into a void in the side of the Spire wall is the
Dovecote, a breeding farm for pigeons, doves, ravens
and sparrows maintained by a cadre of leather-coated
and dropping-stained artisans. Megacorvids are ex-
pensive and large, so are rarely found here – but the
best carrier pigeons can be, so the Ministry maintains
an anonymous account with several bird-masters to
ensure that their operatives can communicate over
long distances without recourse to miracles.
