#Location
## Overview
**Type**: Church

Inspired by church of atom from FO4 dlc.

In the network of the Stormwall lies a small, thin path. Not many would even dare to walk along this path, with the sea crashing below, but if you were to do so, you would find nothing but a rock wall. Or at least, what's what it may look like. In fact, one may find an odd handle, built into the wall. A strong tug and the wall moves apart, and the cavern opens. Full of metal structures, candles and small windows peering out into the tumbling depths below, the room is large, but filled with shrines and offerings to ancient and forgotten deities. Constant preaches and sermons in different languages and species are constantly given.
