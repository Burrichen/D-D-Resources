# Stormwall Overview
 
```ccard
type: folder_brief_live
```

 The stormwall is the lowest section of Ark, acting as the support and internal inter-structure for Ark. Not many apart from the severely poor and immigrants. There are two main gates in which boats and [[Steepstridors]] can enter.
