## Overview
Gond is the Goddess of [[Dwarf]], advocate for women-kind. While her husband, [[Helm, God of Protection|Helm]] went out to protect others and fight, Gond would supply him with the best possible equipment. This has lead to many [[Dwarf]] rejecting Helm as a Diety, and believing Gond deserves more respect. Apart from unique designs for Helm, she provides most of her creations to [[Dornia, Goddess of Knowledge|Dornia]], and can even be considered an aspect of her.

**Alignment**: Neutral Good
**Domains**: Knowledge
**Sides with:** Neither
