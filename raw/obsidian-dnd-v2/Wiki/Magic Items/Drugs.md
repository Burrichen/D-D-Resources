# Powders
Venge: Angry, low addiction
Powdered Power (Pow Pow): A glittering substance that looks almost like diamond dust, consumed by snorting. You become confident, and gain adv on everything.
# Liquids
Deluge: Connects you to the ancient water-systems of the world. Can cast spells but after one is cast, you are dependent on it forever.
Oblion: A gray, misty, gross liquid. Forgetful. 
# Injectors
Agony: The distilled essence of pain. Used by torturers and sadists.
Sannish: Allows temporary connection to the weave.
# Other
Coral: Endless, peaceful dreams. High addiction.
Patch: Comes in many different variants, often used to heighten emotions. Medium addiction.
