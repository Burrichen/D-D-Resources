# Traits
Race: Human

Appearance: Tall, gaunt, with silvering black hair pulled back in a severe manner. His face is sharp, with high cheekbones, hollow eyes, and deep lines etched by years of contemplation and worship. He dresses in black ceremonial robes adorned with silver thread, embroidered with the symbols of Kelemvor—a skeletal hand with one finger down. He carries a silvered ceremonial staff topped with a skull carved from onyx.

**Personality**: Cenric is patient, calm, and serious, but he carries an air of fatalism. He believes in predestination and fate, which gives him a detached approach to conflict and governance. Despite his coldness, he has a deep compassion for those suffering loss, offering them counsel in his personal chapel. He is known for his quiet acts of charity, aiding those on the edge of death, though he shows no sympathy for those who attempt to cheat death.

# Background
Cenric Mourbraith comes from a long line of death-priests, with his family having ruled Mourcraft for centuries, their legacy intertwined with the city’s function as a center of death rites. His father, before him, was also High Lord, but Cenric surpassed him in piety and influence within the church of Kelemvor. It is said that Cenric himself performed the death rites on his parents, refusing to grieve publicly, instead urging the city to honor their passage with calm acceptance.

# Relationships
- **Priesthood of Kelemvor**: As High Lord, Cenric holds strong influence over the death-priests and the various religious orders within Mourcraft. He is often at odds with younger, more progressive priests who want to soften the city's rigid practices.

- **Ruling Council**: Mourcraft is technically governed by a small council, but Cenric’s word carries the most weight. The council is composed of various figures who oversee trade, law, and infrastructure, but Cenric’s rule is particularly focused on maintaining the city’s strict adherence to the natural cycle of life and death.

# Conflict
Though Cenric rules with devotion, tensions are rising as Mourcraft faces growing pressures from outside influences. Some wish to modernize or alter the city's focus on death, while Cenric remains a stalwart defender of tradition, refusing to yield to these changes.
