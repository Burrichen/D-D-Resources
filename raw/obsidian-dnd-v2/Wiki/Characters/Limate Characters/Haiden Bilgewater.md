## History
Haiden was born with the name 'Faesolor' - a beautiful wood elf, a princess of her people. She was given practically everything she wanted and given the best treatment and training available. But doubts of her leadership filled her mind, and constant fears of the future plagued her mind. It was during this time she met with an old lady, who promised her anything. And she wished to be unburdened with her future. 

And thus, her entire body changed. Warping and changing her into a Giant Rat. Stealing her memories (maybe her body too) 
Her entire body changed, transforming her into a Giant Rat, and unleashing the [[Were-Rat Curse]] upon the world and herself. With time, Haiden was able to reform her appearance, trying to keep hold of her old form, but mostly failing. She is able to remain a Wood Elf in appearance, but her features are long since lost. 

She since sworn to take anything and everything from the Tolagean's, becoming a pirate using her survivability learnt from her years as a rat. Each of her new 'family' is bitten by Haiden in her Rat form, and also becomes a were-rat as a sign of undying loyalty. Traitors have left the [[Bilgerats]], for one reason or another however, leading to pockets of Were-rat infestations. 

## Skills
Haiden is a charming individual who if underestimated or misplayed can lead to manipulation. She is equally skilled with a sword and loves stories. She is able to command a ship with ease.

## Personality Traits
“*If you want to travel with me, you need to be aware, the person here now and the person you'll meet on the Seas are not the same person.*” 
Haiden's personality is two-fold. In public and private she is a attentive and welcoming person, if a little guarded. However on the Seas she is a different beast - commanding, stern and brutal, Haiden allows the animal in her to take control; however will be merciful is a genuine choice is given. 

## Ideals
"*Those bastards robbed me of everything, and now people complain I take their gold?*"
Haiden is bitter at what she's lost, unaware of the [[Rurgarth Remains]] and determines to steal as much as she can from Tolagean shipments. Occasionally this will go wrong, and theft from the wrong person is not uncommon. If Haiden deems those shipments as having 'good intentions' she will donate them to the [[Purse Cutters]]. An easy way to forge or break a bond with her is your attitude to the Tolageans. 

## Bonds
"*I once had people I cared for, but now, I have a new family.*"
She loves the [[Bilgerats]], as if they are her family but despises the [[Tolagean Empire]] more. She retains a working relationship with the [[Purse Cutters]] but doesn't necessarily believe they can make a real difference. She firmly believes Parliment are for nerds and losers.

She sees members of the [[Bilgerats]] as her own family, and will only induct them if she deems them worthy. Her and [[Drogon Bronzebeard]] will go out for drinks fortnightly, but apart from that doesn't really have any 'friends' which aren't the [[Bilgerats]].

## Flaws
*"The plan? Plans are for people with no imagination."*
Haiden only cares about the now, not the future. She has given up on her life and lives for the [[Bilgerats]], and lacks the motivation to reclaim was is rightfully hers.
