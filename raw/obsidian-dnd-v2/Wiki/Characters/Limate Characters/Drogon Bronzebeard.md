## History
Drogon was born and experienced most of the [[The War for Evon]]. He fought hard against the Humans, and lived to regret each kill. By the end of the war he was filled with regret. The Oblate was an excuse for him to finally do some good in the world, and he helped forge a vital relationship between humans, taking a human wife. 

 However, what started as a way to repair the damaged land and its people became a sweeping force of vengeance and renewed power. Seeing his hard work being corrupted by [[The Five Nobles]], Drogon publicly made a stand against the [[Tolagean Empire]], which resulted in an assassination attempt, leading to the death of his wife and child. Enraged, Drogon fled the captial that night, armed troops following closely behind.
 
 On arrival to Khundur, he joined with various dissenters against the Empire, biding his time, fighting off assassins until enough troops were deemed to make a stand. With knowledge of the landscape, highly motivated troops, and various dwarven and human generals (including [[Mathak Eisenberg]]) the old and new Limates fought together to drive off Tolagean residents and the oncoming troops. 

After the bloodshed, Drogon passed up the opportunity for King, still mourning over the ones he had slain, as well as his own wife and child. He did however, accept a coastal town as a reward for his bravery and sacrifice. Hence how how Drogon Bronzebeard became the mayor of [[Oxwick (Old)]].
