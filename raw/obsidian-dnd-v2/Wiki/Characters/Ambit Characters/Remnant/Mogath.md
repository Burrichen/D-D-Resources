He began his studies with a local mage and quickly became a prodigy in the field. He soon earned a reputation as one of the most talented students in the village, and soon enough he was invited to join a secret society of magic-tech experts. At first, Zircon was hesitant, as he had no desire to leave his family and home behind. But eventually, he accepted the invitation, and he soon found himself in the midst of a group of people who shared his passion for combining technology and magic. Zircon quickly rose in the ranks of the secret society, eventually becoming one of the most respected members. He was often called upon to help devise new inventions and magical artifacts, and his skill in both areas was unmatched. Now, Zircon is a respected member of the secret society, and he often serves as a liaison between the magical and technological.
