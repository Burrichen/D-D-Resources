## History
She was born with a gift for wild magic, but her parents, fearful of its destructive power, taught her to control and contain it from a young age. As she grew older, she became increasingly aware of the ever-growing dangers of wild magic, and she eventually decided to dedicate her life to helping clean up the wild magical messes that were cropping up all over the land. She studied with some of the best magic-tech teachers in the region, honing her skills and eventually becoming one of the foremost experts in her field. She has since become a renowned figure in her village, and is often called upon to help clean up magical messes that occur both in and around the village. She is known for her determination, resourcefulness and courage in the face of danger. Feena is a kind-hearted soul, who takes great satisfaction in helping others. She believes that her work makes a difference and that one day, wild magic will no longer be a threat.
