## History
During the events of [[Seneth's Uprising]], Hadlar and the rest of the [[Chordus]] chapter were forced to defend their boss Arkmos. Upon Arkmos' death, Hadlar gave up on the system that abandoned him. Creating a new chapter of the Ambit, [[Remnant]] Hadlar distanced himself from the main force and became his own squad leader. They now travel the group, solving crimes with kindness and love using Ambit technology. For nearly 100 years, Hadlar had relative peace until some new adventurer's [[The Rise of Orcus|summoned a demon]] until the world.

Whilst the world was clawing for the deaths of the adventurers, Hadlar decided (with Feena's help) that there was probably more to this story. He arranged a 'test' for the recruits via the [[Session 14 + 15 - The Case of the Lamenting Lighthouse (One-Shot)|Case of the Lamenting Lighthouse]], which lead to Valakoss' death. With their attempt to do the right thing proven, Hadlar sent off a construct of a person 'Corvin' - based off of Corrin to help guide them.

The plan? Recruit the adventurer's to find Orcus' artifacts that have awakened across the world. The Ambit are too busy trying to contain Orcus, so they have no space to do it. Upon finding the three artefacts, Feena can get them legally acquitted. Or, will help them run away and craft new identities. If they don't, then the full force of the law will be against them. If they don't, Orcus may very well destroy the world.
