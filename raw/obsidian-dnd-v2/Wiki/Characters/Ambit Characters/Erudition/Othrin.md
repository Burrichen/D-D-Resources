## History
Othrin was raised as a devout follower of Dornia, but others had plans for him. As a sick twist of fate, he attracted Azun's attention with his loyalty, spiking jealousy in her heart. Thus, Azun blessed them with the powers of divination. 

Initially Othrin was glad to use these new skills and used them in the name of Dornia, however Azun struck - taking Othrin's eyesight. They devoted themselves to Azun fully, and Azun blessed him with eyesight, with just one eye. The other eye now glimmers and stars can occasionally be seen in it. Rumours spread and eventually [[Gendor]] heard of him, and got to work recruiting him. Othrin was the third official member of The Ambit.
