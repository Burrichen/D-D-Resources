## Overview
6 adventurers 

The Unspoken Shield was a small-time band of adventurers determined to do the right thing. The six of them had been friends since childhood to create a group to create a life for themselves, and right wrongs in the world. Each contributing slightly to their base of operations. After a few successful dungeon runs, an enigmatic stranger walked into their home, calling himself Harlo Honeygrin. Supposedly an entrepreneur from the Freelands, Harlo offered to help finance the group. For a while, things were good, even making a local name for themselves. 

Then the bills came. Harlo became more and more agitated, becoming more and more aggressive. Blackmail, violence and obsession. A deal was made, one last job and then the group and Harlo would part ways. Harlo needed an ancient vault/tomb raided for an artefact. They agreed.
