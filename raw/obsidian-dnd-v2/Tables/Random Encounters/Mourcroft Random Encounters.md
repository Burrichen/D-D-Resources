1. In Mourcroft, bodies are being found dead without any visible wounds or signs of a struggle. The victims appear to have simply died in their sleep, their hearts stopped for no apparent reason. These mysterious deaths are spreading unease throughout the city, with rumors that Kelemvor, the God of Death, is punishing the people of Mourcroft for some unknown sin.
	The deaths are actually caused by a corrupt sect of Death-Priests experimenting with a rare herbal concoction from their apothecaries, believed to give them control over life and death. They secretly administer this mixture to select townsfolk, hoping to perfect a method of painless euthanasia, which they see as a way to help Kelemvor's faithful pass on peacefully. Unfortunately, their experiments are backfiring, leading to unexpected and premature deaths. The priests did not intend for their actions to cause such widespread fear, but their attempts to keep the experiments hidden have led to tragic consequences.

2. A prominent gravedigger named Malrick has vanished, leaving graves unfilled and creating unrest among the people. The Death-Priests are at a loss, fearing a curse, as rumors circulate that Malrick stumbled upon something he shouldn't have while digging—a hidden chamber filled with relics and artifacts from a bygone age. Curiosity got the better of him, and he began researching the items, leading him to uncover dark secrets about the city’s past and its connection to necromancy. As he delved deeper, he attracted the attention of a malevolent spirit or perhaps even a cult that seeks to reclaim those artifacts for their own nefarious purposes.

4. A revered Death-Priest is rumored to be conducting forbidden rituals to commune with Kelemvor. The townspeople are torn between their loyalty to the priest and their fear of his potential corruption. The party is asked to investigate the priest's activities and determine whether he is a true servant of Kelemvor or has fallen into dark practices. The twist: the priest is attempting to gain divine favor to protect Mourcroft from a looming threat, and the party must decide whether to support his actions or expose him to the council.

6. **The Mourning Tome**: An old book, believed to be a historical account of Mourcroft’s burial practices, has been stolen from the town's library. The book was rumored to contain valuable knowledge about the city’s death customs and rituals, which some believe could hold the key to understanding recent mysterious deaths in the area. The Death-Priests are worried that without the book, the community may lose touch with its traditions and rituals surrounding death, potentially leading to unrest among the townsfolk. **Twist**: The theft was orchestrated by a desperate local who believed the book contained secrets that could protect their family from the curse of a recent death in the family. They had heard whispers that the book held a way to commune with the dead. The party must track down the thief and recover the book, only to discover that it contains no magic or secrets but rather practical advice on how to cope with grief. The real resolution lies in helping the thief confront their fears and finding solace in the community rather than searching for supernatural solutions.
