Short-Term Madness

|d100|Effect (lasts 1d10 minutes)|
|---|---|
|01–20|The character retreats into his or her mind and becomes [paralyzed](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_8). The effect ends if the character takes any [damage](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Combat?expansion=34047#toc_46).|
|21–30|The character becomes [incapacitated](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_6) and spends the duration screaming, laughing, or weeping.|
|31–40|The character becomes [frightened](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_4) and must use his or her [action](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Combat?expansion=34047#toc_20) and [movement](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Movement?expansion=34047#content) each round to flee from the source of the fear.|
|41–50|The character begins babbling and is incapable of normal speech or [spellcasting](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Spells?expansion=34047#toc_1).|
|51–60|The character must use his or her [action](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Combat?expansion=34047#toc_20) each round to [attack](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Combat?expansion=34047#toc_21) the nearest creature.|
|61–70|The character experiences vivid hallucinations and has [disadvantage](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_2) on [ability checks](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_4).|
|71–75|The character does whatever anyone tells him or her to do that isn’t obviously self-­destructive.|
|76–80|The character experiences an overpowering urge to eat something strange such as dirt, slime, or offal.|
|81–90|The character is [stunned](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_13).|
|91–100|The character falls [unconscious](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_14).|
Long-Term

|d100|Effect (lasts 1d10 × 10 hours)|
|---|---|
|01–10|The character feels compelled to repeat a specific activity over and over, such as washing hands, touching things, praying, or counting coins.|
|11–20|The character experiences vivid hallucinations and has [disadvantage](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_2) on [ability checks](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_4).|
|21–30|The character suffers extreme paranoia. The character has [disadvantage](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_2) on [Wisdom](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_30) and [Charisma checks](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_33).|
|31–40|The character regards something (usually the source of madness) with intense revulsion, as if affected by the antipathy effect of the [Antipathy/Sympathy](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Antipathy%20Sympathy?expansion=34047#content) spell.|
|41–45|The character experiences a powerful delusion. Choose a potion. The character imagines that he or she is under its effects.|
|46–55|The character becomes attached to a “lucky charm,” such as a person or an object, and has [disadvantage](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_2) on [attack rolls](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Combat?expansion=34047#toc_32), [ability checks](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_4), and [saving throws](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_35) while more than 30 feet from it.|
|56–65|The character is [blinded](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_1) (25%) or [deafened](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_3) (75%).|
|66–75|The character experiences uncontrollable tremors or tics, which impose [disadvantage](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_2) on [attack rolls](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Combat?expansion=34047#toc_32), [ability checks](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_4), and [saving throws](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_35) that involve Strength or Dexterity.|
|76–85|The character suffers from partial amnesia. The character knows who he or she is and retains [racial traits](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Races?expansion=34047#toc_1) and [class](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Classes%20by%20Name?expansion=34047#content) features, but doesn’t recognize other people or remember anything that happened before the madness took effect.|
|86–90|Whenever the character takes [damage](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Combat?expansion=34047#toc_46), he or she must succeed on a DC 15 [Wisdom](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_29) [saving throws](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_35) or be affected as though he or she failed a saving throw against the [confusion](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Spells:Confusion?expansion=34047#content) spell. The confusion effect lasts for 1 minute.|
|91–95|The character loses the ability to speak.|
|96–100|The character falls [unconscious](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_14). No amount of jostling or damage can wake the character.|
