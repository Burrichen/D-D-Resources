# Level 3
## Shabiz
These abilities require one 'Hero' token to use each. These abilities will affect your affliction in the long run and which ones you choose to use will be noted. You do not need to share these abilities with other players until they are used.

- **Higher Marmot**: You embrace your Marmot curse and allow it to give you strength. You turn into a Marmot without using a wild shape charge, however are able to speak, and cast spells as normal. This lasts until 1 hour is over, or you choose to end it. I
- **Marmot's Poison**: You expel your Marmot curse and channel is absence to empower your attacks, when used in Marmot form, gain 1d6 extra poison damage every time you successfully attack another creature. This lasts until 1 hour is over, you choose to end it or you transform back into a human.
## Din'jul:
This ability requires one Hero token to use. You do not need to share this ability with other players until is is used.

- **Forget-me-now:** Your latent powers of sorcery have become twinned and exacerbated with your forgetfulness. When you use this token, any NPC you are speaking to will forget the last sentence that was spoken to them. You do not need to share this ability until it is used. In combat, this is a bonus action.

## Valakos

You can pick ONE of these:


Complex: 
You use a token to enter a Primal Rage. This does not require drugs. 
A primal rage lasts 5 turns, OR until a turn passes without you taking damage or dealing damage. A primal rage lasts exactly as a normal rage, aside from the following benefits:

Upon a successful attack, gain 1 Ferocity, up 5 stacks of Ferocity. Ferocity last until the next time you enter combat.
You can spend the tokens on the following effects:
Intimidate (1 Ferocity): The next intimidation check you make it made with advantage.
Charge! (1+ Ferocity): You may move up 5 feet x the ferocity spent without invoking attack of opportunity.
Hurricane Blow (3 Ferocity): You can activate this exploit whenever you take the Attack action with a melee weapon. The first time you hit a creature with a weapon attack this turn, the attack deals its normal effects, and you can push the target up to 10 feet away from you.
Cleave (5 Ferocity): You can activate this exploit whenever you take the Attack action with a melee weapon. Automatically deal half damage of your attack towards another target within 5 foot of you. 
More options will unlock at higher levels.
