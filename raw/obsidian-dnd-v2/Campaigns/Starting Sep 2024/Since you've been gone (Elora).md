On your journey back to Irvar, you start reminiscing about your time there. Your parents were decently respected, and in such a small town as Irvar, it seems odd that more discussion hasn't been around your parents disappearance. Furthermore, you knew that a few years ago, a local group of adventurers had taken up residency just past the town's border, and sworn by the Gods to protect Irvar. So why hadn't they done anything? These questions continue to plague you, and with no solution in sight you try and push your concerns down.



Due to growing up around this area, you gain the following benefits (you may make note of this, or not its up to you! Realistically we won't be spending ages in Irvar):
- Advantage on history checks relating to Irvar.
- Advantage on survival / guiding (ill explain this roll on the day!) whilst within a days travel of Irvar.
- Local discount - You will gain a minor purchase discount at stores at Irvar (unless the store is new.
