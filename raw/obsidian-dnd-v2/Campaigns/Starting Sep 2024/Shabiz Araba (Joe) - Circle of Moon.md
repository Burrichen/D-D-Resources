DEBRIEF - Khanate Roman, “Shabiz Araba”

Born the youngest son to a noble family in the Thevenovgrad Imperial holdings Roman [REDACTED] always had a lot to prove. 500 years ago the [REDACTED] family ran an upstart shipping company based in coastal regions of the Imperial Holdings, acquiring a lucrative contract from The High Trade Council of Confederated Cities, Freeports and Bishoprics of The Thevenovgrad Imperial holdings for a monopoly of shipping routes importing pearls, pelts and perfumes. The [REDACTED] family were an ethnic minority in the imperial holdings but had firmly rooted themselves in the empire's culture and noble hierarchy. Coming from a family which has had to work twice as hard to advance in social standing within the emporesses’ court to earn stewardship of the county of [REDACTED], expectations for Roman were high. While Roman’s three elder siblings became a barrister, a clergyman and a tax collector Roman’s mother decided that military school would be most appropriate for the shy and effeminate Roman.

Roman’s doting mother, “Pinkie” moved across the street from the Grodzisk imperial military academy where they dined together every night at Pinkie’s request. While Roman was no longer forced to wear the dresses his mother would pick out for him as a child, boys in the Imperial Holdings that were tough, aggressive and bloodthirsty made their names in musical theatre - not the army. Difficult years followed Roman at the all boys academy. Tactical ability was lacking, his meagre voice was unrousing and with one leg longer than the other his physical profile was less than ideal. His only discernible skill seemed to be logistics as after a particularly hurtful comment about his weight from a classmate he was courting, Roman would regularly skip meals.

The Imperial Holdings military was a declining institution. The once great army that was feared all across Kurovia were reduced to an overgrown and ineffective mess, the Imperial Holdings were disconnected and inefficient to run after the Oblate. With no chance of challenging the Rhalâin Empire, the job of the Imperial Holdings army was to put down domestic unrest and manage the decline of the holdings’ few frontiers. 

Graduating with honours, Roman was shipped off as far from Thevenovgrad as possible, to the island of Zhoujin. The Thevenovgradian Zhoujin Mission established itself as a major player in the region after the Zhoujin Empire had fractured into warring states post Oblate, with the Thevenovgradian presence growing from a concession port to dominate a northern section of the island. While small Zhoujin imperial remnants fought one another for control, Thevenovgradian influence in the south was disconnected by the Bogd Divine Khanate, inhabiting the Zhoujin’s steppe stretching across the middle of the island.

Roman was initially assigned as the commander of a military attaché to the Bogd army; he found an affinity for the food, the customs and the people of the Khanate. As Thevenogradian influence grew in the Khanate under our noses, Roman was selected to be the imperial holdings puppet - to make the Khanate a pawn in a bid to expand south. Roman was not the Colonial Grandee the Imperial Holdings high command would have wanted but out of his two other rivals, Roman could speak the language and wasn’t nicknamed “hostage killer” in the barracks; so he would do for now in order to keep relations smooth between Imperial Command and the natives.


Roman was installed as the Khan of Khans after the previous Khan had suddenly taken ill after drinking a portion of bad Skija milk. The Imperial Holdings high command agreed with the delegates representing the Tei Chung people's army that the Bogd Divine Khanate belonged to the Republican Tei Chung sphere of influence and at the next meeting between the envoys, a suitable timetable would be decided to grant the divine kingdom “true” independence. However, the imperial holdings high command postponed the follow up meeting indefinitely and so Roman remained Khan of Khans. 


Later, a source would recall that 3 months into ruling Roman had told him that he was: “mystically and scientifically convinced of being possessed not by a demon, but by a spirit from burrowing rodent mythological pre-history”. He subsequently lost all desire to rule as Khan, renamed himself “Shabiz Araba” and fled the Khan’s palace on horseback in the dead of night without a word. In the Bogd Khan constitution, written by the Imperial High Command, a Khan abdicates through declaration. With no way to contact Roman and with the rump Bogd parliament having no authority to remove him, the Thevenovgrad Imperial holdings plan for southern domination had come to a halt.


The situation in Zhoujin is on a knife edge due to Khan Roman being missing. Larger warring states wish to make a move against the Imperial Holdings while they are on the back foot but the invasion of Bogd to execute a Thevenovgradian national from a high ranking imperial family would invoke immediate response. Additionally, marching ground troops into Bogd from the The Thevenovgradian Zhoujin Mission to restore order in the Khanate would directly violate previous arrangements with Republican Tei Chung and their so-called “sphere of influence”. The largest warring states would likely form a united front to counteract the Imperial Holdings and so would also plunge the island into war. In the case that Roman is found dead, all parties in the conflict would make a move on the Khanate immediately and preparations in this event have already been prepared as per the Majiang Cabinet. 


Roman [REDACTED] must be found alive, he is the linchpin of the Imperial Holdings entire foreign policy with respect to Zhoujin affairs. His fate decides that of tens of millions of compatriots. The high command would give us anything and everything to get him back.
