## 🖤 QUESTLINE: **The Thousandfold Masque**

### 🎭 Premise

An invitation arrives by black envelope, delivered by a blind courier. It is sealed with wax, stamped with the symbol of **The Thousandfold Society**—a secretive, elite circle of aristocrats, occultists, artists, and exiles.

They are hosting their **once-a-decade Masquerade**, held in a manor that only appears on certain nights: **Vellum House**, built on the skin of forgotten gods.

The Wand of Orcus is rumored to have surfaced again. Not as a weapon… but as **a work of art**.

---

## ✉️ Act I – The Masked Invitation

### Hook:

- The party is hired (or otherwise drawn) to investigate the Wand’s whereabouts.
    
- A noble collector, academic, or oracle whispers that the Wand of Orcus was stolen from a sealed vault three years ago. The thief? An exiled noble named **Ilinya Varn**, known now only as **The Curator**.
    
- Ilinya vanished from public life—until now. Rumor claims she will be unveiling her “final piece” at the Masque.
    

**That piece is the Wand**—rewoven into something **so beautiful** and **so horrifying**, none who look upon it remain unchanged.

---

## 🎭 Act II – The Masque

**Vellum House** appears only when the stars align. It is:

- A shifting, impossible building filled with paintings that whisper.
    
- Every mirror shows someone else's reflection.
    
- Everyone wears masks, and no one speaks their real name.
    
- The food tastes like memories you forgot you had.
    

The Masque is a full evening of:

- **Investigation**: find clues, follow whispers, navigate surreal rules.
    
- **Roleplay & Disguise**: the players must adopt personas, dance with devils, drink with angels, trade secrets like coin.
    
- **Social Combat**: the final moment is a **masked debate/auction duel**, where Ilinya will only give the Wand to the most _compelling soul_—not the highest bidder.
    

**Options**:

- One player makes a grand speech.
    
- One player offers a truth or memory in trade.
    
- One player dances with Ilinya and tries to steal it mid-performance.
    
- One NPC is secretly possessed by the Wand and speaks in its voice.
    

---

## 🩸 Act III – The Dungeon: **The Gilded Hollow**

When the Masque ends, the manor begins to collapse in on itself. If the party acquires or tries to claim the Wand—willingly or not—it **pulls them into itself**.

They awaken in the **Gilded Hollow**: a surreal, rotting under-realm shaped by Ilinya’s wishes and the Wand’s will.

This dungeon is:

- Shifting, theatrical, and warped by ego and memory.
- Filled with phantoms reenacting scenes from Ilinya’s life.
- Populated by living sculptures, dead dancers, and decaying beauty.

To escape with the Wand, the party must:

1. **Defeat or redeem Ilinya**, whose soul is bound to the Wand.
2. **Confront the Wand itself**, which speaks with voices of the dead and offers each player a “wish” tailored to their wounds.
3. Make a **moral choice**: destroy it, contain it, use it—or pass it on.
