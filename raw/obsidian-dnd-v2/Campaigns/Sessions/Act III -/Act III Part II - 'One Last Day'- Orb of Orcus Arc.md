# Flow:
- Estimated level: 7
- Ask players to investigate which individual they think currently has the Orb. 
- Feena and Hadlar sent Spinx to investigate them (takes a week in game).
- If the wrong person is found, either tell them that wasn't the right target, or send them on a goose chase. During this time, players should progress on the [[Act III Part I - Wand Of Orcus Arc]].
- Oberon is found, solve a puzzle to realise that he hasn't got it. Someone beat him in cards for it. Someone called Del'Reeya.
- Ambit investigates them, until they finish, or are close to finishing the [[Act III Part I - Wand Of Orcus Arc]]. State they have a family estate. 
- Family estate only has a birth certificate (plus loot!). Meet Mivra Del'Reeya. Initially think the party is police, say she's so glad to see them, her husband has been kidnapped and her estate robbed. Says she DID buy the Orb, but the kidnappers stole it. She will claim she is SO innocent. If players investigate further they will find an adoption notice (your son has been taken bc you think hes disabled) AND the randsom note. (What you did to your son is despicable. We have taken your husband and your treasures. Once you find love in your heart for him, we will give both back).
- Investigate time
- Another 'Del'Reeya' estate exists... but it didnt before? Send players to investigate.
- Its a whole ass town made from magic. Have some monsters and a boss fight. (his father maybe?).
- Afterwards the illusion disappears. You have killed his Dad. It is just a young, very disabled boy who can't move. He is clutching the orb with all his might. He lets it go without much convincing. He asks if he will finally have a family now. He dies shortly after. 
- CONGRATULATIONS YOU HAVE WON
- Deus ex note in house with a location of where the rest of the loot went.
