# Tier 1
## Spy Room
This room comes as a default. It can be upgraded. 
Level 1: By giving a first and last name to the person(s) working in the Spy Room, they can work to tract their exact location. 70% success rate. +10% for each person assigned to the Spy Room.
Level 2 (Requires 1 hireling in the Spy Room + 500GP): By keeping your informants' ears to the ground, you begin developing your own spy network. Gain the ability to send hirelings on missions to potentially increase their level, gold and artifacts.
Level 3 (Requires 3 hirelings in the Spy Room+ 2,000GP): By smart investing in the right people, you now have a functioning spy network. This upgrade contains at least 1 secret. More contracts for your team and yourself to drastically increase your power become available. 

## Resting Room
Gives +10 temporary hit points when resting here

### Upgrades:
Holographic view: When you long rest here, gain an inspiration until you long rest. 

## Battle Ring
Room Size: 1 Point
Construction costs: 2,500 GP
Construction Time: 8 days
Those that spend at least an hour here became ready for battle. They have this condition until they next long rest. The first attack each combat is taken with advantage.

### Upgrades:
Running track
Construction cost 3,500GP
Construction cost: 4 days
Those that spend at least an hour on the running track become energised. Until the next time they long rest, add +3 to their initiative rolls.

Battle Master
construction cost

## Catacombs
Size Cost: 1 Room Point
Construction cost: 3,000GP
Construction time: 15 days
Dead bodies may be interred here, and suffer no degradation for the purposes of necromancy spells. When this room is first built, it comes with a level 1 unpaid, unskilled skeletal hireling. 
Every full moon (aprox 1 every 15 days) or a celestial event of equal or more importance, an hour long-ritual may be performed by any level 3 hireling. PC or hireling will learn (or will teach one person) a new magic spell. Magic spells cannot be taught to those unattuned to the weave. This room also contains at least 1 secret.

### Upgrades:
Weave-Attunement Centre:
Construction cost: 5,000GP
construction time 7 days.
Every 14 days, this room can do 1 of the following:
1) Attune a PC to the 'Weave'. They gain 3 cantrips at the DM's choice for their character. They are considered a spell-casting going forward.
2) If magical artifacts / oddities equivalent to 1,000GP are brought here, a level 1 hireling will be magically infused and become a level 2 hireling. 

## Chapel
Size cost: 1 Room Point
Construction: 2,500GP
Construction time: 9 days
Choose a God this chapel will be devoted to. Upon completion, this room is under the benefits of the Hallow spell. Religion checks made here regarding that deity are done so with advantage. Every 2 weeks, generates 3d6 x 10gp from tithes.

## Boutique
Size cost: 1 Room Point
Construction cost: 2,500GP
Construction time: 9 days
When you first build this room, roll on the Traders and Merchants table, and then on the quality table. The quality will always be medium or higher. This merchant is a permeant residence of your structure, however, they will not be aware of the true nature of your base..... This room generates 20gp x 3d4 every 14 days.

## Stables
Size cost: 1 Room Point
Construction cost: 1,500GP
Construction time: 7 days
When you build this room, you automatically hire a level 1 hireling. They are proficient at animal care, but it is not a full-time role, and they are available for other duties. However, firing them means no one can look after your animals, and they will die/escape. Upon purchase, the following animals become available to buy (price includes a 30% discount):
 - Camel (50gp)
 - Gila (400gp)
 - Horse (75gp)
 - Mastiff (25gp)
 More animals will become unlocked as you progress. The stable + hireling cost the party 20gp every 14 days. If more than 6 animals exist in the enclosure, another hireling will be required. This room contains at least 1 secret.
## Garden
Size cost: 1 Room Point
Construction cost: 3,000GP
Construction time: 15 days
Contains a brief assortment of verbs and vegetables. Every 14 days, the garden provides either:
- 5 potions of common quality
- 2/3 potions of uncommon quality
- 1 potion of rare quality.
After the herbs have been harvested, a relevant skill check will need to be passed for a successful brew. This process can be automated by assigning a hireling to the Garden. This room contains at least 1 secret.

## Jails
Size cost: 1 Room Point
Construction cost: 2,500GP
Construction time: 15 days
You have cells that can hold up to 2 x your base's total room points. Prisoner's escape chance are calculated every 14 days, depending on room upgrades, assigned hireling and prisoner's skill with thieves tools. This room will not thwart magical escapes without an upgrade. +1 hireling slot. Hadlar and Feena have the final say if in a prisoner should be held here or not. This room also contains at least 1 secret.

### Upgrades:
Wards (2,500GP): The Jail can now hold magical prisoners safely using abjuration wards. Prisoner's escape chance for magical users are calculated every 14 days, with the same formula as a non-magical prisoner, except with a -5 to all rolls. 
Advanced security (3,500GP): -5 to all rolls to escape. For unskilled / average prisoners, escape becomes impossible. 

## Private Smithy
Size cost: 1 Room Point
Construction cost: 3,000GP
Construction time: 12 days
When you first choose this room, choose a specific tool type. A level 2 hireling who is proficient in these tool types AND smithing will automatically be hired. This private smithy will excel in creating these items. The smithy will also be able to forge any mundane item with a 40% discount. This hireling can EITHER be paid 28gp every 14 days, or 14gp every 14 days - with the caveat they sell their goods in the Ninth Maw - a security risk.

### Upgrades:
Training (5,000GP): For a flat fee, the hireling will give proficiency in their tool type to the party.
Hush money (1,500GP): For an upfront cost, you buy the silence of your hireling forever, meaning you will only need to pay them 14gp every 14 days going forward.

## Bank
Size cost: 1 Room Point
Construction cost: 5,000GP
Construction time: 25 days
This room contains five 30-foot by 40-foot vaults, each with a 15-foot tall ceiling. Each vault is allocated to a PC with a key - required to access their vault. If this room is staffed with a skilled hireling trained in either Insight or Investigation, the room generates an income equal to 2.5% every 30 days. 

### Upgrades:
Increased Staff (additional hireling): income % increased to 5%.
Increased Security (3,000GP): Vaults are significantly harder to break into.

## Experimental Research Lab
Size cost: 1 Room Point
Construction cost: 2,000GP
Construction time: 7 days
Having seen the bizarre and undocumented spells in the Ythidrothus shrine, you know there must be more bizarre spells to learn, occult rituals to perform and unearthed arcana to discover. After building this room, a level 2 hireling proficient in bizarre arcana will be hired. After this, you will need to make a choice:
1) A new spell
2) A new ritual
3) Custom magic item
After 14 days, the procedure will be finished, with a 70% success rate. 

## Upgrades:
Better Mad Science (2,000GP): Add a 20% success rate and a -2 day reduction on research.
Mind enhancer (2,000GP each time): Inject a mind-enhancing serum to upgrade a level 1 hireling to a level 2.  

## Biothaumatology Lab:
Size cost: 1 Room Point
Construction cost: 2,000GP
Construction time: 10 days
The Ambit tentatively offer you their biothaumtological services. Upon completion, a low-level ambit member will become a level 2 hireling. This will unlock a brand new upgrade feature of prosthetics, and imbuements to your body.
