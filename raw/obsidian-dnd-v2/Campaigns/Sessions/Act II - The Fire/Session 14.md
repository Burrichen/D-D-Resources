# Housekeeping
## Fate
- Change to the Fate system - I am changing its name to 'Fate' as it makes better sense conceptually. 
- It now works by adding dice to a pool. Anyone, without asking can take and use a token. 
- I am refining its purpose:
Players can do the following: 
- Add a d6 to attack roll, ability check or a saving throw (but not death saving throws).
- Choose to add a minor beneficial element to a scene of an environment (e.g., to add a piece of cover to a battlefield, to establish a positive connection with a non-hostile NPC, to create a timely distraction, or to find a simple or common item when searching an area).
- Activate a unique power.
- Request a "devil's bargain" from the DM. Where you want to bend the rules or narrative to attempt something special.

DM can do the following: 
- Add a d6 to a damage roll or an ability check.
- Add a minor complication to a scene or an environment (e.g., to destroy a piece of cover or terrain, to establish a negative connection between an NPC and a PC, or to create an instance of bad luck (e.g., the players use a point to create good luck where a guard looks around at the right point for them to stealth past – but the DM uses a point to have a butler just happen to come into the room when unexpected)).
- Activate destiny abilities and powers (monsters and NPCs may have things to activate).
