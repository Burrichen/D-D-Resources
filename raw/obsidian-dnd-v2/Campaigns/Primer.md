The world of Kurovia has a long and detailed history. Hundreds of years ago, the world was much more connected, with only a couple of continents, but a massive magical disaster (now known as the Oblate) ripped through the world, corrupting and warping; forming the islands now present in Kurovia. It also unleashed more archaic races into the world, such as Eladrin and Tieflings. From this fallout, societies fell and rose but none as powerful as the Rhalâin Empire. A coalition between Dwarves and Humans, and eventually Elves. This Empire becamed well known for its biothaumtaurgy and studies of knowledge previously ordained to be sacrilegious and forbidden. But in this new world, nothing is left untouched, nothing 'too far'. Thus, A.R.K was born. A towering monstrosity, birthed in the centre of the Great Ocean, towering, beyond all recognition. So large, it dwarfs most of the islands around it. But our story starts far away from that.

Our story starts on Thevethalas, the largest island in this world, this land is rich and varied with its peoples, cultures and places. It also still remains scarred after the Oblate. Nonetheless, the people here still stand proud. Regardless, your story doesn't start in someplace of importance. It starts in a backwater coastal village known as Irvar. For one reason or another (Yes, I would love to hear why your character has travelled/sailed to this place) you have deemed is nessisary to stop in this small village.
