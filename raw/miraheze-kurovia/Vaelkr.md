# Vaelkr

Umbuso

The Rain-Strider

Vaelkr

**Vaelkr**, commonly known as **the Rain-Strider**, is an [[Umbuso]] associated with the coastline of [[Skgurr]].

Reports describe an immense, emaciated organism walking through coastal waters during periods of heavy rain.

No reliable account describes Vaelkr arriving.

No reliable account describes Vaelkr leaving.

It is simply noticed somewhere along the coast.

**ARCHIVAL NOTICE**

The name *Vaelkr* originates from an old coastal Skgurr dialect.

Early translations rendered the word approximately as:

*one who walks beneath bad weather.*

A second translation was removed from circulation following Incident ██████.

## Overview

Vaelkr is one of the few known Umbuso whose appearances demonstrate a consistent environmental pattern.

It is seen almost exclusively during storms.

Witnesses describe a towering dark organism moving parallel to the shoreline, usually several hundred metres from land.

Its body remains partially submerged even in water known to be considerably deeper than its visible limbs should allow.

Vaelkr appears capable of walking normally regardless of water depth, seabed composition or wave conditions.

It has never been conclusively observed swimming.

It does not cross the sea.

It walks through it.

## Appearance

Vaelkr possesses an extremely tall, narrow body supported by several elongated limbs.

The upper body is skeletal in appearance, with branching structures extending from the head and shoulders.

These structures have been compared to antlers, exposed bone and dead coastal trees.

Two luminous white eyes are visible upon the head.

No mouth has been conclusively identified.

The lower portion of Vaelkr is obscured by long, dark structures hanging from its body.

Whether these structures are fur, tissue, vegetation or something else entirely remains unknown.

When Vaelkr moves, they drag through the sea without visibly responding to currents or waves.

## Behaviour

Vaelkr follows the coastline.

This behaviour appears simple.

It is not.

Historical records spanning several centuries describe the creature travelling in the same general direction during every confirmed appearance.

No account exists of Vaelkr reversing direction.

No account exists of Vaelkr reaching a destination.

No known coastline is long enough to accommodate the distances implied by surviving reports.

Attempts to continuously track the entity have failed.

During observation, Vaelkr may remain visible for minutes or several hours.

Heavy rain frequently obscures the creature.

When observers regain visual contact, it may be kilometres farther along the coast.

Occasionally it is simply gone.

## Storm Association

Vaelkr has never been reliably documented beneath clear skies.

Most sightings occur during:

- prolonged rainfall;
- severe coastal storms;
- unusually low cloud;
- rough seas;
- sudden drops in temperature;
- or periods of dense fog immediately preceding rain.

It remains uncertain whether Vaelkr causes these conditions or merely appears during them.

Weather records from several sightings indicate storms began before the entity was observed.

In two documented cases, however, rainfall continued around Vaelkr's estimated location after weather conditions elsewhere had cleared.

One storm persisted for ██ days.

## The Eyes

Vaelkr possesses two brilliant white eyes.

Their luminosity remains constant regardless of surrounding conditions.

Unlike reflected eyeshine, they appear to generate their own light.

They remain visible through rainfall and fog dense enough to completely obscure the creature's body.

Sailors have occasionally mistaken them for distant navigation lights.

This error has resulted in multiple vessels changing course toward the entity.

**MARITIME DIRECTIVE UMB-002-A**

During severe weather along the Skgurr coastline, paired white lights observed offshore must not be used for navigation.

If the lights remain level while waves obscure the horizon:

**TURN AWAY.**

Survivors of close encounters consistently report that the eyes illuminate nothing around them.

Rain crossing directly in front of the lights remains dark.

## Coastal Folklore

Stories resembling Vaelkr encounters occur throughout coastal [[Skgurr]].

Older fishing communities possess numerous names for a tall figure observed beyond the surf.

Translations include:

- **The Long Fisher**
- **The Man Beyond the Breakers**
- **The Storm Walker**
- **The Empty Mast**
- **He Who Never Beaches**

Some communities traditionally extinguish harbour lights during particularly severe storms.

Today, this is often explained as an old practice intended to conserve fuel.

Older documents suggest otherwise.

*Do not light the harbour when the tall one passes.*

*A light from land tells him there is land.*

## Maritime Encounters

Ships account for several of the closest documented encounters with Vaelkr.

Reports are inconsistent.

Some crews describe passing within several hundred metres of the organism without receiving any apparent reaction.

Others report the creature turning its head toward the vessel.

Three surviving records describe Vaelkr reaching toward a ship.

Only one of those ships returned.

The surviving captain refused to provide a complete account.

The official transcript consists of seventeen pages.

Fourteen remain sealed.

The publicly available portion ends with the statement:

*It wasn't reaching for us.*

## Depth Problem

Vaelkr frequently appears to stand in water which should be too deep for its visible anatomy.

This was initially attributed to inaccurate historical estimates of distance.

Modern observations have disproven that explanation.

During Observation UMB-002-11, Vaelkr was photographed approximately 1.7 kilometres offshore.

Recorded water depth at that position was 196 metres.

Based upon the visible proportions of the organism, its lower limbs should have extended approximately 32 metres beneath the surface.

Vaelkr nevertheless walked normally.

Sonar produced no reliable image of its submerged anatomy.

**OBSERVED HEIGHT ABOVE WATER:** 47 m

**EXPECTED SUBMERGED LENGTH:** 32 m

**RECORDED SEABED DEPTH:** 196 m

**METHOD OF LOCOMOTION:** UNKNOWN

One researcher proposed that Vaelkr's limbs do not terminate where its visible anatomy suggests.

The hypothesis was withdrawn.

The original report is no longer publicly accessible.

## The Coastline

No confirmed Vaelkr sighting has occurred away from the sea.

This has led to speculation that the entity may be physically incapable of leaving the coastline.

There is no evidence supporting this assumption.

Researchers are specifically warned against treating the shore as a protective boundary.

Several historical stories describe enormous footprints discovered above the high-tide line after major storms.

No verified photograph exists.

One plaster cast was reportedly produced in ████.

Its current location is unknown.

## Incident UMB-002-07

On ████████, a coastal observation team tracked Vaelkr for approximately six hours during severe rainfall.

The entity remained between 900 metres and 1.4 kilometres offshore.

**03:17** — UMB-002 stopped moving.

**03:19** — all six observers reported that the entity had turned to face inland.

Video footage was obscured by rain.

**03:26** — one observer reported seeing the eyes above the observation station rather than offshore.

The remaining personnel disputed this.

**03:31** — communications failed.

There was no evidence of structural damage.

Vaelkr was no longer visible.

## Relationship with Axius

Vaelkr produces relatively little measurable interference with [[Axius]] at long range.

At closer distances, effects become increasingly unpredictable.

Reported phenomena include:

- incorrect perception of water depth;
- inability to distinguish sea from sky;
- sounds appearing to originate beneath the observer;
- persistent sensations of walking despite remaining stationary;
- and Axius manifestations orienting toward the sea without instruction.

One practitioner exposed during Incident ████ repeatedly stated:

  
*It thinks the coast is moving.*

No accepted interpretation exists.
