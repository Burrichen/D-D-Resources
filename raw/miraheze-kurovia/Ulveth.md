# Ulveth

[[Umbuso|UMB-000]] › [[Umbuso|Umbuso Archive]] › UMB-033

ARCHIVE UMB-033 // SYMBOL-ASSOCIATED HAZARD

**Ulveth**

Umbuso

The Invitation

**DO NOT REPRODUCE THE UMB-033 MARK ON PHYSICAL OBJECTS.**

The effect does not require deliberate activation.

Objects bearing a sufficiently accurate representation must be treated as active hazard sources.

UMB-033 triggering mark withheld. This archive does not contain a reproduction.

**Ulveth**, commonly called **the Invitation**, is an [[Umbuso]] known exclusively through a rune-like marking associated with an anomalous phenomenon termed **Malign Convergence**.

When the mark is inscribed upon an object, harmful people, creatures and circumstances become increasingly likely to converge upon it.

The marked object itself undergoes no known physical change.

Everything around it does.

## Overview

The UMB-033 mark can be applied to almost any physical object.

Documented examples include:

- jewellery;
- weapons;
- doors;
- vehicles;
- buildings;
- clothing;
- books;
- currency;
- and living organisms.

Once activated, adverse events begin occurring around the marked target at rates substantially exceeding chance.

UMB-033 does not make the marked object dangerous.

It makes dangerous things find it.

## Malign Convergence

Researchers avoid the traditional description that the mark **“draws evil.”**

The phrase is scientifically imprecise.

It is also remarkably difficult to replace.

Known convergence events include:

- predators approaching normally avoided settlements;
- hostile creatures changing established migration routes;
- violent individuals becoming interested in marked possessions;
- thefts;
- fires;
- equipment failures;
- structural accidents;
- disease outbreaks;
- unusually severe weather;
- and chains of statistically improbable misfortune.

No common physical mechanism links these events.

**UMB-033 RESEARCH NOTE**

The rune does not appear to attract a particular substance, organism or energy.

It attracts **outcomes**.

This wording is provisional.

It is also deeply unsatisfactory.

## Escalation

The effect typically begins subtly.

Early incidents are easily dismissed as coincidence.

A marked traveller loses supplies.

A hostile animal approaches camp.

A wheel breaks.

A stranger attempts a robbery.

If the mark remains in place, incidents generally increase in both frequency and severity.

**OBSERVED PROGRESSION**

I — coincidence

II — recurring misfortune

III — hostile convergence

IV — serious casualty risk

V — ██████████████████

Stage V has been removed from general field documentation.

## Selection

UMB-033 appears capable of producing circumstances appropriate to its environment.

A marked ship does not attract the same hazards as a marked house.

A marked person does not experience the same events as a marked weapon.

This has produced significant disagreement regarding whether Ulveth:

- predicts dangerous possibilities;
- influences probability;
- attracts existing hostile agents;
- creates new hostile circumstances;
- or possesses some form of intelligence.

There is currently insufficient evidence to distinguish between these models.

## Human Behaviour

One of the most troubling aspects of UMB-033 involves human subjects.

Individuals with no previous connection to a marked object may become unusually interested in it.

This interest can manifest as:

- curiosity;
- envy;
- theft;
- fixation;
- anger;
- or deliberate violence.

Affected individuals do not report external control.

They generally provide plausible reasons for their actions.

SUBJECT: Why did you enter the property?

DETAINEE: I wanted what was inside.

SUBJECT: You did not know what was inside.

DETAINEE: I know.

SUBJECT: Then why?

DETAINEE: It felt like there should be something worth taking.

Whether this constitutes mental influence or probability manipulation is unknown.

## Incident UMB-033-12

A reproduction of the mark was unknowingly engraved upon a freight container.

The container remained in transit for nine days.

During that period:

**Day 1** — shipping documentation misplaced.

**Day 2** — attempted theft.

**Day 4** — transport vehicle mechanical failure.

**Day 5** — second theft attempt.

**Day 6** — unidentified predator entered storage facility.

**Day 7** — warehouse fire.

**Day 8** — ██████████████████████

**Day 9** — marking identified and destroyed.

No further abnormal incidents were reported.

The contents of the container were ordinary ceramics.

## Containment

UMB-033 is unusually manageable for an Umbuso.

The mark requires a physical representation.

Removing or destroying that representation usually terminates further convergence.

Destroy the mark.

Do not preserve the object for study.

The effect normally diminishes within several hours.

Existing threats do not disappear.

A criminal already travelling toward a marked object will not necessarily turn around.

A fire already burning will continue to burn.

A creature already present remains present.

Containment therefore prevents **additional convergence**; it does not reverse events already initiated.

## Copies

Exact reproduction does not appear necessary.

Hand-drawn versions have activated.

Partial markings usually do not.

The threshold separating active from inactive representations remains poorly defined.

For this reason, archival material avoids displaying even heavily altered versions.

**UMB-033 DOCUMENT CONTROL**

Do not trace the mark.

Do not reconstruct missing sections.

Do not test remembered versions.

Do not reproduce it as decoration.

Do not mark another object in an attempt to redirect an existing convergence event.

The final prohibition was added following Incident UMB-033-18.

## Ulveth

No physical manifestation of Ulveth has ever been confirmed.

Whether a creature corresponding to UMB-033 exists is unknown.

Historical traditions nevertheless describe the rune as belonging to an entity variously translated as:

**The Invitation**

**The Open Hand**

**The Caller of Wolves**

**The Door Left Unlocked**

and:

**That Which Sends What You Deserve**

The last title has led to the widespread belief that Ulveth judges its victims.

Research does not support this.

Innocent and malicious subjects appear equally vulnerable.

## Current Status

UMB-033 is classified:

**ACTIVE — INFORMATION-ASSOCIATED**

All known controlled reproductions have been destroyed.

Unauthorised examples are presumed to remain in circulation.

No complete inventory is possible.

**ENCOUNTER GUIDANCE // UMB-033**

If an object is suspected of bearing the UMB-033 mark:

**DO NOT TRANSPORT IT THROUGH POPULATED AREAS.**

**DO NOT SELL OR TRANSFER OWNERSHIP.**

**DO NOT CREATE A COPY FOR IDENTIFICATION PURPOSES.**

Destroy or completely remove the marking where safe to do so.

Remain alert for threats already drawn to the location.

If unrelated adverse events begin occurring in rapid succession:

ASSUME CONVERGENCE IS ALREADY ACTIVE.
