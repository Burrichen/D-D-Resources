# Axius

**Axius** is the collective scholarly name for the supernatural substances and environmental phenomena created when the [[Reality_Machine|Reality Machine]] was activated. Its activation caused the world-changing event known as the [[Oblate]].

In essence, Axius is thought, dreams and human concepts made tangible. Ideas that once existed only within the mind became capable of assuming physical form and altering the material world.

Although all manifestations of Axius originate from the same event, their forms and properties differ radically between regions.

## Principles of Axius

Axius does not possess a single natural form. Instead, it manifests differently in each region according to a particular concept.

These concepts are not straightforward forces of good or evil. Axius embodies them without applying human morality:

- **Progress** can create prosperity while consuming those who pursue it.
- **Greed** can provide great power while concentrating it among those who already possess wealth.
- **Change** can produce extraordinary life while killing those unable to survive it.
- **Wonder** can inspire awe while remaining dangerous to the unprepared.
- **Hunger** can sustain an ecosystem while forcing every part of it to consume and compete.

Axius may appear as a physical resource, an environmental condition or a change to living matter. Known manifestations include ores, liquids, crystals, atmospheric phenomena and magical contamination.

The term *Axius* is primarily used by scholars and international institutions. Most regions have their own name for the manifestation found within their borders.

## Regional Manifestations

### [[Ordon]]: Progress

In [[Ordon]], Axius takes the form of a malleable ore known as **[[Morrow]]**.

Morrow can be condensed into a fuel more powerful than coal or treated to become highly receptive to magical energy. These properties have made it the foundation of Ordon's railways, airships, machinery, weapons and industrial expansion.

The strongest Ordonian armour is also made from Morrow. Prolonged use can permanently bind the wearer to the armour, creating individuals known as the [[Warforged]].

Morrow can additionally be refined into a [[OrdonDrug1|productivity drug]] that increases concentration, energy and the ability to work for extended periods.

### [[Halingo]]: Greed

In [[Halingo]], Axius manifests as a thick black liquid known as **[[Ichor]]**.

Ichor is a powerful fuel for specialised engines, firearms and war machines, but it has proven unsuitable for ordinary public power or medicine. Whether this is an inherent property of Ichor or the result of how it is used remains unclear.

The [[Diesel_Baron|Diesel Barons]] maintain control of Halingo through ownership of its Ichor wells, railways and weaponry. Industrial pollution has stained large areas of desert black, creating the region known as the [[Black_Sea|Black Sea]].

### [[Skgurr]]: The Unknown

In [[Skgurr]], Axius manifests as **[[Morov]]**, a liquid that resembles water without truly behaving like it.

Morov falls as rain and gathers in rivers, pools and coastlines, but it cannot interact reliably with magic. Attempts to manipulate or study it often produce dangerous and contradictory results.

Long-term exposure changes living creatures, materials and environments. The people of Skgurr must either adapt to Morov, as the [[Steel-backs]] have done, or be transformed by it.

### [[Shinkira]]: Change

In [[Shinkira]], Axius forms as volatile crystals known as **[[Utsuro]]**.

When Utsuro comes into contact with plants or animals, it causes rapid transformation and the development of new abilities. These altered organisms are known as [[NotPokemon]].

Human exposure is far less stable. Most people exposed directly to shattered Utsuro die, while some survivors develop manifestations called [[Stand|Stands]]. A Stand is commonly understood as an expression of its user's unrealised potential.

### [[Bratislor]]: Horror

In [[Bratislor]], Axius exists as a form of magical contamination known as the **[[Haze]]**.

The Haze behaves similarly to radioactive fallout. Contaminated places may remain dangerous for generations, while exposed people and creatures can continue changing after leaving the affected area.

Its effects include mutation, magical instability, altered behaviour and the creation of unnatural creatures. Some manifestations appear to reflect the fears or traumatic memories of those exposed to them.

Bratislor has consequently become both a contaminated country and a place where magical threats from elsewhere are confined.

### [[Hjarthland]]: Wonder

In [[Hjarthland]], Axius is suspended throughout the atmosphere as the **[[Huld]]**.

The Huld is responsible for Hjarthland's permanent winter, appearing through snow, frost, unusual lights and powerful magical storms.

Its effects can be beautiful and deadly in equal measure. The Huld is therefore central not only to survival in Hjarthland, but also to its celebrations, stories and cultural understanding of wonder.

### [[Nareth]]: Hunger

In [[Nareth]], Axius has merged with the region's ecosystem rather than forming a separate resource.

It exists throughout the soil, rivers, swamps, plants and animals. Life grows, consumes, decomposes and returns at an unnatural rate.

Nareth's manifestation represents the hunger of life to survive, spread and consume. Scholars have been unable to isolate it or assign it a universally accepted regional name.

### [[Targoth]]: Unidentified

No confirmed manifestation of Axius has been identified in [[Targoth]].

It may be absent, hidden within [[Mount_Targon|Mount Targon]], inaccessible to conventional study or present in a form that scholars do not recognise as Axius.

Targon's isolation and dangerous terrain have prevented thorough investigation, leaving the region unclassified.
