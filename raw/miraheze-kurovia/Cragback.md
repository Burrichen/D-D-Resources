# Cragback

![](/assets/miraheze-kurovia/Cragback.png "A Cragback")

A Cragback

**Cragbacks** are heavy, rock-backed beasts found in [[Ordon]] and [[Hjarthland]]. In the wild they are territorial and dangerous, but when properly domesticated they are widely used as pack animals.

They are especially valued for hauling supplies through steep, broken or snowy terrain.

## Appearance

Cragbacks are broad, low-bodied animals with thick limbs and dense hides. Their backs naturally grow stony ridges and mineral formations, giving them the appearance of walking hills.

Some individuals also develop bright crystalline growths, especially in older specimens.

## Behaviour

Wild Cragbacks are short-tempered and highly defensive. They charge intruders, especially if startled or cornered.

Domesticated Cragbacks have been trained to be more dependable, but are still known for their temper. They can carry heavy loads across mountains, mines and winter roads with little complaint.

## Use as Pack Animals

Cragbacks are prized in both Ordon and Hjarthland because they can travel where wheeled transport struggles.

Ordonian merchants and miners use them in rocky uplands and industrial routes, while Hjarthland communities rely on them to move goods through snow and uneven ground.

Their broad backs and natural sturdiness make them ideal beasts of burden.

## Rock Growths

The formations on a Cragback's back continue growing throughout its life.

These growths can be:

- Bare stone ridges
- Mineral plates
- Crystal clusters
- Moss-covered crags

Handlers sometimes trim or shape these formations to make it easier to fasten cargo.

## Challenge Rating

Cragbacks have a **Challenge Rating of 1**.

A domesticated Cragback is usually docile, but a wild one is dangerous due to its size, aggression and ability to trample or gore attackers.
