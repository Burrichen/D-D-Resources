# Lingering Injuries

A creature might sustain a lingering injury under the following circumstances:

- When it drops to 0 hit points
- When it fails a saving throw by 5 points or more

The type of injury sustained is determined by the damage type that caused it.

## Acid

| d20 | Injury | Description | Length |
|----|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Blindness** | Your eyes are destroyed; you gain the [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] condition. Magic such as the [[Regenerate|regenerate]] spell can restore your sight. | — |
| 2 | **Partial Blindness** | Your eyes are damaged. You have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and cannot see more than 30 feet ahead of you. | — |
| 3 | **Destroyed Hand** | You lose a hand or arm. You can no longer hold anything with two hands, and you can hold only a single object at a time. | — |
| 4 | **Destroyed Leg** | Your speed on foot is halved, and you must use a crutch or cane to move. You fall prone after using the Dash action. You have disadvantage on Dexterity checks made to balance. | — |
| 5–7 | **Destroyed Inventory** | Your armour and backpack become useless. You lose all but `1d4` of your rations, as well as `1d12` other items. | — |
| 8–10 | **Destroyed Weapon** | Your weapons are destroyed. | — |
| 11–13 | **Horrible Disfigurement** | You suffer acid burns so extensive that the scars cannot be easily concealed. You have disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks and advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks. | — |
| 14–16 | **Blisters** | You have severe blisters and disadvantage on Dexterity checks. The blisters heal if you receive magical healing. Alternatively, someone can tend to them and make a DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check once every 24 hours. After seven successes, the blisters heal. | `1d4` weeks |
| 17–20 | **Minor Disfigurement** | You have acid-burn scars, but they have no adverse effect. | — |

## Bludgeoning or Force

| d20 | Injury | Description | Length |
|----|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Coma** | You fall unconscious and cannot be woken by healing. You awaken after one hour. | 1 hour |
| 2 | **Broken Leg** | Your speed on foot is halved, and you must use a cane or crutch to move. You fall prone after using the Dash action and have disadvantage on Dexterity checks made to balance. If your leg is splinted with a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check, it heals twice as fast. | `1d6` weeks |
| 3 | **Destroyed Hand** | You lose the use of a hand or arm. You can no longer hold anything with two hands, and you can hold only a single object at a time. If your arm is splinted with a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check, it heals twice as fast. | `1d6` weeks |
| 4–6 | **Punctured Lung** | You can take either an action or a bonus action on your turn, but not both. If both lungs are punctured, your hit points drop to 0 and you immediately begin dying. | `1d4` weeks |
| 7–9 | **Tinnitus** | Your ears ring following the blow. You have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing. | `1d10` weeks |
| 10–12 | **Concussion** | You have disadvantage on Intelligence checks and saving throws. The concussion heals if you receive any magical healing. | 2 weeks |
| 13–15 | **Severe Bruising** | You suffer severe bruising over an extensive portion of your body. Whenever you take bludgeoning or force damage, you take an additional `1d8` bludgeoning damage. | `1d4` weeks |
| 16–20 | **Broken Nose** | You have a broken nose. Any magical healing mends it, although it may heal crooked if it is crooked when the healing is applied. | `1d4` days |

## Fire and Lightning

Fire injuries are characterised by their stacking effects. Repeatedly falling to fire damage causes increasingly severe burns.

| d20 | Injury | Description | Length |
|----|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Neuralgia** | Whenever you attempt to take an action in combat, you must make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, you lose the action. | `1d6` weeks |
| 2 | **Fourth-Degree Burns** | You have disadvantage on Strength, Dexterity and Constitution checks and saving throws. If you fail a saving throw against an effect that causes fire damage, you are also [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] until the end of your next turn. | — |
| 3 | **Third-Degree Burns** | You have disadvantage on Strength, Dexterity and Constitution checks, as well as Constitution saving throws. If you fail a saving throw against an effect that causes fire damage, you are also stunned until the end of your next turn. Someone can tend to the burns and make a DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check once each week. After ten successes, the burns heal. If you already have third-degree burns, you instead suffer fourth-degree burns. Taking more than 15 points of fire damage in a single turn also upgrades the injury to fourth-degree burns. | — |
| 4 | **Second-Degree Burns** | You have disadvantage on Strength, Dexterity and Constitution checks. If you already have second-degree burns, you instead suffer third-degree burns. Taking more than 10 points of fire damage in a single turn also upgrades the injury to third-degree burns. | 1 week |
| 5–7 | **Internal Injuries** | You lose a number of Hit Dice equal to `1d4 + half your level` while injured. | `1d4` weeks |
| 8–10 | **Cardiac Injury** | You gain one level of exhaustion that cannot be removed normally. Whenever you fail a saving throw against fear or a fear effect, you gain another level of exhaustion, which can be removed normally. Magic such as the *regenerate* spell can heal the cardiac damage. | `1d4` weeks |
| 11–13 | **Horrible Disfigurement** | You have burn scars so extensive that they cannot be easily concealed. You have disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks and advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks. | 2 weeks |
| 14–16 | **Blisters** | You have severe blisters and disadvantage on Dexterity checks. Once each week, someone can tend to the blisters with a DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check and create a paste. Applying the paste temporarily suppresses the effect, but does not heal it. | `1d4` weeks |
| 17–20 | **First-Degree Burns** | You have superficial but painful burns. Whenever you take fire damage, you take an additional `1d4` fire damage. If you already have first-degree burns, you instead suffer second-degree burns. Taking more than 5 points of fire damage in a single turn also upgrades the injury to second-degree burns. | `1d8` weeks |

## Necrotic

Necrotic injuries tend to have longer-lasting and debilitating effects, further hindering characters without divine intervention.

| d20 | Injury | Description | Length |
|----|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Full Decay** | Your body is fundamentally damaged. Your hit point maximum is reduced by 1 every 24 hours. For every week that passes, a new body part falls off. Holy intervention is required. | 10 weeks |
| 2 | **Incubator** | You become an incubator for a new plague. | `1d6` weeks |
| 3 | **Dark Spirit** | You believe that you can see a demon inside you. It may appear to you. | — |
| 4 | **Necrotic Discharge** | Everything you touch, including allies and objects, takes `1d6` necrotic damage. | `1d4` weeks |
| 5–7 | **Insanity** | Roll on the [[Madness#Long-Term_Madness|Long-Term Madness]] table. | — |
| 8–10 | **Undead Stench** | You smell like rotting flesh. You have disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks. Magical healing of 6th level or higher removes the smell. | `1d4` weeks |
| 11–13 | **Minor Decay** | Your hit point maximum is reduced by 1 every 24 hours while the wound persists. If your hit point maximum drops to 0, you die. The wound heals if you receive magical healing. | — |
| 14–16 | **Inflammation** | Your muscles are irritated and inflamed. You have disadvantage on Strength checks. | 2 weeks |
| 17–20 | **Necrotic Discolouration** | White and grey spots appear on your cheeks. The spots have no adverse effect. Magical healing of 6th level or higher removes them. | — |

## Piercing and Slashing

Piercing and slashing damage are relatively common, so their lingering injuries are generally less severe.

| d20 | Injury | Description | Length |
|----|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Lose an Eye** | You have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight and on ranged attack rolls. Magic such as the *regenerate* spell can restore the lost eye. If you have no eyes remaining after sustaining this injury, you are blinded. | — |
| 2 | **Throat Injury** | You gain one level of exhaustion that cannot be removed normally. You also have disadvantage on Constitution checks. Magic such as the *regenerate* spell can heal the injury. | — |
| 3 | **Groin Injury** | Your speed on foot is halved, and you must use a cane or crutch to move. You cannot take the Dash action and are sterile. Magic such as the *regenerate* spell can heal the injury. | — |
| 4 | **Cardiac Injury** | You gain one level of exhaustion that cannot be removed normally. Whenever you fail a saving throw against fear or a fear effect, you gain another level of exhaustion, which can be removed normally. You frequently find yourself short of breath. | `1d4` weeks |
| 5–7 | **Pierced Stomach** | When you complete a long rest, you must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or gain the [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] condition until you complete another long rest. Magical healing of 6th level or higher, such as [[Heal|heal]] or *regenerate*, heals the pierced stomach. It also heals naturally if you spend ten days doing nothing but resting. | 10 days |
| 8–10 | **Infested Wound** | Dirt, grime or poison enters the wound. You take `1d10` poison damage every day until it is cured. | — |
| 11–14 | **Horrible Scar** | You are disfigured to the extent that the wound cannot be easily concealed. You have disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks and advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks. Magical healing of 6th level or higher removes the scar. | `1d2` weeks |
| 15–20 | **Necrotic Discolouration** | White and grey spots appear on your cheeks. The spots have no adverse effect. Magical healing of 6th level or higher removes them. | — |
