THE WORLD

**Regions**

[[Ordon]]

The industrial heartland of Kurovia, built upon [[Morrow]], steam machinery and an obsession with progress.

[[Halingo]]

A vast desert divided between wealthy cities, impoverished settlements and the rule of the [[Diesel_Barons|Diesel Barons]].

[[Skgurr]]

A storm-covered coastal region where the water-like [[Morov]] makes even familiar concepts alien.

[[Shinkira]]

A rapidly changing country where [[Utsuro]] crystals transform living matter.

[[Bratislor]]

A magically irradiated region contaminated by the [[Haze]] and used to contain dangerous monstrosities.

[[Hjarthland]]

A permanently frozen northern country whose harsh environment is balanced by a deep cultural commitment to hospitality.

[[Nareth]]

A region of vast rivers, swamps and rainforests where life grows with relentless hunger.

[[Targoth]]

An abandoned region dominated by [[Mount_Targon|Mount Targon]], the highest known point in Kurovia.

[[Armada]]

A floating city assembled from captured ships and ruled by [[Wintrow]], the [[Pirate_King|Pirate King]].
