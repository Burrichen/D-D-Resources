## By Rarity

Rarity describes how frequently a creature is encountered in the world. It does not necessarily indicate how dangerous it is.

| Rarity | Definition | Creature category |
|----|----|----|
| **Common** | Frequently encountered within its natural habitat. Usually familiar to local inhabitants. | [[:Category:Common_creatures|Common creatures]] |
| **Uncommon** | Known to local communities but not encountered regularly. | [[:Category:Uncommon_creatures|Uncommon creatures]] |
| **Rare** | Seldom encountered and usually limited to particular environments or circumstances. | [[:Category:Rare_creatures|Rare creatures]] |
| **Very Rare** | Known from only a small number of sightings, territories or surviving populations. | [[:Category:Very_rare_creatures|Very rare creatures]] |
| **Legendary** | Famous creatures whose existence may shape local history, religion or folklore. | [[:Category:Legendary_creatures|Legendary creatures]] |
| **Unique** | A single known individual, species-ending survivor or creature without confirmed equivalents. | [[:Category:Unique_creatures|Unique creatures]] |

A creature may be common in one region while remaining almost unknown elsewhere. Creature pages should use the rarity that best represents their status within their principal habitat.

## By Challenge Rating

Challenge Rating, usually abbreviated as **CR**, represents the approximate threat a creature poses in combat.

| CR range | General threat | Creature category |
|----|----|----|
| **CR 0** | Normally harmless, although it may still possess unusual abilities. | [[:Category:CR_0_creatures|CR 0 creatures]] |
| **CR 1/8–1/2** | A threat to an untrained individual or poorly prepared traveller. | [[:Category:CR_1/8_to_1/2_creatures|CR 1/8–1/2 creatures]] |
| **CR 1–4** | A local threat suitable for lower-level adventurers. | [[:Category:CR_1_to_4_creatures|CR 1–4 creatures]] |
| **CR 5–10** | A serious regional danger capable of threatening settlements or trained forces. | [[:Category:CR_5_to_10_creatures|CR 5–10 creatures]] |
| **CR 11–16** | A major threat requiring experienced adventurers or organised military action. | [[:Category:CR_11_to_16_creatures|CR 11–16 creatures]] |
| **CR 17–20** | An exceptional threat capable of devastating cities or influencing entire regions. | [[:Category:CR_17_to_20_creatures|CR 17–20 creatures]] |
| **CR 21+** | A world-level threat possessing extraordinary or near-mythic power. | [[:Category:CR_21_plus_creatures|CR 21+ creatures]] |
| **Unrated** | A creature without completed combat statistics or one not intended for combat. | [[:Category:Unrated_creatures|Unrated creatures]] |
