**Nhalyss**, commonly referred to as **the Unblinking Hunger**, is an [[Umbuso]] documented throughout almost every known region of the world.

Unlike other Umbuso, UMB-006 possesses no recognised territory, habitat or environmental preference.

And, on at least one confirmed occasion, █████████████████████████.

Whether these manifestations represent the movement of a single organism, multiple individuals, or portions of an entity which does not occupy space conventionally remains unresolved.

Nhalyss is considered the most dangerous Umbuso presently known.

## Overview

Nhalyss is a highly irregular organism consisting of numerous looping, serpentine structures covered in eyes, jaws and pale appendages.

No central body has ever been conclusively identified.

Individual segments intersect, knot and disappear behind one another in ways which make the organism's overall anatomy impossible to reconstruct.

Most visible surfaces possess eyes.

Many also possess mouths.

Some possess both.

The creature's appearance differs substantially between observations.

This was originally attributed to movement.

It is now known that separate observers viewing the same manifestation may perceive different numbers of eyes, mouths and body segments simultaneously.

There is no verified complete description of Nhalyss.

Every witness sees less than the whole.

It is unknown whether a whole exists.

## Appearance

The most consistently reported component of Nhalyss is a series of thick golden-brown or orange structures forming loops, coils and interconnected rings.

Numerous eyes are embedded directly into these structures.

Confirmed iris colours range the whole spectrum, including colours previously unseen.

Large pale jaws emerge between the eyes.

These mouths contain irregular teeth and smaller tendril-like structures.

Individual jaws sometimes appear connected to the larger body.

Others appear suspended inside open loops with no visible supporting anatomy.

Attempts to establish a consistent count have failed.

The highest verified single-observer report contained:

████ eyes.

████ mouths.

████ distinct loops.

The photographic record from the same encounter contains fewer of all three.

## Scale

Nhalyss possesses no reliable recorded size.

Manifestations have ranged from structures small enough to appear inside a building to entities spanning significant portions of the visible horizon.

It is unknown whether Nhalyss changes size.

An alternative hypothesis proposes that observers are seeing the same organism from differing apparent distances.

This explanation creates a separate problem.

During Incident UMB-006-14, observers positioned over 300 kilometres apart simultaneously reported Nhalyss appearing approximately fifty metres away.

Both were correct according to available imaging.

Anywhere is within its known range.

## Simultaneous Manifestations

For much of its study, researchers assumed Nhalyss travelled rapidly between locations.

This assumption is no longer considered viable.

Multiple confirmed manifestations have occurred simultaneously.

The most significant publicly acknowledged case involved five observation sites across four regions.

All five maintained visual contact with UMB-006 during an overlapping eleven-minute period.

Each manifestation appeared different.

All produced identical anomalous responses from monitoring equipment.

When Site Three attempted to physically damage the entity, manifestations at Sites One, Two, Four and Five reacted.

The manifestation at Site Three did not.

## The Eyes

Every known Nhalyss manifestation contains numerous eyes.

They blink independently.

Some never blink.

Their purpose is unknown.

Tracking experiments indicate that different eyes can follow separate individuals simultaneously.

Closing one's own eyes does not reliably prevent the sensation of being watched.

Neither does leaving the creature's direct line of sight.

During controlled observation, personnel behind solid structures have correctly identified the moment individual UMB-006 eyes changed orientation toward them.

No mechanism for this awareness has been determined.

## The Mouths

Nhalyss possesses numerous independent jaws.

The mouths do not appear primarily adapted for feeding.

Observed behaviour includes:

- biting nearby surfaces;
- biting other parts of Nhalyss;
- biting empty space;
- remaining open for several hours;
- moving without producing sound;
- and producing sound without visible movement.

Some have spoken.

The resulting vocalisations are heavily restricted.

Unlike [[Oruvath]], Nhalyss does not appear to mimic identifiable individuals.

Instead, recordings contain speech in multiple languages, frequently overlapping.

Most statements are incoherent.

Several recur.

*I SEE THE DOOR.*

*THIS IS NOT WHERE YOU ARE.*

*THERE ARE MORE OF YOU.*

██████████████████████████████████████████████████

*OPEN.*

The final word has appeared in recordings separated by more than three centuries.

## Recognition Events

Several manifestations appear to begin only after an observer becomes aware of something unusual.

The sequence frequently follows:

A person notices an object.

They dismiss it.

They look again.

Additional anatomical features become visible.

Others nearby then begin seeing the same entity.

This process is called a **Recognition Event**.

It remains unclear whether Nhalyss becomes visible gradually or whether perception itself changes.

The first person to see Nhalyss is rarely the first person it was visible to.

They are only the first person who realised what they were seeing.

## Why Existential?

The classification does not mean Nhalyss is known to be capable of destroying the world.

It means there is presently no demonstrated mechanism which would prevent it from affecting the entire world.

No known territory limits it.

No environmental condition prevents manifestation.

No reliable defence exists.

No confirmed method of injury exists.

No evidence suggests manifestations must occur sequentially.

And there is no verified upper limit to how many can occur simultaneously.

The theoretical worst-case scenario therefore does not involve Nhalyss travelling from region to region.

It involves Nhalyss appearing in all of them.

## Historical Records

Possible descriptions of Nhalyss exist throughout unrelated cultures.

This was not recognised until modern comparative archival work.

Recurring motifs include:

- circles made of eyes;
- mouths appearing in the sky;
- many-coloured watchers;
- rings which consume themselves;
- creatures described as being “everywhere behind the world”;
- and warnings against drawing certain arrangements of eyes.

No single ancient name dominates.

## The Hundred Gods

Nhalyss presents a significant problem for the historical concept of the [[Umbuso|Hundred Gods]].

Several traditions appear to treat it differently from other Umbuso.

Rather than describing one god among many, some texts imply that Nhalyss existed **outside** the traditional count.

One damaged Shinkiran record lists several entities believed to correspond to known Umbuso.

At the bottom of the text, in a different hand, is written:

*Do not count the one made of eyes.*

*It counts us.*

Whether this refers to Nhalyss is disputed.

## Relationship with Axius

Nhalyss does not consistently suppress [[Axius]].

Instead, Axius phenomena become increasingly difficult to localise during manifestations.

Users have reported:

- effects occurring from the wrong direction;
- abilities manifesting behind the caster;
- sensing several identical targets;
- seeing through perspectives which are not their own;
- temporary inability to distinguish nearby minds;
- and awareness of locations they have never visited.

During UMB-006-18, one practitioner correctly described a room located on another continent.

UMB-006 was simultaneously present outside both locations.

The practitioner had never travelled there.

When asked how they knew what the room looked like, they replied:

*I used the wrong eye.*

The practitioner permanently lost vision approximately three hours later.

No physical damage to either eye was identified.

## Attempts at Destruction

No attack against Nhalyss has produced a confirmed injury.

Conventional weapons may damage visible tissue.

The effect is temporary.

Destroyed eyes have reappeared.

Severed segments have disappeared.

Burned tissue has been observed intact in separate manifestations.

Attempts involving Axius have produced inconsistent outcomes.

One operation apparently destroyed an entire manifestation.

Seven minutes later, another appeared approximately ██████ kilometres away.

It contained an additional mouth.

The operation was not repeated.
