# Snus

![](/assets/miraheze-kurovia/Snus.png "A Snus")

A Snus

**Snus**' are burrowing predators native to [[Halingo]]. They disguise themselves as desert flowers, remaining motionless until prey comes close enough to seize.

When a target approaches, the Snus erupts from beneath the sand, drags the victim down and feeds below the surface.

## Appearance

A Snus has a low, muscular body, powerful digging claws and a lure resembling a pale desert flower. When buried, only this false bloom remains visible above the sand.

## Behaviour

Snus are patient ambush hunters. They bury themselves beneath loose ground and wait for animals, travellers or scavengers to approach the lure.

They attack suddenly, using their weight and claws to pull prey off balance and beneath the sand.

## Hunting

The false flower is used as bait. Some creatures approach out of curiosity, while desperate travellers may mistake it for a real sign of water or plant life.

Experienced desert guides warn against any bloom found growing alone in open sand.

## Challenge Rating

Snus' have a **Challenge Rating of 2**.

They are dangerous because of their ambush tactics rather than speed or endurance. A prepared traveller can avoid them, but the unwary may be killed before they understand the threat.
