**Saqramallki** (pronounced **SAH-krah-MAHL-kee**) is an [[Umbuso]] exclusive to [[Nareth]].

It resembles a colossal rotted tree with root-limbs, a warped humanoid trunk, and branches from which corpse-like pods hang in clusters.

Although often mistaken for part of the forest, it is animate, territorial, and highly dangerous when disturbed.

## Overview

Saqramallki inhabits deep rainforest, swamp, and drowned ruins far from major settlements.

It remains still for long periods, blending into the surrounding jungle, then moves suddenly when its territory is entered.

Saqramallki is not a tree.

It is a predator that survives by being mistaken for one.

## Behaviour

UMB-034 appears to respond to intrusion, noise, fire, and cutting activity.

When active, it uses root-like limbs to drag itself forward and seize nearby organisms.

Victims are believed to be hung from its branches or partially incorporated into the swollen masses around its body.

Whether the suspended forms are food, trophies, or growth structures remains unknown.

## Territory

Saqramallki is known only from [[Nareth]].

Confirmed sites are marked by:

- black standing water;
- heavy root spread over old stone;
- unusual silence among animals;
- and suspended remains high in the canopy.

These signs make it more avoidable than some Umbuso, though few survive long enough to recognise the pattern twice.

## Current Status

UMB-034 is classified:

**ACTIVE — NARETH INTERIOR**

Its precise location is unknown.

This should not be interpreted as absence.

It indicates only that no expedition is currently maintaining visual contact.
