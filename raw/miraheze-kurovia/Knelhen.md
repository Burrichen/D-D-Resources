# Knelhen

![](/assets/miraheze-kurovia/Knelhen.png "A Knelhen")

A Knelhen

**Knelhens** are very rare guardian-creatures spotted only in [[Armada]], but they exist in the ocean world-wide. They are calm and surprisingly friendly toward the people of the floating city, but become ferociously violent in it's territory.

## Appearance

Knelhens are large, heavy-bodied creatures with powerful limbs, fin-like growths and a shell-like back covered in coral, barnacles and other sea growths.

They often appear decorated by the sea itself, as though part beast and part drifting wreck.

## Behaviour

Knelhens are intelligent, social and unusually gentle around those they recognise as part of Armada.

They may allow trusted people to approach, follow workers through the docks or sleep beneath moored vessels.

When threatened, however, a Knelhen becomes immensely dangerous. They defend themselves and others with sudden speed and overwhelming force.

## Habitat

Knelhens are found around the ocean, but only have been spotted in [[Armada]].

## Danger

A peaceful Knelhen is approachable. An angered Knelhen is a disaster. Stories have been told of them tearing through whole boarding parties, smashing weak hulls. Conversely, rare stories tell of them defendng wounded sailors against larger prey.

## Challenge Rating

Keelwardens have a **Challenge Rating of 8**.

They are dangerous because of their strength, resilience and protective instincts. Most do not attack without cause, but once provoked they are capable of killing heavily armed opponents.

## Adventure Uses

A Keelwarden might:

- Guard a hidden area of Armada
- Adopt an unusual interest in a party member
- Defend a ship carrying important cargo
- Attack anyone disturbing a particular wreck
- Be mistaken for a monster when it is actually protecting someone
