# Oblate Archeologist

## Background Proficiencies

| Category | Proficiencies |
|----|----|
| Skill proficiencies | Perception, Survival |
| Tool proficiencies | [Cartographer's tools](https://5e.tools/items.html#cartographer's%20tools_phb) or [Navigator's tools](https://5e.tools/items.html#navigator's%20tools_phb) |
| Languages | Common and one language of your choice |

The **Oblate Archeologist** is a custom character background available in the Kurovia world setting.

Before becoming an adventurer, you spent much of your early life crawling through dust-filled crypts, abandoned settlements and forgotten ruins in search of relics. Most of your discoveries were of questionable value, but a fortunate few provided enough coin for you to purchase proper adventuring equipment.

One item from those years remains especially important to you.

## Equipment

You begin with the following equipment:

- A [wooden case](https://5e.tools/items.html#map%20or%20scroll%20case_phb) containing a rough map of the [[Oblate]] explosion
- A [bullseye lantern](https://5e.tools/items.html#bullseye%20lantern_phb)
- A [miner's pick](https://5e.tools/items.html#miner's%20pick_phb)
- A set of [traveller's clothes](https://5e.tools/items.html#traveler's%20clothes_phb)
- A [shovel](https://5e.tools/items.html#shovel_phb)
- A [two-person tent](https://5e.tools/items.html#two-person%20tent_phb)
- A vial of acid
- Your signature item

## Feature: Wild Magic Attunement

Your prolonged exposure to the ruins left by the [[Oblate]] has made you unusually sensitive to its lingering influence.

Whenever Wild Magic is cast within one mile of you, you sense its general location.

You can cast *detect magic* at will. When cast in this way, the spell only reveals whether an object, creature or person has been affected by the Oblate. It does not otherwise detect magical auras or reveal their schools of magic.
