# Lanthart

![](/assets/miraheze-kurovia/Lanthart.png "A Lanthart")

A Lanthart

**Lantharts** are small deer-like creatures found only in the rainforests and wetlands of [[Nareth]]. They are recognised by the warm light carried at the end of their tails.

Lanthart are naturally friendly and often approach travellers who behave calmly. They rarely remain close to loud, violent or heavily armed groups.

## Appearance

Lantharts have light bodies, branching wooden antlers and patches of green growth resembling moss or leaves.

The orb at the end of the tail produces a soft yellow light. Its brightness increases when the creature is excited, frightened or attempting to attract the attention of another Lanthart.

## Behaviour

Lantharts live in small herds and use their tail-lights to remain together within dense vegetation.

They are curious, playful and unusually trusting. A Lanthart may follow travellers for several hours, particularly when offered fruit.

When threatened, nearby Lantharts rapidly flash their lights to warn the rest of the herd before fleeing into the undergrowth.

## Habitat

Lantharts are found near rivers, flooded forests and rainforest clearings throughout [[Nareth]].

Their light attracts insects, which in turn draw birds and other small animals. A resting herd often becomes the centre of a small temporary ecosystem.

They do not survive well outside Nareth. Lantharts removed from the region gradually lose their light, appetite and energy.

## Relationship with People

Many Narethian communities consider Lantharts signs of safe passage.

Travellers sometimes follow their lights through dense forests, as the creatures instinctively avoid unstable ground, deep water and the territories of large predators. Locals deeply disapprove of this process.

Harming a Lanthart is considered cruel in most settlements. Capturing one for sale is forbidden by several local communities.

## Challenge Rating

Lantharts have a **Challenge Rating of 1/8**.

They are not aggressive and normally flee from combat. Their speed, small size and warning flashes make them difficult to approach unnoticed.

## Adventure Uses

A Lanthart might:

- Guide travellers through a dangerous swamp
- Lead the party towards an injured creature
- Refuse to enter an apparently safe area
- Be targeted by poachers
- Become separated from its herd
- Reveal something hidden when its tail-light grows brighter
