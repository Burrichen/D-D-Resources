# Tathis

[[Umbuso|UMB-000]] › [[Umbuso|Umbuso Archive]] › UMB-076

ARCHIVE UMB-076 // COGNITOHAZARD // VISUAL MATERIAL RESTRICTED

**Tathis**

Umbuso

The Following Sign

**DO NOT REPRODUCE THE TRIGGERING RUNE OUTSIDE AUTHORISED MATERIAL.**

UMB-076 is associated with a visual cognitohazard.

Confirmed exposure occurs upon recognising the rune.

Post-exposure subjects report persistent stalking phenomena followed by fatal attack.

**Tathis**, commonly called **the Following Sign**, is an [[Umbuso]] believed to manifest through a specific rune.

Individuals who view the rune become marked.

Following exposure, the subject is stalked by UMB-076 over a variable period before being killed.

The creature itself is rarely observed clearly.

Most records describe only partial glimpses, silhouettes, movement in peripheral vision, or signs of approach shortly before the attack.

## Overview

UMB-076 is unusual among the Umbuso in that its primary danger does not arise from territorial presence, direct predation, or large-scale destruction.

Instead, the hazard is initiated by **visual recognition** of a specific rune.

Once the rune is seen and consciously processed, the subject is considered exposed.

The rune is not a symbol of UMB-076.

The rune **is** the encounter.

Available evidence suggests that Tathis does not attack immediately.

It follows.

It waits.

Then it kills.

## The Rune

The triggering image is treated as restricted visual material.

Surviving descriptions characterise it as an irregular pale mark with soft edges and an asymmetrical outline.

Minor variations in reproduction appear possible without preventing activation.

Because of this, exact duplication is not considered necessary for the hazard to persist.

**ARCHIVAL NOTE**

Do not assume that an altered copy is safe.

Do not circulate traced versions.

Do not test partial reproductions without approval.

## Post-Exposure Progression

Known cases follow a broadly similar pattern:

- initial exposure to the rune;
- a symptom-free interval;
- recurring sensations of being watched;
- sightings of a distant, malformed figure or movement;
- progressive increase in proximity;
- fatal physical encounter.

The duration of the stalking period varies considerably.

Some subjects survive for days.

Others survive for several weeks.

No confirmed subject has permanently escaped once exposure has occurred.

## Manifestation

Descriptions of Tathis are inconsistent, but several recurring traits appear in surviving testimony:

- an unnaturally thin or misshapen humanoid outline;
- jerking or unnatural movement;
- damp, distorted or broken-limbed motion;
- silence during visual approach;
- and sudden extreme violence at close range.

Most victims die before providing a full description.

## Lethality

Tathis typically kills exposed subjects through direct physical attack.

Recovered bodies demonstrate severe trauma.

The exact method varies.

This has led some scholars to argue that the entity's form may shift between encounters, or that only portions of the attack are consistently perceptible.

UMB-076 is rated **L2** not because every encounter is instantaneously fatal, but because successful survival following a full stalking cycle is extremely rare.

## Containment

Despite its frightening reputation, Tathis receives a comparatively high containment score.

The reason is straightforward:

Avoiding the rune prevents the encounter.

Controlling the rune limits the threat.

Archive policy therefore focuses on:

- suppressing unauthorised copies;
- restricting visual circulation;
- isolating contaminated documents;
- and monitoring suspected exposure events.

No confirmed method exists for saving an already marked individual.

Containment applies primarily to **information control**, not neutralisation of the entity itself.

## Distribution

UMB-076 has no fixed territory.

Cases have been recorded in multiple unrelated regions.

However, every confirmed encounter begins with prior rune exposure.

For this reason, Tathis is not considered a roaming regional Umbuso in the same sense as [[Vaelkr]] or [[Nhalyss]].

Its range is defined by the movement of the symbol.

## Knowledge

UMB-076 is comparatively well documented by Umbuso standards.

Its activation condition is clear.

Its general progression is clear.

Its likely outcome is clear.

What remains unknown is the true nature of the entity itself:

- whether Tathis physically travels;
- whether it manifests near the victim;
- whether the rune summons it;
- or whether the rune simply allows it to perceive the observer.

## Current Status

UMB-076 is classified:

**ACTIVE — INFORMATION-ASSOCIATED**

No permanent physical habitat is known.

The triggering rune remains in restricted archival storage.

Additional copies are presumed to exist outside official control.
