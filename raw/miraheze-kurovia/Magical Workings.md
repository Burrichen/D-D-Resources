# Magical Workings

A **Magical Working** is a permanent enchantment bound to a place rather than a portable magic item. Examples include ever-burning lamps, a self-maintaining house, enchanted gates, and powerful wards. These rules adapt the five-tier Working system for *D&D 2024* and the [[Base_Building|Base Building]] rules.

A Working normally persists until its magical anchors are deliberately destroyed, suppressed, or eroded by age. It requires an existing site or structure; constructing a magical wall does not pay for the wall itself.

## Quick Rules

| Tier | Difficulty points per effect | Saving throw DC | Typical scale |
|----|----|----|----|
| Trivial | 1–4 | — | Household comforts and simple magic |
| Minor | 4–8 | 13 | Useful enchantments, limited guardians, and traps |
| Major | 8–16 | 15 | Strong defences and powerful conveniences |
| Great | 16–32 | 17 | Exceptional spatial and magical alterations |
| Supreme | 33–64 | 19\* | Legendary transformations requiring a unique ritual |

*\*A Supreme ritual may set a different save DC. The DM can adjust an individual effect's point cost within its tier if its scope warrants it.*

### Area and Coverage

| Area affected | Difficulty multiplier | Meaning for a Bastion |
|----|----|----|
| Room | ×1 | One Bastion facility, whether Cramped, Roomy, or Vast |
| Building | ×4 | One entire connected building |
| Village / Estate | ×16 | Multiple buildings and their immediate grounds |
| City | ×64 | A town-sized or city-sized settlement |
| Region | ×256 | A broad surrounding territory |

**Increasing area expands coverage only.** It does not increase how much material is created, how many guardians are summoned, how many traps are installed, or how much damage a trap deals. For those benefits, design additional effects or separate Workings.

### Calculate the Difficulty

1.  Choose one or more thematically related effects from the tables below. Each effect has a difficulty-point (DP) value.
2.  For a single effect, use its listed DP. For combined effects, take the **highest DP, plus half the sum of all other DPs (round up)**. Unrelated effects must be separate Workings.
3.  Multiply the resulting number by the **Area multiplier**. This is the **Final Difficulty**, used for construction and design requirements.

**Example:** An enchanted kitchen combines Plumbing and Ventilation (4 DP), Food Preservation (2 DP), and Enchanted Cooking (1 DP). Its base difficulty is 4 + half of (2 + 1), rounded up = **6 DP**. Applied to one room, the Final Difficulty is **6**. Applied to an entire building, it is 6 × 4 = **24**.

### Architect and Assistance

The owner need not be a spellcaster. The Working must be designed by a character or hired NPC proficient in **Arcana**; the DM may allow **Religion** or **Nature** for an appropriate tradition. A character's **Design Capacity** is:

  
`Character level × Proficiency Bonus × 2`

- If the architect's capacity equals or exceeds the Final Difficulty, they can design the Working alone.
- If the architect's capacity is at least half the Final Difficulty, qualified assistants may contribute their own Design Capacity until the group reaches the full requirement.
- If the architect's capacity is below half the Final Difficulty, a more accomplished lead architect is needed.
- **Supreme Workings** always require special discoveries, materials, and a campaign-scale ritual in addition to ordinary expertise. The DM may let those exceptional preparations replace part of the normal resource or design requirements.

### Price and Construction

| Requirement | Rule |
|----|----|
| Magical construction cost | **100 gp × Final Difficulty** |
| Construction time | **4 + (Final Difficulty ÷ 5, rounded up) Bastion Turns** |
| Rush construction | Double the gp cost to halve the number of Bastion Turns (round up) |
| One Bastion Turn | 7 in-game days |

The cost covers only the enchantment, not the underlying building or machinery. Work can usually continue during adventures if the architect and any needed assistants can return to supervise the site when required.

For the enchanted kitchen above, the **Room-sized** Working costs **600 gp** and takes **6 Bastion Turns**; the **Building-sized** version costs **2,400 gp** and takes **9 Bastion Turns**.

### General Limits

- Workings are fixed to the area and cannot be carried away as magic items.
- Workings cannot provide Bastion Orders, Special Facility slots, or unique Special Facility benefits, though they may improve the environment where those facilities operate.
- Identical numerical effects do not stack within overlapping areas.
- A guardian is not a Bastion Defender for abstract events. A fixed trap does not create extra installations merely because it covers a larger area.
- When a Working calls for a saving throw, use the tier's DC unless the effect specifies otherwise. Wisdom saves replace the original system's mental saves; most damaging traps use Dexterity saves for half damage.

## Working Effects

Each table gives practical 2024 examples. Point costs for Trivial effects and the Minor guardian, traps, and material output are fixed for this homebrew; the DM can tailor other effects within the tier's range to suit the design.

### Trivial Workings

| Effect | DP | Result |
|----|----|----|
| Vermin Ward | 1 | Ordinary insects, rats, and similar mundane pests cannot infest the affected area; monsters are unaffected. |
| Magical Illumination | 2 | Permanent lights or clearly artificial illusions illuminate or decorate the area, without defeating magical Darkness. |
| Magical Sanitation | 2 | Dust, dirt, and ordinary nonliving refuse are removed; equipment and valuable objects are not destroyed. |
| Temperature Regulation | 3 | Maintains comfortable temperatures under normal weather conditions, without protection from extreme environmental hazards. |
| Food Preservation | 2 | Food stored within the area does not spoil naturally; it is not cleansed of existing poison or contamination. |
| Enchanted Cooking | 1 | Designated ovens, hearths, or stoves produce steady cooking heat without fuel. |
| Plumbing and Ventilation | 4 | Existing water and air supplies circulate through installed pipes and vents; no water or breathable air is created. |

### Minor Workings

| Effect | DP | Result |
|----|----|----|
| Magical Communication | 5 | Two fixed points within the area allow two-way speech; they cannot contact locations outside the Working. |
| Convincing Illusions | 6 | Creates a believable programmed scene; touching it reveals the illusion, while a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check may expose it sooner. |
| Lesser Guardian | 8 | Creates **one CR 1/4** guardian that can patrol, follow simple commands, and fight within the Working. If destroyed, it reforms after one Bastion Turn if its anchor survives. |
| Material Generation | 7 | One fixed source creates **20 gallons of water per hour** or **1 cubic foot of ordinary inedible solid material per day**. Choose the material when built. Output does not grow with area. |
| Restorative Aura | 7 | Once per Long Rest, a creature finishing a Short Rest here regains 2 extra Hit Points per Hit Point Die it spends, to a maximum bonus of twice its Proficiency Bonus. |
| Lesser Magical Defences | 6 or 8 | One fixed trap deals **1d6 damage (6 DP)** or **2d6 (8 DP)**, with a DC 13 Dexterity save for half damage. It resets after 1 minute. |
| Self-Maintenance | 6 | Repairs wear and minor structural damage over time; it cannot restore missing major sections or rebuild a destroyed structure. |
| Simple Animation | 6 | Operates one installed device, such as a lift, pump, or gate, using basic predefined controls rather than intelligent judgement. |
| Environmental Shelter | 7 | Keeps the area habitable despite extreme natural heat or cold, but does not negate direct elemental attacks or the need for air. |
| Selective Ward | 8 | One clearly identifiable category of creature must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom save to enter willingly. On a failure it cannot enter for 24 hours. |

### Major Workings

| Effect | Suggested DP | Result |
|----|----|----|
| Food Production | 10 | Creates up to ten nourishing meals each day. Food remains real and useful after leaving the area. |
| Greater Guardian | 12 | Creates one guardian around **CR 2** with a modest special feature, bound to the Working. |
| Near-Perfect Illusions | 12 | Creates realistic illusions that withstand casual physical examination; close interaction permits a DC 15 Wisdom save to perceive the deception. |
| Environmental Adaptation | 14 | Makes an otherwise lethal natural environment habitable, such as an underwater chamber or a structure in deadly heat. It does not ward against deliberate attacks. |
| Disease Protection | 10 | Occupants are protected against contracting diseases while in the area; the Working does not automatically cure an existing disease. |
| Subtle Influence | 12 | On a failed DC 15 Wisdom save, a creature follows a simple nonhazardous suggestion while affected by the area. |
| Greater Magical Defences | 12 or 16 | One fixed defence deals **4d6 (12 DP)** or **5d6 (16 DP)** damage, DC 15 Dexterity save for half. |
| Intelligent Animation | 14 | An installed mechanism carries out a complex purpose with limited independent decisions, such as operating a defensive gate system. |
| Sustaining Enchantment | 12 | Up to ten designated occupants need no food or drink while remaining within the area; this produces no portable supplies. |
| Local Teleportation | 14 | Transports creatures or objects between two designated points inside the area, according to rules fixed when the Working is created. |
| Greater Ward | 16 | Bars several defined intruder categories using DC 15 Wisdom saves, or bars one category until the enchantment is suppressed. |

### Great Workings

| Effect | Suggested DP | Result |
|----|----|----|
| Powerful Guardian | 24 | Creates one guardian around **CR 6** with several appropriate magical abilities, tied to the area. |
| Valuable Material Creation | 24 | Produces a modest, DM-defined amount of material of commercial value. It does not grant Trade or Craft Orders. |
| Folded Space | 24 | Expands or contracts internal dimensions by up to a factor of 20 without changing the structure's exterior footprint. |
| Compelling Influence | 24 | On a failed DC 17 Wisdom save, imposes a complex course of action, potentially involving substantial risk. |
| Deadly Magical Defences | 24 or 32 | A fixed defence deals **8d6 (24 DP)** or **10d6 (32 DP)** damage, DC 17 Dexterity save for half. A more absolute lethal curse requires DM approval. |
| Absolute Exclusion | 32 | Only specifically permitted creatures may enter; other creatures must suppress or break the Working to cross its boundary. |

### Supreme Workings

**Supreme Workings cannot be purchased through gold and downtime alone.** Each requires a unique ritual, rare materials, and a significant quest or comparable campaign achievement. Their DP values provide a baseline for an extraordinary project.

| Effect | Suggested DP | Result |
|----|----|----|
| Temporal Alteration | 48 | Changes the passage of time within the area at a rate established by the ritual. |
| Absolute Domination | 48 | Imposes overwhelming, potentially dangerous mental control over creatures in the area. The ritual sets any saving throw or exception. |
| Guardian Legion | 48 | Creates a fixed contingent of lesser guardians, such as up to twenty CR 1/4 defenders, to protect the Working. |
| Flying Bastion | 64 | Enables a large enchanted structure or settlement to fly and manoeuvre under conditions defined by the ritual. |
| Awakened Structure | 64 | Gives the structure an artificial mind capable of sensing, monitoring, and directing its installed systems. |

## Magical Defences and Bastion Attacks

Minor guardians, traps, and wards can participate in a **played-out combat encounter** using their normal creature statistics, damage, and saving throws. They are not added to the Bastion Defender roster and do not grant any Bastion Order.

For an **abstract Bastion Attack** using the 2024 rules, an operational guardian, trap, or ward that meaningfully protects the entrances or perimeter grants **Magical Protection**: choose one attack die before rolling, roll that die twice, and use the higher result. This applies at most once per Attack, no matter how many magical defences are present. Defensive Walls and Armory benefits work normally alongside it.

If an attack is played out tactically, do not also apply the abstract Attack roll to inflict losses from the same assault.

## Damage, Suppression, and Decay

Workings are usually durable, but deliberate sabotage, damage to important anchors, or long-term magical deterioration can disable or corrupt them. The DM determines how the Working is suppressed or repaired and whether a failed enchantment causes dangerous side effects.
