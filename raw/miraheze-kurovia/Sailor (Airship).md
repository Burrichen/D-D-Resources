**Airship Sailor** is a custom character background available in the Kurovia campaign.

Before becoming an adventurer, you served aboard one of Kurovia's airships. You may have worked on an Ordonian freight vessel, military patrol craft, passenger liner, exploration ship or independent airship.

Life above the clouds taught you how to work as part of a crew, keep your balance in dangerous conditions and remain alert when open sky lay beneath your feet.

## Equipment

You begin with the following equipment:

- A belaying pin, treated as a [club](https://5e.tools/items.html#club_phb)
- [50 feet of silk rope](https://5e.tools/items.html#silk%20rope%20(50%20feet)_phb)
- A set of [common clothes](https://5e.tools/items.html#common%20clothes_phb)
- A pouch [pouch](https://5e.tools/items.html#pouch_phb) containing 10 gp, or the equivalent value in local currency

## Feature: Airship Passage

When at a major port, you can secure free passage aboard an airship for yourself and your adventuring companions.

The vessel may be an airship on which you previously served, one commanded by a former crewmate, or another craft whose crew owes you a favour.

Because you are relying upon personal connections rather than purchasing ordinary passage, you cannot guarantee that the vessel's route or schedule will perfectly suit your needs. The Dungeon Master determines when an appropriate airship is available, where it is travelling and how long the journey takes.

In exchange for passage, you and your companions are expected to assist the crew during the voyage.

This feature does not guarantee access to military vessels, restricted airspace or destinations considered exceptionally dangerous.

| d8  | Role                 |
|-----|----------------------|
| 1   | Deckhand             |
| 2   | Navigator            |
| 3   | Engineer's assistant |
| 4   | Lookout              |
| 5   | Cargo handler        |
| 6   | Gunner               |
| 7   | Ship's cook          |
| 8   | Cabin attendant      |

Your shipboard role does not provide additional proficiencies.
