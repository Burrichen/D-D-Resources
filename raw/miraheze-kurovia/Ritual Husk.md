# Ritual Husk

![](/assets/miraheze-kurovia/Ritual_Husk.png "A Ritual Husk")

A Ritual Husk

**Ritual Husks** are very rare arcane horrors formed when a ritual is interrupted, abandoned or catastrophically miscast.

They are not a species in the ordinary sense. Each Ritual Husk is the remains of a magical working that never finished becoming what it was meant to be. A miscast ritual is like a stench to them, drawn to it

## Appearance

A Ritual Husk appears as a half-formed creature of smoke, exposed magical structure and unstable flesh. Parts of its body seem complete, while others trail away into drifting vapour or collapse into empty gaps.

Its form often looks as though it is still trying to become something else.

## Origin

A ritual miscast, a broken seal, an interrupted resurrection. Powerful magical rituals

## Behaviour

A Ritual Husk behaves like a purpose that has outlived its instructions.

Some wander aimlessly. Others remain bound to the site of their creation. Many attack creatures nearby, especially spellcasters, as though trying to complete themselves through stolen magic or life.

## Danger

Ritual Husks are dangerous not only because they are physically lethal, but because they disrupt magic around them.

Their presence may cause:

- Spells to misfire
- Wards to weaken
- Ritual circles to reactivate
- Hallucinations
- Sudden bursts of arcane energy
- Magical items to behave unpredictably

A Ritual Husk should never be treated as a normal beast or monster. Destroying one often requires understanding the ritual that created it. There is only one way to 'kill' a ritual husk - by completing the failed ritual. Otherwise, one can displace it, or make it not worth their time.

## Habitat and Range

Ritual Husks can appear anywhere in [[Kurovia]] where significant magic has gone wrong.

Because they are created rather than bred, they are considered a worldwide phenomenon rather than a regional creature.

## Challenge Rating

Ritual Husks have a **Challenge Rating of 9**.

They are powerful, unpredictable and especially dangerous to parties relying heavily on magic.

## Adventure Uses

A Ritual Husk might:

- Haunt the site of a failed ritual
- Be the true cause of a noble family's curse
- Hunt a bloodline linked to its creation
- Guard a place no longer remembers why it was sealed
- Be mistaken for a demon, ghost or summoned creature
