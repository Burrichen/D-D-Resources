# Veyrath

[[Umbuso|UMB-000]] › [[Umbuso|Umbuso Archive]] › UMB-004

ARCHIVE UMB-004 // VISUAL RECORD RESTRICTED

**Veyrath**

Umbuso

The Black Saint

![](/assets/miraheze-kurovia/Veyrath.png "Veyrath")

Veyrath

Reconstruction based upon multiple sightings above an industrial district of [[Ordon]]. No verified photograph has revealed additional anatomical detail.

**Veyrath**, known throughout parts of [[Ordon]] as **the Black Saint**, is an [[Umbuso]] associated with dense urban centres, industrial districts and prolonged atmospheric pollution.

Unlike other documented Umbuso, Veyrath has never been clearly observed.

Every known sighting presents the entity in exactly the same manner:

**as a black silhouette against the sky.**

No face has been seen.

No skin has been described.

No reliable account has identified where its body begins or ends.

Only the lights remain consistent.

**ARCHIVAL NOTE UMB-004-A**

Do not refer to recovered imagery as photographs *of Veyrath*.

Current evidence does not establish that the silhouette represents the physical appearance of UMB-004.

Use:

**“photograph containing a UMB-004 manifestation.”**

## Overview

Veyrath is associated almost exclusively with the cities of [[Ordon]], particularly districts dominated by dense gothic architecture, heavy industry, foundries, elevated machinery and old steam infrastructure.

Witnesses typically notice the entity above the skyline.

It appears enormously tall.

Its upper body is irregular and branching, commonly interpreted as possessing multiple heads, horns, limbs or tendrils.

Descriptions are inconsistent.

Illustrations are inconsistent.

Photographs are inconsistent.

The silhouette itself is not.

Nobody has ever seen what Veyrath looks like.

People have only seen the shape which says that Veyrath is there.

## Appearance

Descriptions of Veyrath's anatomy are considered unreliable.

The entity appears as a complete absence of visible detail.

Its body is darker than surrounding structures even when viewed at night.

Artificial illumination does not reveal texture.

Searchlights directed toward the silhouette have failed to produce reflected light.

Lightning behind the entity increases the clarity of its outline without exposing any internal features.

Observers commonly describe:

- several elongated upper appendages;
- between three and seven apparent heads;
- a large central mass;
- narrow structures resembling arms;
- curling projections resembling tails or smoke;
- and numerous white lights embedded within the silhouette.

No two anatomical diagrams agree.

This is not solely the result of poor visibility.

During simultaneous observations from different locations, witnesses have reported **different outlines**.

## The Silhouette Condition

The inability to observe Veyrath directly is known as the **Silhouette Condition**.

It persists regardless of distance, weather or method of observation.

Known attempts include:

- optical telescopes;
- industrial searchlights;
- photographic plates;
- high-speed cameras;
- thermal imaging;
- airborne observation;
- Axius-enhanced vision;
- and direct observation from neighbouring rooftops.

All have failed.

Equipment capable of detecting surrounding buildings normally records those buildings without issue.

The region occupied by Veyrath appears as featureless darkness.

**VISIBLE SURFACE DETAIL:** 0

**THERMAL SIGNATURE:** NONE

**REFLECTED LIGHT:** NONE

**KNOWN MASS:** UNKNOWN

**CONFIRMED PHYSICAL BODY:** UNRESOLVED

Researchers initially assumed that smoke or atmospheric pollution concealed the entity.

This theory was abandoned following Manifestation UMB-004-12.

The air was completely clear.

The silhouette remained.

## The Lights

Veyrath contains several points of intense white light.

These are the only features ever observed within the silhouette.

They are usually interpreted as eyes.

This interpretation is not officially recognised.

The number varies.

Most sightings contain between eight and fourteen.

Several historical illustrations show significantly more.

The lights sometimes appear in pairs.

At other times, their arrangement shows no obvious biological symmetry.

**OBSERVATION UMB-004-06**

Observer A reports 11 lights.

Observer B reports 8 lights.

Observer C reports 14 lights.

Photographic plate contains 9.

All observations made simultaneously.

Do not attempt reconciliation.

There is no evidence that these lights serve as visual organs.

Nevertheless, witnesses frequently report the impression of being watched.

## Smoke

Veyrath sightings have historically been associated with industrial smoke.

The oldest Ordon records refer to enormous shapes glimpsed between chimney stacks during periods of intense factory activity.

For generations, the phenomenon was dismissed as smoke distortion.

The discovery of older accounts complicated this explanation.

Descriptions resembling Veyrath exist from centuries before Ordon industrialised.

Those accounts describe:

  
*a black congregation above the chimneys of winter.*

No chimneys existed in the recorded settlement at the time.

The original manuscript contains an illustration.

The architecture depicted resembles modern Ordon.

The document has been removed from public exhibition.

## Behaviour

Veyrath has never been observed performing conventional locomotion.

It does not walk.

It does not fly.

It does not climb.

Its location simply changes.

During manifestations lasting several hours, Veyrath may appear behind one section of skyline and later be observed elsewhere.

No transition has been documented.

Witnesses who maintain uninterrupted visual contact frequently insist that the creature has not moved.

Observers elsewhere simultaneously report it in a different location.

Both reports may be correct.

Never attempt to determine which building Veyrath is standing behind.

This instruction appears in Ordon folklore centuries before formal Umbuso research began.

Its significance remains unclear.

## The Skyline Rule

One of the most consistent features of UMB-004 manifestations is Veyrath's relationship with architecture.

The entity is almost always observed **behind** structures.

Chimneys.

Church spires.

Clock towers.

Factory stacks.

Bridges.

Cranes.

Rooftops.

Even when observers relocate, Veyrath continues to appear behind the skyline from their perspective.

Attempts to physically approach the supposed position of the entity have therefore produced contradictory results.

An observer may estimate Veyrath to be two kilometres away.

Another, viewing from the opposite side of the district, may also place it two kilometres away.

Both describe the entity as being behind the buildings between them.

**FIELD NOTE**

We reached the street where it should have been standing.

Nothing was there.

We turned around.

It was behind the building we'd just left.

The team responsible for this observation immediately terminated the investigation.

## Architecture of Ordon

Some historians believe Veyrath influenced the development of traditional Ordon architecture.

Older districts frequently contain:

- excessively tall spires;
- narrow towers;
- crowded rooflines;
- decorative iron frameworks;
- bridges between buildings;
- elaborate chimney structures;
- and sightlines intentionally interrupted by architectural features.

The conventional explanation is aesthetic.

Several recovered construction documents suggest another purpose.

A design specification dated ████ repeatedly instructs architects:

**NEVER ALLOW AN UNBROKEN VIEW OF THE WESTERN SKY.**

No explanation is provided.

Similar instructions have been discovered in plans for churches, railway stations and factories.

## The Black Saint

The title **Black Saint** originates in working-class districts of industrial Ordon.

During the height of early industrialisation, workers occasionally reported an enormous dark figure standing beyond factory roofs.

The phenomenon became associated with industrial accidents.

According to surviving folklore, seeing the Black Saint before beginning a shift meant that someone would not return home.

Factory owners publicly discouraged the superstition.

Privately, several facilities suspended operations following reported sightings.

One surviving notice states:

**FOUNDRY CLOSED UNTIL MONDAY**

Atmospheric conditions unsuitable.

Machinery undergoing inspection.

Do not ring the upper bell.

The final instruction appears only on the handwritten original.

## Bells

Veyrath possesses an unexplained association with bells.

Church bells, factory shift bells and clock towers have all reportedly malfunctioned during manifestations.

Common reports include:

- bells ringing without mechanical movement;
- clocks striking incorrect hours;
- bells producing no sound despite visible movement;
- multiple towers ringing simultaneously;
- and bells being heard from structures which possess none.

During UMB-004-08, seventeen clock towers across central Ordon rang thirteen times at precisely 02:11.

Only four contained functional bells.

Veyrath was photographed above the eastern industrial district nine minutes later.

**ORDON MUNICIPAL DIRECTIVE // UMB-004**

During a suspected UMB-004 manifestation:

Do not investigate unexplained bells.

Do not enter bell towers.

Do not attempt manual correction of malfunctioning clock mechanisms.

If a clock strikes **thirteen**:

Leave the building.

## Recorded Images

Hundreds of photographs purportedly depicting Veyrath exist.

Most are fraudulent, misidentified smoke formations or conventional atmospheric effects.

Approximately ██ remain unexplained.

Confirmed imagery shares a peculiar property.

Image enhancement reveals additional detail in:

- buildings;
- clouds;
- smoke;
- machinery;
- rainfall;
- birds;
- and distant terrain.

It never reveals additional detail within Veyrath.

Increasing exposure produces a brighter environment surrounding the silhouette.

The silhouette remains black.

One researcher described the phenomenon as:

*It isn't underexposed.*

*There is simply no image there.*

## Witness Disagreement

Witness accounts of Veyrath contradict one another more severely than those of most Umbuso.

Observers disagree about:

- number of heads;
- number of limbs;
- height;
- orientation;
- distance;
- position;
- and whether the entity was moving.

They almost universally agree on three details.

It was black.

There were lights inside it.

They knew they were looking at something alive.

Why that certainty is so consistent remains unexplained.

## Relationship with Axius

[[Axius]] displays unusual perceptual effects around UMB-004 manifestations.

No direct suppression has been documented.

Instead, Axius users frequently report becoming aware of architectural spaces which should not exist.

Examples include:

- additional floors in buildings;
- windows visible only from certain streets;
- staircases continuing above the top floor;
- doors appearing between adjacent buildings;
- chimneys extending significantly farther upward than physically possible;
- and silhouettes visible through solid walls.

These effects disappear after manifestations end.

One Axius practitioner claimed to have followed a staircase upward for approximately forty minutes.

The building contained six floors.

When recovered, the practitioner was found on the ground floor.

They refused to explain what was at the top.

## Current Status

Veyrath is classified:

**ACTIVE — INTERMITTENT**

Unlike several known Umbuso, no specific territory can be sealed around UMB-004.

Manifestations occur within populated areas.

Major evacuations are therefore rarely feasible.

Modern Ordon maintains automated atmospheric and visual monitoring throughout several industrial districts.

The system primarily searches for simultaneous reports involving:

- unexplained white lights;
- silhouette anomalies;
- abnormal bell activity;
- contradictory distance measurements;
- and visual obstructions appearing above the skyline.

The most recent confirmed manifestation occurred in ████.

It lasted approximately thirteen minutes.

No casualties were officially recorded.
