# Wintrow

![](Wintrow_portrait.jpg "Wintrow, Pirate King of Armada")

Wintrow, Pirate King of Armada

**Wintrow** is the current (and first) Pirate King of [[Armada]] and captain of the *[[Vestrit]]*. Born into a wealthy merchant family in [[Ordon]], he was originally educated as a healer and theologian rather than a sailor.

During a pilgrimage to [[Mount_Targon|Mount Targon]], his ship was raided by bloodthirsty pirates. Being held as a slave for many a year, it wasn't until he was freed that he realised his work as a priest did no real good in the world.

Wintrow did not seek piracy or political power. When he was freed by the crew of the [[Vestrit]], he commandeered the ship. Ruthlessly, he killed anyone who opposed his tenure, demanding that the world needed to change, and the wheel of fate should spin.

Following a [[Seven_Flags_War| period of conflict between pirate fleets]], he was chosen as the first ever Pirate King of [[Armada]]. His reign has been defined by attempts to restrain the cruelty traditionally associated with Armada without destroying the freedom on which the region was founded.

## Appearance

Wintrow is a lean young man of average height, with dark hair usually worn to his shoulders. His grey-green eyes and reserved expression often make him appear more like a scholar than a pirate captain. He only has nine fingers, and is tattooed with the mark of slave.

A pale scar runs from beneath his left ear towards his collarbone. It was received during his first year in Armada and is normally visible above the collar of his shirts.

Unlike many Armadan captains, Wintrow avoids elaborate jewellery and displays of captured wealth. He generally wears:

- A long, dark sea coat
- Plain shirts in white, grey or faded blue
- Hard-wearing boots
- A red captain's sash worn only during formal gatherings
- A black cord bearing tokens from the fleets that recognise his rule

Wintrow does not wear a conventional crown. The black cord serves as the symbol of the Pirate King.

## Personality

Wintrow is thoughtful, observant and deeply compassionate. He prefers negotiation to intimidation and considers violence a failure rather than a demonstration of strength.

His gentle manner is sometimes mistaken for weakness. In practice, Wintrow possesses a stubborn moral certainty and is willing to place himself in considerable danger rather than surrender someone under his protection. When angered, his cruel disposition can show itself.

He is particularly opposed to:

- Slavery and forced service
- The mistreatment of prisoners
- Violence against civilians
- Captains withholding a crew's rightful share

Despite his ideals, Wintrow is not naïve. His years in Armada have made him cautious, politically calculating and capable of deception. He understands that ruling pirates requires compromise, and he is frequently troubled by the concessions he makes to maintain peace.

## Background

### Early Life

Wintrow was born in the [[Lowlands]], to a prosperous merchant family involved in maritime trade. His relatives expected him to eventually join the family enterprise, but he showed little interest in commerce, industry or social advancement.

As a child, he was quiet and intensely curious. He was particularly interested in medicine, dreams and helping others (from his ivory tower)

At his own request, Wintrow was sent to study with a religious and scholarly order. There, he trained as a healer and learned theology, history, languages and basic surgery.

He expected to remain with the order permanently.

### Burial At Sea

Determined that **something** lay at the peak of [[Mount_Targon|Mount Targon]], he gathered several other priests and set out on a holy pilgrimage to finally establish civilisation there.

During a voyage through these waters, the vessel was intercepted by the pirate ship [[Vestrit]]. Wintrow was taken captive alongside several surviving crew members.

His medical knowledge made him useful to his captors. Rather than remaining a prisoner, he gradually became the ship's healer and an adviser to its captain.

Too satisfied with his work as a healer, the prior captain of Vestrit kept him on - sending news of his death to his family.

### Life on Vestrit

Wintrow struggled with life aboard the Vistrit. He was horrified by the casual violence of the captain, but he also discovered a society full of life and culture.

Many pirates were escaped labourers, deserters, exiles, debtors or people driven from other countries. Their loyalty to piracy was often secondary to their desire to live beyond the authority of the nations that had failed them.

Wintrow earned respect by treating wounded crew members without regard for rank and by repeatedly placing himself between prisoners and the violent captain.

After a dreadful storm, the captain was killed, and the surviving crew selected Wintrow as their temporary captain. He initially refused the position but accepted when no other candidate could command the confidence of the whole crew.

The temporary appointment became permanent.

### Becoming a Pirate

The harsh realities of being the captain of a pirate vessel was initially a shock to Wintrow. He could no longer look away as his crew killed, raided and plundered. But they would no longer take slaves. Instead, Wintrow allowed those who wanted to escape fate another chance. Those on a vessel often had a reason to be there - debts, loneliness, insanity. All reasons to escape the old world.

The Vestrit and Wintrow averaged each other out. He softened the crew, and the crew hardened him. He realised - quite simply - he was beloved. As he sat in his private quarters, surrounded by people who'd die for him, with a chest full of valuable goods acting as his doorstop - this wasn't enough.

### Rise to Pirate King

Before Wintrow's reign, Armada was divided between several powerful captains and competing fleets. Their rivalries eventually developed into the conflict known as the [[War_for_Armada|War for Armada]].

Wintrow initially acted as a neutral healer and negotiator. As the conflict worsened, he organised an alliance of smaller crews, independent ports and civilian settlements threatened by the fighting.

His coalition did not possess the largest fleet, but it controlled vital supplies and safe harbours. Wintrow used this position to force the major captains into negotiation. Those who did not agree, were surprisingly killed in brutal ways, by extremists who saw Wintrow's way as better.

At the resulting [[Black_Moot|Black Moot]], he was chosen as Pirate King.

Wintrow accepted the title only after its powers were formally restricted. He maintains that the Pirate King is not the owner of Armada, but the servant responsible for preventing its captains from destroying one another.

## Reign as Pirate King

Wintrow's authority depends upon the continued support of Armada's captains. He cannot issue laws in the same manner as a conventional monarch, and many of his decisions must be negotiated through the [[Black_Moot|Black Moot]].

His major reforms include:

- Prohibiting the sale of captives within Armadan ports
- Establishing rules for the treatment and exchange of prisoners
- Standardising the division of captured wealth
- Recognising sanctuary aboard ships displaying the white lantern
- Supporting the use of [[Currency#PrizeMarks|Prize-Marks]]

These reforms remain controversial. Some Armadans consider Wintrow a necessary reformer, while others believe he is attempting to turn Armada into another version of Ordon - the same rules they wanted to run from.

## Skills and Abilities

### Medicine

Wintrow is an accomplished healer with knowledge of surgery, disease, herbal medicine and injuries suffered at sea.

He frequently treats members of his own crew and continues to work in the sickbay despite his position as Pirate King.

### Diplomacy

Wintrow's greatest strength is his ability to understand competing interests. He rarely attempts to defeat an opponent outright when he can instead give them a reason to cooperate.

### Languages

His education and years at sea have given him knowledge, [[Languages#Common|Common]], [[Languages#Elven|Elven]], \[Languages#Celestial\|Celestial\]\], [[Languages#Cant|Cant]]

### Sailing

Wintrow was not raised as a sailor and initially lacked practical experience. He has since become a competent navigator and captain, although he continues to rely heavily on more experienced members of his crew during severe weather.

### Combat

Wintrow is capable with a short sword and boarding knife but is not considered an exceptional duellist. His fighting style is defensive and focused on surviving long enough to escape or receive assistance.

## Relationships

### The Crew of the Vestrit

The crew of the *[[Vestrit]]* are Wintrow's closest allies and the foundation of his power. He avoids maintaining a strict distance from them and continues to eat, work and keep watch alongside ordinary sailors when possible.

This closeness inspires fierce loyalty but creates difficulties when he must discipline or overrule them.

### [[Captain_Kenneth_Voss|Captain Kenneth Voss]]

[[Kenneth_Voss|Captain Kenneth Voss]] was the original captain of the armada

one of the first Armadan captains to recognise Wintrow's value. She taught him the customs of Armada and helped establish his reputation among its crews.

Maela supports Wintrow personally but opposes several of his reforms. Their relationship shifts regularly between mentorship, friendship and political rivalry.

### His Ordonian Family

Wintrow see's no point in returning to a world he no longer belongs - especially when they believed him dead for so long. With his new infamy, it can be inferred Wintrow is not his real name.

Wintrow still carries a wooden [[Currency#Standard|Standard Coin]] from his childhood. He claims it has no sentimental value but has never allowed anyone to discard it.

### The Captains of Armada

Wintrow's relationship with the Armadan captains is complicated. Some genuinely respect him, while others support him only because his rule has increased stability and trade.

A number of captains are actively waiting for an opportunity to remove him.

### Prisoners and Refugees

Wintrow is widely respected among escaped prisoners, debtors and political exiles. The white lantern displayed by the *Pilgrim's Wake* has become a recognised symbol of temporary sanctuary.

This reputation places continuous pressure on Wintrow to intervene in disputes beyond Armada's borders.

## Possessions

- **The black cord** — The closest thing Wintrow possesses to a crown.
- **Ordonian Standard** — A wooden coin carried since childhood.
- **Healer's case** — Surgical and medicinal equipment from his former order.
- **Captain's knife** — A practical boarding knife rather than a ceremonial weapon.
- **The white lantern** — Displayed by the *Pilgrim's Wake* when offering sanctuary.

## Reputation

Wintrow's reputation is mixed.

Across Armada, general consensus is supportive and adoration, but there are some notable outliers. A minor religious cult has formed around him, referring to him as the **Priest of Tides**.

Skgurr, Ordon and Halingo are fearful of him, aware of his 'weak' nature but believe that unifying the pirates is an utterly terrifying concept.
