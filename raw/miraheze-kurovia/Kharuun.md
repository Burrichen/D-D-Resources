# Kharuun

ABERRATION // RARE CREATURE

**Kharuun**

The Faceless Hornbeast

![](/assets/miraheze-kurovia/Kharuun.png "A Kharuun beneath Mount Targon")

A Kharuun beneath Mount Targon

Adult Kharuun in the wastes beneath [[Mount_Targon|Mount Targon]].

**Kharuun** are rare quadrupedal [[Aberrations]] found exclusively in [[Targoth]].

They possess heavily armoured bodies, enormous crescent horns and a completely featureless head lacking eyes, nostrils or a visible mouth.

Kharuun are particularly feared for their ability to distort weight around themselves, lifting loose stone and debris from the ground before violently bringing it back down.

**DANGER — CR 8**

Kharuun are highly territorial and capable of crushing mounted travellers or breaking through light fortifications during a charge.

Do not mistake the absence of eyes for blindness.

## Appearance

Adult Kharuun are heavily built creatures covered in pale coarse fur and dense black crystalline armour.

Their most recognisable features are two enormous violet-black horns curving around an otherwise featureless head.

No eyes, nostrils, mouth or conventional ears have been identified.

The tail is long and segmented, terminating in irregular crystalline growths which continue developing throughout the creature's life.

Small fragments occasionally break away without apparent injury.

**Anatomical anomaly**

Dissections have located neither a conventional mouth nor a digestive tract capable of sustaining an animal of this size.

How Kharuun obtain nourishment remains unresolved.

## Spatial Distortion

Kharuun tissue contains unusually dense concentrations of [[Axius]], particularly around the horns and crystalline growths.

When threatened, an adult can create a short-lived distortion in the area surrounding itself.

Recorded effects include:

- loose stones rising from the ground;
- sudden changes in perceived weight;
- people and animals being pulled off balance;
- projectiles changing trajectory;
- fragments of rock orbiting the creature;
- and suspended debris crashing violently back to the ground.

The phenomenon ordinarily lasts only a few seconds.

The stones rising are the warning.

Do not wait for them to reach their highest point.

Some researchers believe the Kharuun is manipulating gravity.

Others argue that the effect represents an unusual manifestation of Axius rather than gravity itself.

Neither explanation has been conclusively demonstrated.

## Senses

Kharuun possess no visible eyes or ears.

Experiments suggest that they detect movement through ground vibration, changes in air pressure and fluctuations in surrounding Axius.

This does not fully explain their awareness.

Kharuun have accurately located stationary individuals at distances where neither vibration nor sound should have provided sufficient information.

Attempts to blind or visually distract a Kharuun are therefore ineffective.

## Habitat

Kharuun are found exclusively within [[Targoth]].

They favour:

- high mountain valleys;
- exposed rocky plains;
- ancient crater fields;
- abandoned settlements;
- and the barren country surrounding [[Mount_Targon|Mount Targon]].

Known territories frequently contain unusual quantities of dark crystalline material.

Whether the Kharuun seek these deposits, feed upon them, or somehow produce them remains disputed.

Sightings become considerably more frequent closer to Mount Targon.

## Crystalline Growths

The black structures covering a Kharuun are neither ordinary stone nor conventional biological armour.

They grow directly from underlying tissue and contain unusually high concentrations of Axius.

Damaged formations slowly regenerate.

Larger crystals are particularly common along the back, forelimbs and tail.

Material recovered from dead specimens may retain weak spatial effects for several hours.

Older fragments eventually become inert.

This material is highly valuable to researchers, although transporting freshly recovered samples requires specialised containment.

## Challenge Rating

CR 8

**Threat: Severe**

Kharuun combine heavy natural armour, tremendous charging power and short-range spatial distortion.

Their most dangerous attacks are usually preceded by nearby stones and debris beginning to rise.

Experienced parties are advised to remain mobile and avoid fighting on unstable terrain.

## Adventure Uses

A Kharuun may appear as:

- a creature blocking an important mountain pass;
- the inhabitant of a newly discovered crater;
- the reason a mining settlement was abandoned;
- the source of unexplained gravitational disturbances;
- a specimen sought by researchers studying Aberrations;
- a territorial creature disturbed by excavation near [[Mount_Targon|Mount Targon]];
- or evidence that something beneath Targoth is affecting the surrounding wildlife.
