# Driftling

WIDESPREAD CREATURE

**Driftling**

Little travellers of Kurovia's rivers

**Driftling**

![](/assets/miraheze-kurovia/Drifling.png "A Driftling")

A Driftling

Known to fall asleep while travelling.

**Driftlings** are small semi-aquatic mammals found throughout the rivers, lakes and sheltered coastlines of [[Ordon]], [[Shinkira]], [[Hjarthland]] and [[Nareth]].

They are named for their habit of floating on their backs and allowing rivers to carry them downstream.

Driftlings are famously lazy, but form strong and lasting bonds with their families, companions and human caretakers.

“Never ask a Driftling where it's going.

It probably doesn't know either.”

— Common riverside saying

## Appearance

Driftlings have rounded bodies, short limbs, broad tails and thick water-resistant fur. Their large eyes allow them to see effectively in dark or murky water.

Their coat colours vary between brown, cream, grey and pale gold.

Regional populations have developed minor differences suited to their environments.

**Natural swimmers**

Despite their small limbs and relaxed appearance, Driftlings are extremely capable swimmers.

Their broad tails provide most of their propulsion, allowing them to move considerably faster in water than on land.

## Behaviour

Driftlings spend much of their lives sleeping, eating or drifting with the current.

Although they are capable swimmers, they avoid unnecessary effort. A Driftling travelling downstream may float for several miles without deliberately choosing a destination.

Groups often sleep close together in calm water.

They may hold paws, tails or pieces of floating vegetation to prevent themselves from drifting apart.

Driftlings are highly social. Once one has bonded with a person or another animal, it may remain loyal to them for the rest of its life.

They are also notably reluctant to leave comfortable sleeping locations.

Attempts to move a sleeping Driftling often result in the animal immediately returning to the same location.

## Habitat and Range

Driftlings favour slow-moving rivers, sheltered lakes, estuaries and coastlines with plentiful food.

They are considered a [[Widespread_creatures|widespread Kurovian species]] because healthy populations exist in several otherwise very different regions.

They adapt particularly well to waterways altered by human settlement.

Canals, docks, artificial ponds and reservoirs may all support Driftling populations.

## Regional Populations

### [[Ordon]]

**Ordonian Driftlings** are commonly found in canals, freight rivers and industrial docks.

Many have learned to climb aboard barges and simply allow themselves to be carried between settlements.

Dockworkers often consider them good-luck animals, despite their habit of sleeping inside unattended cargo.

Some individual Driftlings become so familiar with particular trade routes that crews recognise them on sight.

### [[Shinkira]]

**Shinkiran Driftlings** commonly possess lighter beige or cream-coloured coats.

They favour irrigation channels, quiet riverbanks and ponds close to human settlement.

Driftlings living around agricultural communities frequently become semi-tame, returning to the same houses or farms when food is available.

Some rural families consider a Driftling repeatedly visiting the same home a sign of good fortune.

### [[Hjarthland]]

**Hjarthland Driftlings** have especially thick coats and can survive in water which would be dangerous to most similar animals.

They are often allowed into homes during severe winter storms.

Refusing shelter to a Driftling is considered needlessly cruel in many Hjarthland communities.

Allowing one inside without securing the pantry is considered needlessly foolish.

### [[Nareth]]

**Narethian Driftlings** inhabit rivers, swamps and flooded rainforests.

They are generally more alert than populations elsewhere due to the region's numerous predators.

Rather than sleeping in open water, they often rest among roots, reeds or upon the backs of sufficiently large and slow-moving creatures.

The latter behaviour appears to irritate the animals being used as resting places considerably more than it concerns the Driftling.

## Relationship with People

Driftlings are commonly kept as pets and shipboard mascots.

They are particularly popular among fishermen, bargemen and communities living near rivers.

They can be trained to retrieve objects and locate familiar people, but make poor conventional working animals.

A Driftling usually performs a task only when motivated by:

- food;
- affection;
- curiosity;
- or concern for a bonded companion.

Their fondness for food can make them a nuisance around markets, docks and kitchens.

A Driftling which learns where food is stored is remarkably difficult to convince that it should not return.

**Domestication**

Driftlings are considered partially domesticated in several regions.

However, the distinction between a domestic Driftling and a wild Driftling which has discovered free food is frequently unclear.

## Hunting and Pelts

Driftling fur is dense, soft and naturally resistant to water.

It is traditionally used for waterproof clothing, winter garments, gloves and expensive coat linings.

The value of their pelts has led to organised hunting in parts of [[Ordon]] and [[Hjarthland]].

This practice is controversial because Driftlings are social, intelligent and often unafraid of people.

Hunters can exploit their trusting behaviour to approach them easily.

Some riverside settlements have banned Driftling hunting, while others permit it only during particular seasons.

Several regions have increasingly moved toward obtaining Driftling fur through brushing and grooming domesticated animals rather than killing them.

## Challenge Rating

1/8

Driftlings have a **Challenge Rating of 1/8**.

They are not naturally aggressive and normally escape danger by swimming or hiding.

When cornered, injured or defending a bonded companion, a Driftling may bite and scratch.

Their threat comes primarily from their speed in water and determination rather than physical strength.

## Adventure Uses

A Driftling could appear as:

- A missing pet belonging to an important NPC
- A barge or ship mascot
- A guide to something hidden beneath a river
- A creature carrying evidence tangled in its fur
- The target of illegal pelt hunters
- The only surviving animal from a contaminated waterway
- A loyal companion searching for its missing owner
- An [[Utsuro]]-altered Driftling developing an unusual ability
