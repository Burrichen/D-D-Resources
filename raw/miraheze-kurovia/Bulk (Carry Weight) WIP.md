# Bulk (Carry Weight) WIP

# Bulk - A carry weight edit

Simplification of carry weight, so you can keep the gameplay but discard the fluff.

## Discussion & Ethos

Let's be real, carry weight is not fun. Most DMs i've played with completely ignore it, those who do keep it, stop keeping track by session 10. I think carry weight is a mechanic that *can* be fun, but it's implementation in D&D is so strange. 5e and 1st have a 'simple is better' approach to everything.... apart from carry weight. Like all my systems, I try and make sure it solves multiple problems with D&D.

Firstly, it means that DMs and Players can keep track of carry weight simply and quickly, meaning they are more likely to actually do so.

Secondly, it means players can start making meaningful choices again. Cool new weapon that's 1 Bulk more than the previous one? Now you have to discard something of note. It also means you never run into the edge case of 'I'm literally 1kg away from carrying the most important item'. I especially recommend running Bulk with my Survival and Travel rules to create really fun emergent gameplay (a new weapon, or their tent?)

Finally, it buffs strength. Strength is by far the weakest physical stat, and when you discard carry weight rules, it becomes even **more** useless. This effectively shadow-buffs strength by making it useful - if all your players start dumping it, life on the road becomes harder.

## Mechanics

### Items

All items Bulk is converted to the following values

0 = for items that players can theoretically have endless of (rope, coins, etc)

0.5 = for items that you want to be plentiful (potions, lanterns etc) but to not be endless.

3 = For very heavy / cumbersome items (e.g. Giant Hammer, Tent). Remember that bulk isn't just 'weight' but also connivence of carrying.

Tip: You can use 0.1 bulk for potions and various items too. The reason I don't include this out the box is because I think it adds complexity to a system which is designed to simplify. In theory I think this is better than scrapping 0.5 though.

Tip: Don't convert every single item. Convert the ones players CHOOSE and items you will give them just before you do.

### Players

Players bulk = 10 +/- STR score modifier
