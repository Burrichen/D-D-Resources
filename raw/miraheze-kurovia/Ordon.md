# Ordon

![](Ordon_splash.jpg "The industrial cities and surrounding countryside of Ordon")

The industrial cities and surrounding countryside of Ordon

![](Ordon_flag.png "Flag of Ordon")

Flag of Ordon

**Ordon** is the largest and most influential country in [[Kurovia]]. It is known for its industrial cities, steam-powered machinery, airships and the workable form of [[Axius]] found beneath its land.

Ordon's Axius manifestation represents the concept of **progress**. It can be refined into fuel, conductive machinery, armour and substances that increase human productivity. Although the country's capital possesses advanced technology, the settlements beyond its industrial centre increasingly resemble older and more traditional societies, deprived of the progress that so defines Central Ordon.

## Overview

Ordon is a country divided between rapid industrial development and older rural traditions. Its central cities are filled with factories, railways, workshops and Axius-powered technology, while its distant provinces remain dependent on agriculture, traditional crafts and local customs.

## Axius in Ordon

Ordon's manifestation of [[Axius]] represents **progress**. It appears as a malleable ore that can be treated in several different ways.

### Fuel

When condensed, the ore becomes an energy source more powerful than coal. Much like coal, it also belches out fumes at a much higher rate. The resulting energy is connected to the [[Weave]].

### Conductive Material

When treated lightly, Axius becomes highly conductive. This property has allowed Ordonian engineers to develop complex steam machinery and other advanced technologies.

### Armour

Ordon's strongest armour is manufactured from Axius ore. The armour provides exceptional protection, but prolonged use can create an attachment between the wearer and the metal.

Some wearers eventually become unable to remove it.

This effect is not widely known among the public.

## History

[[Quinten_Hearspar|Quinten Hearspar]] always wanted to be a scholar. Defeat crept into his becoming, his most familiar feeling. He found himself toiling in the fields, just like his father before him. His blood, sweat and body being given to the ground, for an owner who never even showed his face at their plot, but would still demand ever-increasing payments.

Quinten's luck wouldn't end there, after a bandit-raid led to his mother being left immobile and brain damaged. Five months later, his father abandoned them - going on to raise a family with another woman. A year after that, he stopped supporting them financially, while mother and son burned through their collective savings to rehabilitate her.

One hot day, Quinten suffered a stroke whilst working in the field. His body spasmed, falling to the ground, choking on his own spit. His fractured mind glimpsed something no other mortal had. A world of pure order, of complete logic - lacking in all the wrongs this world had shown him.

He awoke days later in that field, his body deemed unworthy of a coffin. Quinten could never see the world in the same way again. Lines of logic, as clear as words on parchment began appearing to him. He tried to tell those who asked about his recovery, but the stroke made his speech slurred, and went he was speaking coherently, the content of his speeches made very little sense. When he followed the logic, good things began to happen. He began making real money by implementing new irrigation techniques on his plot, which owned him a promotion. His mother was left alone, funds pulled when his new brain couldn't understand the familial bond.

Using his acquired funds, he left his farm. He traveled, day and night refusing to eat, drink of sleep until he found an old quarry. The lines of logic showed him where to go. A golden nugget, in the middle of a large pile of rocks, unseeable by the naked eye. Painfully, severing his vocal cords, he ate the ore.

Like a man possessed, he dug. By the time his body gave out, he had collected another 5 of these ores, his body wrapped around them like a nursing mother. The first he sold, and using those funds, gathered a group of hearty workers to dig the area. With those profits, he secured the land itself. Then, he stopped selling. Creating blueprints and schemes he saw in his feverish dreams the first night he imbibed the ore - he made the first rudimentary steam engine. He used this for nothing over than drilling.

One day, the lines began to fade. It wasn't long after that when teh losses began. In a desperate bid to see what he once did, he sank money into the last golden lines of order. Into a machine to show him the logic of the world, to show him that clockwork universe. [[Eye_of_Gold|Eye of Gold]] was it's name. By utilising its power, Quinten was able to transform his wealth into a settlement, then a town, then a city. Eventually it began the capital.

Technological advancements filled the city, and it became an industrial powerhouse, at the cost of those living there. The use of [[Morrow]] became widespread. Quinten found his body growing old, less useful. The logic told him to discard it. His body was found, mutilated and partially fused to the engine. The engine, if it ever worked, never will again.

## Foreign Relations

### [[Halingo]]

Halingo and Ordon stand at a tense, but neutral standoff. The rail network connecting the two countries was financed by both of them, and a sign of co-operation, an outward truce. The truth of it is both countries are aware they both fill a niche - the Ordon ore and [[Ichor]] generate power. The leaders of Ordon are too self-centered and the [[Diesel_Barons|Diesel Barons]] are too greedy to ever work together - both are aware only one can truly survive. Both vie for [[Shinkira|Shinkira's]] affection - a stalemate in the game they play. For now though, for the commonfolk, it appears as is both are in a truce.

### [[Skgurr]]

For several hundred years after the Oblate, the area now known as **Skgurr** was once a violent, desolate wasteland, even if it did have inhabitants. The ancient order of the [[Ambit]] (Political allies of Ordon) was primarily responsible to turning Skgurr into a habitable area. Locals were begrudgingly accepting. It was when Ordon believed it was entitled to the mysterious magical items that washed up along Skgurr's shoreline that things got heated.

A war machine was deployed to scare the locals and rescue the troops. When this machine began eroding due to the [[Acid_Rain|Acid Rain]], a group of [[Steelbacks]] rescued the crew inside. This one act of heroism turned a simmering fued into a tenative alliance. The deployed platoon paying off their debt by joining the [[Steelbacks]] themselves, but not before returning to Ordon with some minor magical items as a delegacy. Knowing Skgurr's weather will forever hold off Ordon's war machines, the two countries became unlikely allies - with Ordon supplying basic goods in return for magical items, the two countries work in harmony.

### [[Shinkira]]

For hundereds of years, Shinkira was unassailable. Inhabited by [[Umbuso]], it was believed no humans could live on that island. It was only when an Ordonian [[airship]] flew over the island patrolling for [[Armada]] that they realized the towering creatures that once defined the island were now gone. Instead, villages had sprung up. People were there all along.

Attempting to deploy a delegacy as soon as possible, news of this new country was leaked by an informant. The beuocracy of Ordon had slowed it down, whilst other countries acted instinctively. In an act of cruelty, Halingo representatives reached the island on an oil powered vessel - and, knowing they had no chance of winning over the noble people, spent their time besmirching Ordon's name - not lying, but feeding them the worst stories of Ordon's sordid past. By the time the delegacy arrived, they were treated as hostile.

Instead, Shinkira decided to be independent, becoming increasingly aware that if they were to ally firmly with any greater power, that power would instantly revolt against the other. Thusly, Shinkira remains politically neutral, but accepting of all - for the greater good. It is not known how long this neutrality will last.

### [[Bratislor]]

It is safe to assume without the [[Barrier]], the two would be at violent war with one another. But, given the choice, Bratislor would be at war with everyone. The great barrier divides the two countries, and ensures Bratislor is contained. That isn't to say the barrier is impenetrable - far from it. Scholars and architects from both Ordon and Hjartheland worked in-tandem to construct this barrier, but it is Ordon that powers it and keeps it from functioning.

It is unclear wether its the lack of power or a deliberate, sinister implacation that the further north you travel, the weaker the barrier becomes. It isn't unheard of for the occasional monstrosity vaguely recognised as human passes through. Most are destroyed instantly.

There have been no attempts from inside the barrier to communicate formally.
