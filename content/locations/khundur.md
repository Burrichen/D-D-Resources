#Category/Location #Locations #Limate #Capital

# Story
Khundur is one of the oldest cities in the known world

# Basic Information:
**Origin - First Settlement:** This city was the first recorded place Dwarf emerged from the underground.
**Age - Ancient**: The capital is older than recorded memory.
**Size - Very large**: The capital can support ~150,000 people.
**General condition - Impressive:** The capital is well taken care of, and cleanliness is clearly a priority.
**Fortification - Extreme**: The capital is surrounded with stone walls, with many staffed watchtowers. The gate is massive, reinforced with iron bands, augmented with a polcullis and is always manned. Nothing goes in or out without the watch's knowledge or permission.

# Districts:
**4 total districts:**
**Arcane**. Magnificent condition. It is open and unguarded. Crime is infrequent.
Contains:
- Grand Library
- Academy
- A magic shop for clothing / armour
- A magic shop for wonderous items
- Magic mercanaries
- Barber
- Tavern
There are two notable locations: Firstly, a club, 'Magi Infernus' which is known for its generosity. Secondly is an alchemist, Skjur which has odd rumours of experimentation and live testing.

**Khundur Market**. Impressive condition. It is gated and guarded. Crime is common.
Contains:
- Baker
- Butcher
- General Store
- Magic Item Shop
- Smithy
- Tailor
- Tavern
There are two notable locations: Firstly, a Rare Trade Goods (shop) which is a common meeting place due to its exotic appearance. Secondly, a Hired Help - Caravan & Mount which has large advertising campaigns throughout the city.

**Stone Garden**. Dilapidated condition. It is guarded. Crime is infrequent.
Contains:
- Outdoor Recreational Area
- Inn
- Stable
There are two notable locations: Apothecary, which always seems to have some insane injury or event occurring. An artist, which is renowned for her services and her wife, who plays beautiful pieces whilst you wait and watch them work. This also hides the Zhentarim HQ.

**Temple of Gond**. Impressive condition. It is guarded. Crime is common.
Contains:
- Temple
- Library
- Scribes
- Priestly Guidance
- Hands of the Divine (divine magic in combat)
There is one notable location which is a bathhouse which is also a front for a strip area / drug dealing area.

# Government and High Society:
**Leadership: Hereditary**
**Governing Priority: Control**
**Priority approach: Aggressive (Moderate)**. A defensive stance is taken, though opportunity is provided for good faith.
**Priority success: Very Sucessful.** The leadership has made great strides at achieving its inital vision.

**Leader Lifestyle: Humble.** The leader requires only what is nessisary to live and function basically.
**Residence: Small mansion.** The leader's residence likely has several spare rooms, as well as space for a recreational room or two.
**Intent: Genuine**

# Military:
The capital has its own standing military force. The members are conscripted, criminals, and handpicked, which form different regiments. It is a grand size.
The criminal regiments have access to highly experimental and deadly diseases/chemicals.
The conscripted regiments have simple structures inside the capital, but the handpicked have moderate structures outside the capital.

**Intelligence Gathering**: N/A. The capital is not engaged in any intelligence gathering operations.
**Counterintelligence**: Modest. The capital has up to a few operatives working within its borders.
**Counterintelligence Watchfullness**: Weary. Efforts are diligent and concerted.

# Community:
**Population density: Crowded.** The capital is filled with jostling throngs of people. Practically all structures are occupied. Bumping into people is difficult.
**Population wealth: Wealthy.** Nearly everyone has what they need to live comfortably, many are able to live well, and some are very prosperous.
**Visitor traffic: Groups.** There are a fair amount of vistors to the capital.
**Disposition: Open.** Since they are cleared by the city watch, it means they are to be trusted and embraced as if they live here.
**Night activity: Slow.** Almost everything is closed except for taverns.

# Crime & Law
**Law Enforcement: Robust City Watch.** Run by a captain and two sergeants, the watch are able to place extra support at key points, as well as establish 3 or 4 patrols a day.
**General crime: Frequent.** The streets are unsafe alone, and a purse in plain sight is destined to be stolen. Vandalism and muggings are fairly regular.
**Organised crime: Talk.** Enogu hincidents have occured that most folk don't have trouble believing there is an organised criminal element in the capital.
