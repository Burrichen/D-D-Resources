**Plane god:** Cthulhu (also called the Woodland Man, and the Librarian)

**Plane god's motivation:** Dissolution. It despises anything tangible: form, thought, memory or consciousness. It desires nothing. The Oblate showed Cthulhu light, and awoke Him.

## Key features

- **Wrecks of Sanity.** The Far Realm is mostly featureless, but every now and then there are things the Great Old One has dreamed of: a shell of a dead god's thoughts, the petrified memory of a forgotten city, a continent of a mortal's failed dream.
- **Aberrations.** Tumours of thought.
- **Abyssal Strain.** When the Oblate receded, it didn't leave the material plane unscathed. Like greased paper, reality seeps through, just as the Far Realm seeps back. Seeping Maw cultists on the other side are desperate to pierce reality.

## Effect on players

Creatures of the Far Realm may have these traits:

- **Entropy of the Abyss.** Whenever the creature deals bludgeoning, piercing or slashing damage, it can change the damage type to necrotic.
- **Poison Tolerant.** The creature has advantage on saving throws it makes to avoid or end the poisoned condition on itself.
- **Siege Monster.** The creature deals double damage to objects and structures.

## GM notes

- The v3 note calls this plane "the Abyss" in several places, and two traits keep that wording. The user confirmed (2026-11-04) that the text describes the Far Realm. The Abyss is a separate Chaotic Evil plane.
- The Seeping Maw is a Cthulhu cult (v3, Random Characters and Hooks). It isn't curated yet.
