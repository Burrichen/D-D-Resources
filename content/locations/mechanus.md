**Plane god:** [Primus](/content/deities/primus.md)
**Plane god's motivation:** Perfection

Mechanus is one of the least-visited planes; its ruler, Primus, never leaves it and is notoriously isolationist. Even so, it is one of the only planes a mortal has had direct contact with. That mortal was Quinten, who went on to found [Ordon](/content/locations/ordon.md).

## GM notes

- Primus as ruler is inspired by Legion from *Mass Effect*.
- v3 calls the city Quinten founded "Ordus". The user confirmed this is Ordon (2026-11-04).
