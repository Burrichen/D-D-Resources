Sometime after creation, an interplanar bargain was struck between the Old Gods: there would be those who rival their power, who offend them, who challenge them. They would need a place to teach these beings a lesson, because death was not final enough.

A pact was created: as long as no god opposed it, any such creation would be sent to a new creation, Carceri, a sub-realm of [Limbo](/content/locations/limbo.md). This technically makes it the first demi-plane, predating the others by epochs.

Moulded like a whirlpool at the centre of Limbo, it ironically forms the largest coherent landmass in the plane. A multitude of beings dwell there, often as powerful as gods, but without their mercy. To be locked in Carceri is the greatest sentence known to the universe.

## Known prisoners

- Dianye, before she became [Kelemvor](/content/deities/kelemvor.md): her soul was held here until her father Abernath gave up the role of Death.
