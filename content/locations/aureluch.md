**Aureluch** is one of the largest industrial cities in [Ordon](/content/locations/ordon.md) outside [Morgenwelde](/content/locations/morgenwelde.md). Half built into a cliff face and half stretching across the lowlands, the city consists of rail-yards, foundries, lift-chains, suspended neighbourhoods and vertical engines connected by an enormous network of gates and bridges. It is the city that **binds Ordon together**.

Passengers, grain from the outer regions, military freight, machinery and Morrow fuel all pass through the city on their way to trade, to the capital, to the coast, or just to another city.

Its importance is not based upon political authority or cultural prestige, but upon infrastructure. A disruption in Aureluch can delay trade across much of Ordon, and thus the world.

## Overview

Aureluch developed around a natural break in the cliffs separating Ordon's inland provinces from the lower western routes. What began as a fortified crossing and freight station gradually expanded into one of the country's largest transport centres.

The city is divided into two halves, **cliffside** and **groundside**. Cliffside consists of smaller districts and observatory posts, as well as major passenger lines. Groundside consists of stations, rail-lines and most housing. It is commonplace to see Thunderrails being lifted cliffside for inspection and boarding.

Movement through Aureluch depends upon:

- Chain-driven lifts
- Suspended walkways
- Freight platforms
- Rail bridges
- Cliff tunnels
- Mechanical gates
- Inclined railways
- Stairways cut directly into the rock

## Geography and layout

Aureluch occupies both sides of a steep ravine known as the **Aureluch Cut**. The oldest districts are carved into the cliffs, while newer neighbourhoods have been constructed on platforms, bridge foundations and suspended frames.

## History

### The Old Crossing

Before Aureluch became a city, the ravine contained one of the few reliable routes through the surrounding cliffs. Early settlements maintained bridges, toll gates and storage houses for merchants travelling between inland Ordon and the coast. Its beauty and danger made it a landmark for traders.

### Discovery of Morrow

The industrial importance of the settlement increased following the widespread use of Morrow. The ore could be transported from inland mines to the crossing, refined locally and then moved towards Morgenwelde or the coast.

The first large foundries were established near the base of the ravine, where water and cliffside tunnels made heavy construction possible.

### The Railway Expansion

Aureluch grew rapidly after the construction of Ordon's national railway network.

Existing roads and bridges could not handle the volume of freight passing through the city. Engineers responded by building new rail levels above the old settlement, followed by lifts capable of moving entire freight wagons between elevations.

Aureluch's population increased as workers arrived from across Ordon.

### The Binding of Ordon

The completion of the **Grand Junction** transformed Aureluch into the central interchange of Ordon's freight network.

Grain from rural provinces, ore from inland mines, military equipment, coastal imports and Morrow fuel could now be redirected through a single city.

### Recent history

Aureluch continues to expand, although much of its oldest infrastructure is under severe strain.

## Districts

Working ideas for the city's districts:

- **Crown Station**: largest passenger terminal and administrative centre. Known for its clocks and cleanliness.
- **Grand Junction**: centre of the rail network, across several levels.
- **Chainward**: the lift-engine area.
- **Hanging Rows**: residential neighbourhoods suspended from reinforced cliff frames.
- **Blackgate**: controls the largest western freight entrance into Aureluch.
- **Furnace Deep**: contains Aureluch's heaviest foundries and oldest Morrow refineries.
- **Old Steps**: remains of the settlement that existed before the railways.
- **Underspan**: occupies the spaces beneath the largest bridges and freight platforms. Dangerous.

## Transport links

Aureluch is the most important transport interchange in Ordon outside the capital.

### Railways

The city connects several major railway routes:

- The **Morgwen Line** carries passengers, officials and manufactured goods to the capital.
- The **Inland Ore Line** transports raw Morrow and other minerals from Ordon's mining regions.
- The **Harvest Line** carries grain, livestock and preserved food from the outer provinces.
- The **Western Line** connects Aureluch to coastal ports.
- The **Military Spur** serves fortified depots and is restricted to authorised traffic.
- The **Highland Line** passes towards Ordon's northern settlements.

Most long-distance freight entering central Ordon passes through at least one Aureluch yard.

### Vertical engines

Aureluch's vertical engines move freight and passengers between the city's different elevations.

The largest can lift entire railway wagons. Smaller lifts transport workers, livestock and goods between neighbourhoods.

Lift access is controlled through gates, schedules and tolls. Breakdowns can leave whole districts temporarily isolated.

### Bridges

The city contains hundreds of bridges, ranging from small footbridges to enormous rail structures. Several major bridges contain buildings, markets and service roads along their length.

Important crossings include Union Bridge, Morrow Span, Founders' Crossing, The Nine Chains and the Grand Freight Bridge.

### Air travel

Aureluch maintains a limited airship platform at Crown Level.

The difficult winds around the ravine make airship travel unreliable. Most commercial and military traffic therefore continues to use rail.

## Economy and industry

Aureluch's economy is built around movement, storage and processing.

The city does not produce most of the goods that pass through it. Instead, it profits from controlling where those goods go.

The city is also a major centre for scrap dealing. Damaged machinery, broken rail components and rejected industrial materials are repaired or repurposed throughout the lower districts.

## Government

Aureluch is governed by a municipal council composed of civic officials, railway representatives, industrial owners and engineers. The council is commonly called the **Gate Council**.

The position of **First Gatekeeper** serves as the city's senior civic office. The title originated when the settlement was controlled through a single fortified crossing.

## Law and crime

The **Aureluch Gatewatch** is responsible for public order, freight inspection and transport security.

Smuggling is widespread due to the enormous volume of goods moving through the city. Contraband is hidden inside legitimate cargo, abandoned tunnels and unofficial lift systems.

The Underspan and Blackgate contain the city's largest criminal networks.

## Notable locations

- **Crown Station**: Aureluch's principal passenger terminal.
- **Grand Junction**: the centre of the national freight network.
- **The Nine Chains**: a series of enormous lift mechanisms spanning the ravine.
- **Union Bridge**: one of the city's oldest inhabited bridges.
- **Gate Council Hall**: the seat of Aureluch's municipal government.
- **The Last Lantern**: a well-known inn used by railway workers and travellers.
- **Furnace Twelve**: a major Morrow refinery with a troubled safety record.
- **The Silent Platform**: an abandoned station whose original destination has been removed from official records.
