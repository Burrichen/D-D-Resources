Before becoming an adventurer, you spent much of your early life crawling through dust-filled crypts, abandoned settlements and forgotten ruins in search of relics. Most of your discoveries were of questionable value, but a fortunate few provided enough coin for you to purchase proper adventuring equipment.

One item from those years remains especially important to you.

## Feature: Wild Magic Attunement

Your prolonged exposure to the ruins left by the Oblate has made you unusually sensitive to its lingering influence.

Whenever Wild Magic is cast within one mile of you, you sense its general location.

You can cast *detect magic* at will. When cast in this way, the spell only reveals whether an object, creature or person has been affected by the Oblate. It does not otherwise detect magical auras or reveal their schools of magic.
