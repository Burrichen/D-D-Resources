**Dustkips** are small desert creatures native to [Halingo](/content/locations/halingo.md). They are harmless, resourceful and capable of surviving in conditions that would kill many larger animals.

Wild Dustkips are commonly found near rocks, abandoned machinery and the outskirts of settlements. Some are kept as household pets.

## Appearance

Dustkips are small, yellow-scaled creatures with rounded bodies, large eyes and thick tails.

Their colouring helps them blend into Halingo's sand and dry grass. Dustkips living near the Black Sea often develop darker patches from soot and industrial waste.

## Survival

Dustkips possess no meaningful combat abilities. They survive by avoiding danger rather than confronting it.

They can burrow into cool sand, find small sources of water and eating roots, insects and scraps. Their thick tails store fat and moisture, allowing them to survive several days without food or water.

The Halingan phrase **“alive as a Dustkip”** is used for someone who survives circumstances that should have killed them.

## Behaviour

Dustkips are cautious but naturally curious. They often observe travellers from behind rocks before slowly approaching in search of food.

When frightened, a Dustkip freezes, flattens itself against the ground or disappears into the nearest available gap.

They communicate through soft chirps, tail movements and rapid blinking.

## Relationship with People

Dustkips are sometimes kept as pets in Halingan settlements.

They are inexpensive to feed, tolerate extreme heat and quickly become affectionate toward people who treat them kindly. A domesticated Dustkip often sleeps inside boots, cupboards or piles of clothing.

## Challenge Rating

Dustkips have a **Challenge Rating of 0**.

They cannot effectively harm most creatures and will always attempt to flee or hide when threatened.
