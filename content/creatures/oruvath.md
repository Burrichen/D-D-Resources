**Oruvath**, commonly referred to as **the Buried Choir**, is an [Umbuso](/content/lore/umbuso.md) documented in archaeological and historical records across several otherwise unrelated regions.

Unlike most known Umbuso, Oruvath displays no confirmed geographical territory.

Every credible appearance has instead occurred among extremely old ruins.

In several cases, no evidence has been found that those ruins were ever inhabited.

The entity is characterised by a massive central body, numerous independently moving jaws, several differently coloured eyes and long structures which disappear beneath the surrounding ground.

The full organism has never been observed.

**ARCHIVAL DIRECTIVE UMB-041-A**

Personnel entering a suspected UMB-041 site are prohibited from speaking unless necessary.

If vocal communication is required, use preassigned numeric codes.

If an unidentified voice addresses personnel by name:

**DO NOT RESPOND.**

## Overview

Oruvath is one of the few Umbuso documented beyond a single cultural or geographic region.

Records consistent with UMB-041 have been recovered from:

- ruined settlements;
- abandoned religious complexes;
- subterranean chambers;
- ancient fortifications;
- isolated stone monuments;
- and archaeological sites with no known builders.

Descriptions separated by centuries repeatedly mention the same characteristics:

numerous mouths;

dark fibrous or fur-like tissue;

isolated coloured eyes;

root-like appendages;

and voices.

For much of modern scholarship, these similarities were considered coincidental.

That interpretation changed after the discovery of ████████████████.

██████████████████████████████████████████████████████

UMB-041 classification was formally established shortly afterwards.

## Appearance

Oruvath possesses a massive, irregular body covered in dark brown and black material resembling coarse fur, bark or bundled fibres.

No consistent skeletal structure has been identified.

Numerous serpentine structures emerge from the central mass.

Many terminate in jaws.

Others appear to terminate in nothing at all.

The number of visible mouths varies dramatically between reports.

The lowest confirmed count is four.

The highest is ██.

Each mouth contains several rows of pale teeth.

Their shape differs between jaws.

Some resemble the teeth of carnivorous mammals.

Others do not correspond to any known organism.

Several independent eyes are embedded throughout the visible body.

Confirmed colours include:

- yellow;
- blue;
- red;
- white;
- and ██████.

There is no evidence that the eyes correspond anatomically with individual mouths.

## The Mouths

The mouths of Oruvath are capable of speech.

This behaviour is considered the defining characteristic of UMB-041.

Different mouths appear to possess different voices.

Documented examples include:

- adult men;
- adult women;
- children;
- elderly individuals;
- unfamiliar animal-like vocalisations;
- speakers of extinct languages;
- and individuals personally known to observers.

The mouths do not ordinarily speak together.

Each appears to maintain its own vocabulary and cadence.

No confirmed voice has ever originated from a person known to still be alive.

Whether Oruvath imitates the dead, records voices it has previously encountered or possesses some other mechanism remains unknown.

## Voice Identification

The most disturbing evidence surrounding UMB-041 originates from Voice Record 17.

During investigation of Site UMB-041-B, audio equipment recorded an unidentified female voice speaking from within the ruins.

The language was not recognised by the expedition.

The recording remained unclassified for twenty-three years.

A later archaeological team identified the language as a dialect which disappeared approximately six centuries before the recording was made.

The speaker described:

*The stones are warm.*

*It has opened another mouth.*

*We should not have answered.*

A written text containing nearly identical phrasing was subsequently discovered at another archaeological site approximately ████ kilometres away.

The inscription predates the audio recording by approximately 611 years.

## Mimicry

Oruvath appears capable of reproducing voices with extraordinary accuracy.

During Incident UMB-041-04, an expedition member reported hearing another researcher call for assistance from beyond the excavation perimeter.

The researcher whose voice had been heard was standing beside them at the time.

Shortly afterwards, the voice repeated the request.

It then began addressing individual members of the expedition by name.

23:11 — voice detected east of camp.

23:13 — identity resembles Researcher Lorne.

23:13 — Lorne confirms he did not speak.

23:14 — second voice detected.

23:16 — voice uses Researcher Ishar's name.

23:17 — third voice.

23:18 — ████████████████████

23:20 — all personnel ordered silent.

23:41 — voices continue.

02:04 — voices cease.

Oruvath was not visually observed during the incident.

The excavation was abandoned the following morning.

## The Ruins

Every confirmed Oruvath manifestation is associated with ancient architecture.

Initially, researchers assumed these locations represented abandoned settlements.

Several do.

Others present a more difficult problem.

At Site UMB-041-C, investigators uncovered extensive stone structures resembling:

- towers;
- roads;
- defensive walls;
- arches;
- monuments;
- and foundations.

No domestic objects were recovered.

No tools.

No pottery.

No graves.

No food remains.

No written records.

No evidence of habitation whatsoever.

There was a city.

There is no evidence that anyone ever lived there.

Subsequent excavations discovered that several apparently architectural structures extended far deeper underground than expected.

Analysis of recovered material produced inconsistent results.

Some samples were stone.

Some were mineralised organic tissue.

Some could not be classified.

Excavation was suspended.

## Structural Association

A minority of researchers have proposed that Oruvath does not inhabit ruins.

The theory proposes that the ruins are **part of Oruvath**.

Evidence remains inconclusive.

Several structures associated with UMB-041 contain formations displaying unusual similarities to biological growth.

Examples include:

- arches containing structures resembling ribs;
- towers with repeating internal cavities;
- walls displaying branching patterns similar to blood vessels;
- stone pillars which respond weakly to heat;
- and foundations containing material genetically related to no known organism.

The theory remains officially unaccepted.

## Movement

Oruvath has rarely been observed moving its central body.

Individual heads, jaws and tendrils move extensively.

The larger organism appears largely stationary.

This may be misleading.

Historical records place apparently identical manifestations in locations separated by enormous distances.

There are three accepted possibilities:

1.  multiple Oruvath-class entities exist;
2.  Oruvath is capable of travelling between sites by an unknown method;
3.  all known sites connect to the same organism.

The third possibility is considered improbable.

It has not been eliminated.

## Subterranean Extent

Attempts to map Oruvath beneath the surface have produced contradictory results.

Ground-penetrating equipment frequently identifies large organic structures extending away from manifestation sites.

The detected structures resemble roots, veins or tunnels.

At Site UMB-041-A, one such formation was tracked for approximately 19 kilometres.

At Site UMB-041-D, a matching structure was detected.

The two sites are separated by ████████.

**VISIBLE BODY LENGTH:** Unknown

**MAXIMUM CONFIRMED SUBTERRANEAN EXTENT:** 19 km

**POSSIBLE EXTENT:** ████████████

**NUMBER OF CONFIRMED MANIFESTATION SITES:** ██

**NUMBER OF CONFIRMED INDIVIDUALS:** Unknown

No attempt has been made to physically follow these structures.

Following Incident UMB-041-08, such excavation is prohibited.

## Feeding

Oruvath has been observed consuming animals.

Its individual mouths are fully capable of tearing and ingesting conventional organic material.

It is unclear whether feeding serves a nutritional purpose.

No digestive system has been identified.

Food entering one mouth has never been observed exiting elsewhere.

The creature has also been reported biting:

- stone;
- timber;
- metal;
- masonry;
- other portions of its own body;
- and empty air.

One manifestation spent approximately six hours repeatedly biting the side of an ancient tower.

The structure displayed no visible damage.

When investigators returned after the entity disappeared, the entire interior of the tower was missing.

The exterior remained intact.

## Incident UMB-041-08

Incident UMB-041-08 resulted in the permanent prohibition of deep excavation at known sites.

A team investigating organic material beneath Site UMB-041-A opened a chamber approximately 74 metres below ground.

The chamber contained no visible manifestation.

Audio equipment recorded breathing.

The team continued.

At 14:22, drilling equipment penetrated an unidentified soft material.

At 14:23, every known mouth of the surface manifestation opened simultaneously.

No sound was produced.

At 14:24, voices were heard throughout the excavation.

██████████████████████████████████████████████████████

████████████████████████████████████████

At 14:31, surface observers reported that UMB-041 had developed three additional mouths.

The underground team contained three personnel.

Recovery operations were not authorised.

## Missing Persons

Numerous disappearances have been associated with suspected UMB-041 locations.

Physical remains are rarely recovered.

Several months or years later, expedition personnel have occasionally reported hearing voices resembling missing individuals.

This correlation cannot be conclusively verified.

One recording remains particularly controversial.

A researcher disappeared during Exploration UMB-041-02.

Eleven years later, their voice was apparently recorded from one of Oruvath's mouths.

The recording contained one sentence:

*It isn't eating us.*

████████████████████████████████████████

The remainder of the recording has never been publicly released.

## Historical Records

Possible Oruvath references appear across numerous cultures.

This is unusual for an Umbuso.

Recurring titles include translations approximating:

- The Buried Choir;
- The House of Mouths;
- The Speaking Ruin;
- The King Beneath Stones;
- The Many Hungry;
- and The City That Answers.

There is no clear evidence these traditions influenced one another.

Several originated in cultures with no known historical contact.

One recurring instruction appears in some form in nearly every surviving tradition:

**Do not answer.**

## Relationship with Axius

Oruvath produces unusual effects upon Axius related to memory and communication.

Reported phenomena include:

- hearing voices with no physical source;
- remembering conversations which never occurred;
- recognising languages the observer does not understand;
- confusing spoken thoughts with internal thoughts;
- repeating words involuntarily;
- and briefly perceiving nearby stone structures as living tissue.

Several Axius-sensitive researchers describe UMB-041 sites as extraordinarily crowded.

When asked to clarify, responses are remarkably similar.

*There are too many people talking.*

No additional individuals are physically present during these reports.

Multiple probable manifestation sites are known.

Several abandoned sites remain under passive monitoring.

Visual contact lasted approximately nine minutes.
