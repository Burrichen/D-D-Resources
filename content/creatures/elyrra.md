*Sanitised reconstruction of UMB-052. Individual manifestations display substantial anatomical variation.*

**Elyrra**, commonly titled **the Mother of Strangers**, is an [Umbuso](/content/lore/umbuso.md) whose physical contact causes uncontrolled biological growth within living organisms.

The resulting life does not need to correspond to the species of the affected organism.

Documented growths include fungi, additional organs, limbs, eyes, teeth, parasitic organisms and complete animals.

Some remain alive after removal.

## Overview

UMB-052 possesses an irregular body composed of eyes, mouths, tendrils and brightly coloured organic structures.

No stable anatomy has been established.

The entity's primary anomalous property occurs through physical contact.

Affected organisms undergo a process formally designated **Heterologous Proliferation**.

Elyrra does not cause organisms to reproduce.

It causes them to grow **other life**.

The process may begin within minutes or remain dormant for several days.

No reliable method exists for determining which tissue will develop.

## Heterologous Proliferation

Post-exposure growth varies drastically between subjects.

Recorded examples include:

- fungal colonies growing beneath human skin;
- additional eyes developing upon livestock;
- fully formed teeth appearing inside muscle tissue;
- plant structures growing from animals;
- independent worm-like organisms budding from limbs;
- duplicate organs with incompatible anatomy;
- and small vertebrates developing externally from hosts belonging to unrelated species.

Genetic analysis has produced inconsistent results.

Some growths correspond to known species.

Others contain characteristics from several unrelated organisms.

A significant number match **nothing currently catalogued**.

## Anatomy

Elyrra's visible body contains an extraordinary concentration of sensory structures.

Numerous eyes of varying sizes and colours appear across the surface.

Mouths occur without obvious digestive organisation.

Structures resembling:

- feathers;
- fur;
- roots;
- fungal growth;
- tentacles;
- cartilage;
- exposed nerves;
- and flowering tissue

have all been documented.

Whether these structures constitute Elyrra's natural anatomy is unknown.

One hypothesis proposes that much of its visible body consists of organisms produced by previous contact with itself.

This has not been verified.

## Behaviour

Elyrra does not display consistent predatory behaviour.

It nevertheless makes frequent physical contact with its surroundings.

Whether this is deliberate is disputed.

The entity has been observed brushing against vegetation, animals and structures without pausing.

Affected organisms may remain apparently normal after Elyrra has moved on.

This makes post-manifestation contamination considerably more dangerous than the initial encounter.

The disappearance of UMB-052 does not end an UMB-052 incident.

In most cases, it marks the beginning.

## Incident UMB-052-19

Following a confirmed manifestation, authorities located twelve exposed animals.

All were isolated.

Over the next four days, biological growth was detected throughout the surrounding area.

No additional contact with Elyrra had occurred.

Investigators subsequently identified:

**12** confirmed exposed animals.

**37** secondary organisms.

**114** tertiary biological anomalies.

The concept of **tertiary exposure** was introduced following this incident.

The mechanism remains disputed.
