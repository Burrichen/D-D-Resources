**Kharvassa**, commonly titled **the Mother Below**, is an [Umbuso](/content/lore/umbuso.md) believed to inhabit the deepest desert regions of [Halingo](/content/locations/halingo.md).

The entity bears a strong anatomical resemblance to the numerous species collectively referred to as Sandwyrms, though on a scale unmatched by any confirmed specimen.

Historical tradition identifies Kharvassa as the **mother of the Sandwyrms**.

For centuries this was regarded as mythology.

Modern biological evidence has made the distinction considerably less comfortable.

**ARCHIVAL SUMMARY**

UMB-028 is not to be approached for reproductive study.

UMB-028 is not to be sampled.

Previous exemptions to these rules have been revoked.

## Overview

Kharvassa is an immense subterranean organism associated with the deserts of [Halingo](/content/locations/halingo.md).

Most of its body remains beneath the sand.

The visible organism consists of a vast central mass from which numerous serpentine necks emerge, each terminating in an independent jaw.

The exact number of heads is disputed.

Reports have documented between seven and thirteen simultaneously.

No complete view of the body has ever been recorded.

Unlike many Umbuso, Kharvassa appears to have a clear ecological relationship with a widespread group of lesser organisms.

Those organisms are the Sandwyrms of Halingo.

Every Sandwyrm tested in Halingo has contained genetic material which does not belong to the Sandwyrm.

The unidentified material is identical between specimens.

The source of this material is presently classified as **UMB-028-compatible**.

## Appearance

Kharvassa possesses an enormous dark body resembling a partially buried mass of overlapping serpentine organisms.

Several long necks rise independently from the central body.

Each terminates in an elongated jaw lined with numerous pale teeth.

No eyes have been identified.

The necks appear capable of acting independently and have been observed:

- investigating separate targets;
- consuming different organisms simultaneously;
- responding independently to sound;
- retreating beneath the sand individually;
- and displaying apparently conflicting behaviour.

Whether the heads possess independent nervous systems is unknown.

The central body has never been excavated.

Ground-penetrating surveys indicate that the visible portion constitutes only a small fraction of the organism.

How small remains disputed.

## Sandwyrms

Numerous species of Sandwyrm inhabit Halingo.

Until relatively recently, their resemblance to Kharvassa was considered an example of regional folklore influencing natural history.

This changed following genetic analysis of tissue recovered from three unrelated Sandwyrm species.

All three contained the same unidentified sequence.

Further testing found it throughout Halingo's Sandwyrm population.

The sequence is absent from Sandwyrms collected outside the region.

**HALINGO SANDWYRM SPECIES TESTED:** 17

**SPECIES CONTAINING SEQUENCE UMB-028-A:** 17

**KNOWN NON-HALINGO SPECIES CONTAINING SEQUENCE:** 0

**SOURCE ORGANISM:** PRESUMED UMB-028

Kharvassa may therefore be:

- the direct ancestor of Halingo's Sandwyrms;
- a parent organism which continues producing them;
- an organism capable of altering existing Sandwyrm populations;
- or something for which conventional concepts of ancestry are inadequate.

The common title **Mother Below** predates this discovery by several centuries.

## Reproduction

No confirmed Sandwyrm birth has ever been observed in proximity to Kharvassa.

No eggs have been recovered.

No reproductive organs have been identified.

Nevertheless, increases in Sandwyrm activity frequently follow suspected UMB-028 manifestations.

The most significant event occurred after the ████████ Tremor.

For twelve days, Sandwyrms emerged from approximately 140 kilometres of desert surrounding the suspected location of Kharvassa.

The organisms did not attack nearby settlements.

## The Brood Call

Sandwyrms appear capable of detecting Kharvassa across extraordinary distances.

Prior to suspected awakenings, local populations exhibit a behaviour known as the **Brood Call**.

The Sandwyrms stop hunting.

Individuals abandon established territories.

Migration begins toward a shared destination.

Species which ordinarily attack one another travel alongside each other without conflict.

During major events, hundreds may move simultaneously.

When the Sandwyrms stop eating, leave.

They have found something more important than hunger.

The mechanism responsible for this behaviour has not been identified.

No audible call has been detected.

Axius monitoring produces inconsistent results.

## Behaviour

Kharvassa spends the majority of its known existence beneath the desert.

Periods of surface activity are rare.

When active, individual heads may emerge across an area spanning several kilometres.

Early explorers therefore occasionally recorded separate creatures without realising they belonged to the same organism.

The heads demonstrate aggressive feeding behaviour toward large animals and unfamiliar movement.

Human beings are not specifically targeted.

This distinction provides little practical protection.

Kharvassa has consumed:

- caravans;
- livestock herds;
- military vehicles;
- Sandwyrms;
- structures;
- and sections of exposed rock.

The function of this feeding is unknown.

No corresponding increase in body mass has been measured.

## Beneath Halingo

The greatest uncertainty surrounding Kharvassa concerns its actual size.

Surface sightings occur across locations separated by distances too great to reasonably belong to a single conventional organism.

Two explanations dominate current research.

The first proposes that multiple Kharvassa-class organisms exist.

The second proposes that all known sightings represent a single subterranean body.

The latter was once considered biologically impossible.

Subsurface mapping conducted in ████ detected a continuous structure beneath several confirmed manifestation sites.

The survey was repeated.

The structure remained.

Its dimensions were removed from the public report.

## Settlements

Several ancient Halingo settlements were built upon elevated stone formations despite nearby access to substantially more hospitable terrain.

Local traditions typically attribute this to protection from Sandwyrms.

Archaeological evidence suggests another purpose.

Many settlements were positioned specifically outside zones associated with historical Kharvassa activity.

Some contain enormous stone markers buried beneath their foundations.

These markers point downward.

The same inscription appears repeatedly:

*The children travel.*

*The Mother remains.*

No surviving tradition explains the phrase.

## Relationship with Axius

Kharvassa exhibits no consistent vulnerability to Axius.

However, unusually high Axius concentrations have been recorded within areas recently traversed by Sandwyrms participating in a Brood Call.

Whether the creatures produce, transport or accumulate Axius remains unknown.

Direct exposure to Kharvassa has caused several anomalous effects.

These include:

- Axius techniques manifesting beneath the user rather than at the intended target;
- persistent perception of movement below solid ground;
- inability to correctly judge distance;
- temporary attraction of nearby Sandwyrms;
- and auditory hallucinations resembling movement inside walls or floors.

One surviving practitioner described the sensation as:

*Standing on something that was breathing.*

No geological movement was recorded during the incident.
