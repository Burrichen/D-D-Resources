## Overview

Gond is the goddess of the dwarves and an advocate for womankind. While her husband [Helm](/content/deities/helm.md) went out to protect others and fight, Gond supplied him with the best possible equipment. This has led many dwarves to reject Helm as a deity and to believe that Gond deserves more respect.

Apart from her unique designs for Helm, she gives most of her creations to [Dornia](/content/deities/dornia.md), and can even be considered an aspect of her.

**Sides with:** neither.

## Worship

Gond is worshipped in:

- **Limate**, where she is worshipped alongside [Tyr](/content/deities/tyr.md).
- **Khundur**: the Temple of Gond is in impressive condition and guarded, though crime is common.
- **Drunstowr**, which has an oratory to Gond.
- **Stonesthrow**.
- **Oluodehorn** (Maillon, Thevethalas), alongside Helm.

Followers include Drogon Bronzebeard of Limate.
