Tyr, God of Justice, believes in "an eye for an eye". This sets him at odds with [Helm](/content/deities/helm.md), who believes everyone deserves protection and that the world should live in peace. [Azun](/content/deities/azun.md) is his sister.

## Worship

- **Limate**, alongside [Gond](/content/deities/gond.md).
- **Winterfort** (Kostin, Thevethalas).
