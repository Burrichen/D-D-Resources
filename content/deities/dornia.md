Dornia, Goddess of Knowledge, receives most of [Gond](/content/deities/gond.md)'s creations, and Gond can even be considered an aspect of her.

## Followers

- Othrin of the Ambit was raised a devout follower, until [Azun](/content/deities/azun.md) claimed him out of jealousy.
