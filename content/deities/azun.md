## Overview

Azun, God of Night and of Sun, is typically depicted as two beings twinned together.

## Relationships

- Sister to [Tyr](/content/deities/tyr.md).

## Deeds

- **Othrin.** Othrin, of the Ambit (Erudition), was raised a devout follower of [Dornia](/content/deities/dornia.md). His loyalty caught Azun's attention and stirred jealousy in her heart, so she blessed him with the power of divination. At first he used it in Dornia's name, so Azun struck and took his eyesight. Othrin then devoted himself to Azun completely, and she gave him back sight in one eye. The other eye now glimmers, and stars can sometimes be seen in it.

## Worship

- Saltmere (Maillon, Thevethalas).
