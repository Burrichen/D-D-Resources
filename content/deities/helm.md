## Overview

Helm, God of Protection, believes everyone deserves protection and that the world should live in peace.

## Relationships

- **[Gond](/content/deities/gond.md)**, his wife. While Helm went out to protect others and fight, Gond supplied him with the best possible equipment. Because of this, many dwarves reject Helm as a deity and believe Gond deserves more respect.
- **[Tyr](/content/deities/tyr.md)**, with whom he is at odds. Helm believes everyone deserves protection and peace; Tyr believes in "an eye for an eye".

## Worship

- Oluodehorn (Maillon, Thevethalas), alongside Gond.
