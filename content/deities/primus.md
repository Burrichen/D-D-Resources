Composed of 1,182 modron programs, Primus has become the assigned leader of [Mechanus](/content/locations/mechanus.md). Composed in part of every metal currently available across the planes, Primus is content to operate within its own plane.

Primus does not leave its plane and is notoriously isolationist, which makes Mechanus one of the least-visited planes. It is known to send modrons to other planes to perform tasks, such as the construction of Fleshless, ostensibly to help itself, but its true motivations remain unknown.

It is protective of the modron "creatures", believing them imperfect but an adequate enough approximation, unlike other races. It has no ego, despite considering its own construction 99% perfect.
