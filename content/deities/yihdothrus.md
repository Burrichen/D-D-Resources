## Overview

A demon lord of the horrific side of time: erosion, decay, entropy, the dread of growing old, and worms. Known as the **Ravager Worm**, it works slowly and lets time do its work. It appears as an endless black worm with many jaws.

## Worship

Its cultists are usually loners desperate to escape death and ageing, who often turn themselves into liches or other horrors. They serve the Worm less for its power than to stay out of its reach, letting it decay other things in return for being preserved themselves. Their devotions are grave-like: meditating in a coffin of worm-ridden earth.
