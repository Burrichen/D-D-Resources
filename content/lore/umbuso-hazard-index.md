Standardised Assessment of Umbuso Threat

**Designation:** UHI\
**Current Revision:** IV\
**Status:** ACTIVE STANDARD\
**Authorising Body:** ████████████████

**RESEARCH NOTE**

The UHI measures the **hazard presented by an Umbuso**, not its physical strength.

A low score indicates greater danger.

**1 is the worst possible rating. 5 is the best.**

## Method

Every confirmed [Umbuso](/content/lore/umbuso.md) is assessed across six factors:

| Code  | Factor             | Measures                                         |
|-------|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| **S** | **Scale**          | Maximum credible population or area affected.    |
| **L** | **Lethality**      | Expected severity of direct exposure.            |
| **A** | **Activation**     | Likelihood of hostile or hazardous behaviour.    |
| **C** | **Containability** | Humanity's ability to restrict or neutralise it. |
| **P** | **Predictability** | Ability to anticipate or avoid an encounter.     |
| **K** | **Knowledge**      | Reliability of current scientific understanding. |

**UHI = (S + L + A + C + P + K) ÷ 6**

Decimal scores are retained in all formal documentation.

## Assessment Scale

| Score | Scale | Lethality | Activation | Containment | Predictability | Knowledge |
|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| **1** | World | Terminal | Inherent | Uncontainable | Unavoidable | Incomprehensible |
| **2** | Continental | Extreme | Aggressive | Unmanageable | Unpredictable | Poorly understood |
| **3** | Regional | Severe | Reactive | Restrictable | Detectable | Partially understood |
| **4** | Local | Moderate | Passive | Manageable | Predictable | Well documented |
| **5** | Individual | Low | Dormant | Controllable | Avoidable | Understood |

**Scale does not measure physical size.**

An Umbuso capable of appearing worldwide but threatening only isolated individuals would not automatically receive **S1**.

## Hazard Grades

The calculated UHI determines the entity's formal hazard grade.

| UHI           | Grade   | Classification |
|---------------|---------|----------------|
| **1.00–1.49** | **I**   | EXISTENTIAL    |
| **1.50–2.49** | **II**  | Catastrophic   |
| **2.50–3.49** | **III** | Severe         |
| **3.50–4.49** | **IV**  | Hazardous      |
| **4.50–5.00** | **V**   | Limited        |

A full research designation is written as:

**UMB-028 KHARVASSA**\
**UHI 2.00-II**\
S2 / L1 / A3 / C1 / P3 / K2

## Revision Policy

UHI ratings are **not permanent**.

New behaviour, casualties or research may immediately alter an assessment.

**RECLASSIFICATION EXAMPLE**

FORMER: UHI 3.17-III

CURRENT: UHI 1.83-II

REASON:

██████████████████████████████████████████

A reduction of more than **0.50** following a single incident requires mandatory review of all existing containment and evacuation doctrine.
