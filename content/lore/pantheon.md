## Tiers

Gods are ranked by how widely they are worshipped:

- **Major:** celebrated everywhere; everyone knows of them. The equivalent of the great real-world religions.
- **Minor:** niche faiths with a narrower following.
- **Cult:** gods almost no one knows about, worshipped in only one or two places across the world.

**Old Gods.** Sometime after creation, the Old Gods struck the pact that made [Carceri](/content/locations/carceri.md). [Kelemvor](/content/deities/kelemvor.md) is an Old God. Which other gods count as Old Gods isn't recorded yet.

Deity notes also record which **side** each god takes in a divine division that isn't yet described. So far only Gond's is filled in: neither.
