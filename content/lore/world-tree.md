## Origin

At the Dawn, Pia, born from the meeting of the brothers Ornn and Bruen with the darkness of the void, made Kurovia with the darkness from the brothers' remains. Pia then found herself brought into existence again, and again, and each time the story went differently. Sometimes only Ornn and Pia survived; sometimes only the darkness and Ornn; sometimes only Pia. Each outcome created its own plane of existence, each a mirror of another, and yet all somehow linked. Nine planes exist, will exist, have existed. But, Pia thought, none were as perfect as Kurovia.

Each plane births its first creation into being: a personification of its existence, its **plane god**.

The full creation myth is in [The Timeline](/content/lore/timeline.md) (The Dawn).

## Structure

The v3 vault arranges the World Tree into **Outer Planes** (the nine above, apart from Kurovia), **Inner Planes** (the Underdark) and **Astral Space**. The Underdark and Astral Space are named but not yet described.

## The planes after the Oblate

The Oblate obliterated Kurovia, and the planar overlap it caused let the junctions and rules of reality break down further. The world was left unstable. The Far Realm in particular now seeps through into the material plane.
