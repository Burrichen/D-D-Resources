# The Dawn
Summary:  Supposedly, two brothers, Ornn and Bruen, entered the void. The infinite nothing. Their interaction disturbed the great silence. The darkness that had been sleeping forever awoke. The three interacted, protecting themselves from each others accidental harm. Their interplay brought forth Pia, a young woman of immense beauty and strength.

The darkness retreated, believing it had lost, Pia seemed like everything it wasn't - strong, overt and good. But it remained watching. Whilst Pia seemed incorruptible, the two brothers had a rift between them.

Ornn believed Pia should be groomed as a hero, the new champion of to pioneer a new way of life, a shining beacon of the glory of chaos. Bruen believed that life should be so precious just to exist, and to follow the current order, the way things were; being sheltered and living out their life comfortably with no stress. Darkness saw this division and acted, speaking in each brothers ear.

The two began arguing, then fighting. Pia stepped between them, only to accidentally be punched, breaking her jaw. The two looked in horror in what they had done, each blaming the other - a once perfect vision of beauty corrupted. Ironically, seeing Pia's now flawed features, it was the first time darkness ever felt an emotion. Pia's once perfect features, sheer strength was undermined by the folly of others. Darkness realised it could still act with Pia around. It realised it cared for Pia - despite them being polar opposites, there was this implicit understanding between them. A silent acknowledgement of respect. It realised it cared for Pia. It attempted to undo what it had started, but alas the brothers was so twisted against the other, so bitter. Each drew their sword and each drew blood. Both ended up slain by the other.

Pia remained there, watching the bodies of her creators in the infinite void. She reached out, and began to create something new. She invited darkness to join her, to make something of this. She wanted to create a place where they could live in harmony, one family. Equal amounts of each. Taking the blood and remains of the brothers, Pia accepted darkness. All that remained was her ever beating heart. The four of them created Kuroiva.

And yet, Pia remained. It was a curious sensation, to have ceased existing in one reality, but exist in another. So when Pia was brought into existence again, by the two brothers. She smiled. She got to go round again. Each time, it happened differently. Sometimes, Ornn and Pia were the only survivors - creating a different world, a different Plane from their outcome. Sometimes it would be darkness and Ornn. Then, just Pia. Around and around they went. 9 different planes exist, will exist, have existed. Each one made up of the different denizens of the void. But, Pia thought, none were as perfect as Kurovia.

Each outcome created it's own Plane of existence, each a mirror of another, and yet somehow all linked.

Each plane births it's first creation into being, a personification of it's existence. These are listed here:

- Lawful Good - Mount Celestia
-  Neutral Good - Elysium
-  Chaotic Good - Aracadia
-  Lawful Neutral - Mechanus
-  True Neutral - Kurovia
- Chaotic Neutral - Limbo
- Lawful Evil - Avernus
- Neutral Evil - The Far Realm
- Chaotic Evil - Abyss
