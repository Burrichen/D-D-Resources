The creatures of Kurovia have been shaped by their environments, regional manifestations of Axius and the cultures that live alongside them. They can be browsed by location, rarity or Challenge Rating.

The [Umbuso](/content/lore/umbuso.md) (the Hundred Gods) are catalogued separately.

## By region

Creatures are listed according to the region in which they are most commonly found. Migratory or widespread creatures may appear under more than one region.

| Region | Environment | Creatures |
|----|----|----|
| **[Ordon](/content/locations/ordon.md)** | Industrial cities, farmland, mines and older rural wilderness | [Cragback](/content/creatures/cragback.md), [Driftling](/content/creatures/driftling.md), [Shellbill](/content/creatures/shellbill.md) |
| **[Halingo](/content/locations/halingo.md)** | Deserts, rocky wastes, Ichor fields and railway settlements | [Dustkip](/content/creatures/dustkip.md), [Sunsnare](/content/creatures/sunsnare.md) |
| **Skgurr** | Storm coasts, Morov rivers, dark highlands and sea caves | [Shellbill](/content/creatures/shellbill.md) |
| **Shinkira** | Forests, mountains, cities and Utsuro-altered ecosystems | [Driftling](/content/creatures/driftling.md), [Shellbill](/content/creatures/shellbill.md) |
| **Bratislor** | Haze-contaminated wilderness, ruins and containment zones | — |
| **Hjarthland** | Frozen forests, glaciers, mountains and permanent winter | [Cragback](/content/creatures/cragback.md), [Driftling](/content/creatures/driftling.md) |
| **Nareth** | Rainforests, swamps, rivers and rapidly growing ecosystems | [Driftling](/content/creatures/driftling.md), [Lanthart](/content/creatures/lanthart.md), [Shellbill](/content/creatures/shellbill.md) |
| **Targoth** | High mountains, abandoned settlements and isolated valleys | [Kharuun](/content/creatures/kharuun.md) |
| **Armada** | Ships, docks, floating districts and the surrounding seas | [Knelhen](/content/creatures/knelhen.md) |

### Widespread creatures

**Widespread creatures** are found across several regions of Kurovia rather than being associated with a single country: [Cragback](/content/creatures/cragback.md), [Driftling](/content/creatures/driftling.md), [Ritual Husk](/content/creatures/ritual-husk.md), [Shellbill](/content/creatures/shellbill.md).

## By rarity

Rarity describes how frequently a creature is encountered in the world. It does not necessarily indicate how dangerous it is. A creature may be common in one region while remaining almost unknown elsewhere; each creature uses the rarity that best represents its status within its principal habitat.

| Rarity | Definition | Creatures |
|----|----|----|
| **Common** | Frequently encountered within its natural habitat. Usually familiar to local inhabitants. | [Driftling](/content/creatures/driftling.md), [Dustkip](/content/creatures/dustkip.md), [Shellbill](/content/creatures/shellbill.md) |
| **Uncommon** | Known to local communities but not encountered regularly. | [Cragback](/content/creatures/cragback.md), [Lanthart](/content/creatures/lanthart.md), [Sunsnare](/content/creatures/sunsnare.md) |
| **Rare** | Seldom encountered and usually limited to particular environments or circumstances. | [Kharuun](/content/creatures/kharuun.md), [Mournback](/content/creatures/mournback.md) |
| **Very Rare** | Known from only a small number of sightings, territories or surviving populations. | [Knelhen](/content/creatures/knelhen.md), [Ritual Husk](/content/creatures/ritual-husk.md) |
| **Legendary** | Famous creatures whose existence may shape local history, religion or folklore. | — |
| **Unique** | A single known individual, species-ending survivor or creature without confirmed equivalents. | — |

## By Challenge Rating

Challenge Rating (CR) represents the approximate threat a creature poses in combat.

| CR range | General threat | Creatures |
|----|----|----|
| **CR 0** | Normally harmless, although it may still possess unusual abilities. | [Dustkip](/content/creatures/dustkip.md) |
| **CR 1/8–1/2** | A threat to an untrained individual or poorly prepared traveller. | [Driftling](/content/creatures/driftling.md), [Lanthart](/content/creatures/lanthart.md) |
| **CR 1–4** | A local threat suitable for lower-level adventurers. | [Cragback](/content/creatures/cragback.md), [Sunsnare](/content/creatures/sunsnare.md) |
| **CR 5–10** | A serious regional danger capable of threatening settlements or trained forces. | [Kharuun](/content/creatures/kharuun.md), [Knelhen](/content/creatures/knelhen.md), [Ritual Husk](/content/creatures/ritual-husk.md) |
| **CR 11–16** | A major threat requiring experienced adventurers or organised military action. | [Mournback](/content/creatures/mournback.md) |
| **CR 17–20** | An exceptional threat capable of devastating cities or influencing entire regions. | — |
| **CR 21+** | A world-level threat possessing extraordinary or near-mythic power. | — |
| **Unrated** | A creature without completed combat statistics or one not intended for combat. | [Shellbill](/content/creatures/shellbill.md) |
